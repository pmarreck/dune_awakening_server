"The Face Dancer Scytale freed me from my cell" on the Orbital Bastion; the Sleeper later kills him (game text, `JOURNEY_B1C4_FOURTYFEARS_1`). Other Face Dancers in the story compel you to kill Zantara under Old Carthag, pose as Sinnu the Poisoner in the dueling ring, and impersonate Esmar Tuek at Fallen Light (game text). "Face Dancer" and "Face Dancer Assassin" are also enemy types (game text).
