The Tleilaxu Trader is at Pinnacle Station: Zayn de Witte's contract step reads "Talk to the Tleilaxu trader at Pinnacle Station" (game text, `CONTRACT_MENTAT2_CT6_CND0`). Seron Varlin's chain has you "Inquire about eyes with the Tleilaxu trader" and "Receive eyes" from them (game text, `CONTRACT_SWORDMASTER2_CT3`).

Whether this NPC sells anything outside those contracts is unverified; a 2025 community note mentions a Tleilaxu trader at Pinnacle Station (community, pre-1.5).
