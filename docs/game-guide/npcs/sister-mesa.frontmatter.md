Sister Mesa is at Helius Gate, the Atreides fortress in the Shield Wall. Her contract "BENE GESSERIT: BASICS: The Missing Pieces" unlocks the Bene Gesserit tree (game text; verified on this server).

You only need to visit her once; she then sends you to the advanced trainer Jocasta Cleo in Harko Village (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Mesa, edited 2026-10-04). The wiki places Helius Gate in the Eastern Shield Wall (same page).
