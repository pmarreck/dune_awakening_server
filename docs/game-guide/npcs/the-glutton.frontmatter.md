Elara Tuek sends you to kill "a powerful slaver, known only as the Glutton". Start by finding her informant Petyr, hidden among the slavers at their base in the Hand of Khidr (game text, Act 2 card). The fight ends in "The Glutton's dining room", a Jabal Eifrit map location; the exit task is "Exit the Glutton's dining room" (game text). He practiced cannibalism, and a mark like yours glowed under his skin when he died (game text).

Update 1.5 made the Glutton, the Paranoid, the Jackal and Echoes of the Past instanced levels (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/).
