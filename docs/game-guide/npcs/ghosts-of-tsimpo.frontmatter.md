The Ruins of Tsimpo, "a box-canyon village decimated during the war", are an Overland map location (game text). Landsraad missions there reveal that the ghosts are illusions "sustained through solido projection" by raiders; "Ghosts of Tsimpo" ends with "Find the hall of the Ghost of Tsimpo" and killing the leader, and a crafting mission asks you to bring a hand scanner (game text). Enemy types: Tsimpo Reaper, Scourge, Phantom, Specter and an Experimental Solido Projection (game text).
