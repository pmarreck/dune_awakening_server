| 5 | Sinnu the Poisoner | "some sort of worm-cultist"; revealed as the Face Dancer |
| | Dancer of Veils | revealed as Feyd-Rautha, who saves you |

The Face Dancer's dying words point you to trouble among the Smugglers (game text). Whether this ring is the same place as the Neo Carthag Arena with its Duel Ring Vendor is unverified.
