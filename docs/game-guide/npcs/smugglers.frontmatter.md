The Smugglers, who call themselves "Free Traders", are led by Esmar Tuek; his daughter Elara is your main story contact and their secret headquarters is Fallen Light, a crashed Heighliner (game text). Combat types: Smuggler Marauder, Brigand and Ravager; civilians include guards and pilots (game text). Smuggler's Run is a sandbike track on a rock island reached from the Overland map, used by Landsraad missions (game text). Asmara Efendi, Harko's weapons merchant, is Smuggler-affiliated (wiki).
