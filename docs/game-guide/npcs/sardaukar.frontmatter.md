Jabal Eifrit's night skies are "patrolled by Sardaukar dropships" (game text, journey "The Jabal Spires"); scavengers band together for safety from Sardaukar patrols (game text). Combat types: Battle-Hardened Sardaukar and Sardaukar Commando; city/escort types: Sardaukar Guard, Scout Guard and Stillsuit Guard (game text).

Story: the opening is a Sardaukar interrogation before Fenring's offer; in Chapter 4 Sardaukar overrun Staban Tuek's base, ambush you on Zanovar and hold you on their Orbital Bastion, where Commander Aramsham is a boss (game text). Fragments describe the old "Sardaukar pogrom" against the sietches (game text). The Filmic Archive DLC adds Sardaukar building pieces (patch notes; see `economy-social-versions.md`).
