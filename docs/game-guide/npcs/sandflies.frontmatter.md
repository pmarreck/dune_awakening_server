The Shield Wall is "Sandflies Territory" (game text). The Sandflies present themselves as a workers' movement; recruits describe being given food, water and shelter after escaping slavery, while Harkonnen broadcasts call them "glorified bandits" (game text). Their leader-figure the Red Scorpion preaches against the Great Houses, and a Sandflies contract step is "Kill the Red Scorpion" (game text). Enemy types: Sniper, Medic, Enforcer, Abettor, Negotiator, Leader, Boss and a named "The Harpy" (game text).

Story hooks: Osten Laithe's workshop sits inside a Sandflies base in the Eastern Shield Wall (Chapter 2; game text); defection contracts send Atreides turncoats to take logs from Sandflies (game text); Elara Tuek hired Sandflies against Seron Varlin (game text). Both houses offer "Investigate the Sandflies" (wiki, Andrea Ganan and Simone Von Konig pages).
