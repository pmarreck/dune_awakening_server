On Fenring's behalf, Elara Tuek sends you to kill the Jackal, a beggar spreading sedition among the pyons from a deep alley of Arrakeen. She gives you a shigawire garrote that passes Arrakeen's security scanners (game text). Task order: find out how to talk to him, kill him, "Exit the Jackal's alley" (game text). Before dying he says you were not the first sent to "Find the Fremen, Wake the Sleeper" (game text). Instanced level since 1.5 (patch notes 1.5).
