The Vermillius Gap is "Kirab Territory" (game text). The "Young Lord's Guide" ranks the Kirab next above scavengers: no better trained or equipped, but aggressive and operating in packs (game text). Enemy types: Brute, Succor, Slicer, Marauder, Support, Boss, and a "Kirab BOSS EagleEye" (game text).

- Lazaro Fein is a named Kirab boss (game text, `NPC_COMBAT_KIRAB_HOT_ICE_BOSS`). His recordings say water, not spice, is the treasure of Dune; his men rob a water transport and sell water from "the old testing station" (game text). His exact location is unverified.
- Contracts: "Stealing From Thieves" (Kirab looted a settler's sub-fief console shield unit; game text), a Harkonnen spice-theft chain ending "The Man in the Shadows" that implicates Gavril Shigov (game text), and the Lost Harvest search of Kirab camps for abducted Mithra survivors (game text). A CHOAM event asks you to sabotage Kirab emplacements firing on CHOAM ships (game text).
