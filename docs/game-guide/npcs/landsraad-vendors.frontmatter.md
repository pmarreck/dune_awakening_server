The Landsraad vendor names in the game are Crafting, Armor, Weapons, Tools, Vehicle and Cosmetic vendors, plus an Atreides and a Harkonnen Landsraad Quartermaster (game text, chat names). In Harko Village they stand on upper New Court Way (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Harko_Village, 2026-09-29); Arrakeen has a "Landsraad Services" district and Landsraad variant vendors (game text district name; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Arrakeen, 2026-10-01).

- The Crafting vendor takes House Scrip, earned from Landsraad missions; it sells specialized components for high-quality items and augments, with limited stock that restocks weekly (game text).
- Weapon, Armor, Tool and Vehicle vendors can be opened temporarily by winning Landsraad decrees (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Landsraad, edited 2026-10-08).
- PTC 1.5.15.0 limits the tool vendor's Mk5 power packs to 5 per refresh (patch notes, https://duneawakening.com/news/public-test-client-patch-1-5-15-0/; PTC only as of 2026-09-30).

See `economy-social-versions.md` for House Scrip and decrees.
