Thufir Hawat is posted outside the Arrakeen Residence on Residency Approach and is the senior Atreides contact (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Thufir_Hawat, edited 2026-10-04; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Arrakeen). You can deliver a message from Andrea Ganan to him (wiki).

- Defecting: the guide's faction notes say you switch once, permanently, through the other house's Mentat (Thufir or Piter de Vries) and a betrayal contract (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Faction). The Atreides contract for a Harkonnen defector at this tier is "A Betrayal Within a Betrayal": talk to Piter, clear outposts, go to Shield Wall Command, report back to Ambrose (game text, `CONTRACT_FACTIONBETRAYALPITER`). For Atreides defectors, a Harkonnen contract has you take compromising logs from the Sandflies, deliver them to Piter and a false log to Thufir (game text, `CONTRACT_FACTIONBETRAYALTHUFIR`).
- Zayn de Witte, the advanced Mentat trainer, is obsessed with impressing him (game text).
- Unique sniper rifle "Thufir's Best" is `LongRifle_Unique_Poison_05` (item data).
