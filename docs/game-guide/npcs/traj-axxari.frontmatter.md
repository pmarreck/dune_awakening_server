Traj Axxari runs the shop inside Pinnacle Station (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Traj_Axxari, edited 2026-10-11). Stock: Standard GRDA 44, Standard Kindjal, Standard Sword, Standard Disruptor M11, darts, healkits, fuel cells, and components such as Advanced Servoks and Particle Capacitors (wiki). He deals only with those who pay and "respect the deal" (wiki).

Gloos Bohannon stands beside him, a former Atreides soldier dismissed for alleged cowardice who now works as Traj's enforcer. He sells nothing but shares rumors about the Fremen, Harko Village, the Kirab, the slavers and a downed prison ship (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Gloos_Bohannon, edited 2026-10-14).
