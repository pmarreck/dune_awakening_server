Bank Solari and store items with the Spacing Guild banker at the CHOAM Exchange in Arrakeen or Harko Village. "The bankers in Arrakeen and Harko Village will happily hold onto my Solaris"; merchants also take bank credit (game text, journey "Banking Solaris"). The Exchange charges from banked Solari (see `economy-social-versions.md`).

| NPC | Where | Service | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Velma Udec (Spacing Guild banker) | CHOAM Exchange, Harko Village | deposit/withdraw Solari; personal item vault of 500 slots and 30,000 volume; since 1.2.11.0, transfer items between vehicle and bank | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Velma_Udec (2026-10-01) |
| CHOAM Banker | CHOAM Exchange, Arrakeen | same banking service | game text (chat name); wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Arrakeen |
| Ehri Gan (Imperial Treasurer) | Imperial Consulate, Harko Village | took sub-fief taxes until taxation was removed in 1.3; still present | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Ehri_Gan (2026-10-04) |
| Artyom Belov | Harko Village North Terminus and Neo Carthag Arena exit | shuttle between Harko and the arena (Great Convention Part II) | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Artyom_Belov (2026-10-04) |
| Ornithopter Pilot | every tradepost; Spire Keep in Harko | paid taxi between tradeposts and cities; 500 Solari to a social hub and 150 to a tradepost since 1.5 | game text; patch notes 1.5.1.0 PTC (see `world-and-maps.md`) |

The 500-slot vault size is a wiki figure; this server's value is unverified.
