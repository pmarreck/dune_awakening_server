Ghavouri stands at Griffin's Reach Tradepost and gives the first Trooper contract, "TROOPER: BASICS: Proving Grounds", which unlocks the Trooper tree (game text; verified on this server). He is the trainer nearest the starting area (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Ghavouri, edited 2026-10-11).

- His contract text speaks of "unfinished business" with the people who wounded him (game text, `CONTRACT_TROOPER1_CT1`). The wiki lists the targets as three conspirators: Aatu Golven in Kaleff's Stand, Kirsi Tol in Shamal Tunnel and Cardanus Ulun in Edgeway Cavern (wiki, same page; unverified on this server).
- He sells nothing. Training is offered only if you are not already Trooper-trained (wiki, same page).
- Next step: the advanced Trooper trainer, Kara Valk, in Arrakeen (game text).
