Buy Spice Melange (`MelangeSpice`, stack 500) and spiced consumables from Einar Maia (Harko) or Campos Demari (Arrakeen); buy single Cups of Water (`WaterPack_Consumable`, stack 10) at 100 Solari from the water sellers (item data; wiki).

| NPC | Where | Sells | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Einar Maia | Harko Village, main path from landing zone to Hannivar's | Melange Spiced Food, Beer, Coffee, Wine, Liquor; Spice Melange | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Einar_Maia (2026-10-06) |
| Campos Demari | Arrakeen, right side of the path north of the Central Plaza | same six items; also runs "Dune Riders", a pricey ornithopter charter to the Deep Desert | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Campos_Demari (2026-10-10) |
| Ajani Kassa | Harko Village, stall near the entrance on New Court Way; a "town Fremen" | Cup of Water, 100 Solari | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Ajani_Kassa (2026-10-05) |
| Daimo Sharawi | Arrakeen stall; Water Shipper ties | Cup of Water, 100 Solari | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Daimo_Sharawi (2026-10-05) |
| Alastair Christian Chim | upper-floor bar, Salusan Bull, Arrakeen | spiced food and drinks, Cup of Water | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Alastair_Christian_Chim (2026-10-05) |
| Justis Clagg | lower-floor bar, Salusan Bull, Arrakeen | spiced food and drinks, Cup of Water | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Justis_Clagg (2026-10-05) |
| Ishmur | Pilgrims Camp | Melange Spiced Food 150, Beer 300, Coffee 450, Wine 600, Cup of Water 100 | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Ishmur (2026-10-05) |

Melange Spiced Food has the item id `SpiceAddictionConsumable_01` (item data). Chapter 2 asks you to get a poison snooper "through Alastair Chim or Tidjii O'Garee" (game text), so Chim matters to the story too. Where the Pilgrims Camp is was not found; unverified.
