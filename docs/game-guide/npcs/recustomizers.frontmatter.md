To change your look, use Shaffat's Clinic in Arrakeen or Phrakk's Clinic in Harko Village; both are map-legend entries under "Recustomizer" (game text, `Map_Legend_Entry_Recustomizer_*`; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Arrakeen and https://awakening.wiki/Harko_Village).

- Dr. Nailea Shaffat: "My name is Dr. Nailea Shaffat, and I require assistance for a unique problem. See me at my clinic" (game text). Her chain includes bringing posters and a "Shaffat's Import Permit" contract involving an Arrakeen Customs Official (game text).
- Fleshshaper Phrakk: a funnel contract, "Talk to Fleshshaper Phrakk", with a refrigerated spare-parts container (game text).

Whether a contract must be finished before the first recustomization is not stated in the strings; unverified.
