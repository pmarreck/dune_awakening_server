Hagga Basin South is "Scavenger Territory" (game text, region names). Enemy types: Scavenger Thug, Cut-throat and Scrapper, with minion variants (game text). The in-game "Young Lord's Guide" calls them the least threat on the frontier, people who follow conflict to loot what falls from the sky (game text, lore fragment). Their notes mention a "big scav gang formin' at that old base" for safety from Sardaukar patrols and slavers, and selling loot to Myr Charki (game text).
