The Duel Ring Vendor stands in the Neo Carthag Arena, added in 1.3.0.0 ("I got ammo and gear and med packs and more"); its two listed items cost 1,000 Solari or House Scrip, but the wiki does not name them (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Duel_Ring_Vendor, edited 2026-10-04). Artyom Belov ferries players between Harko Village's North Terminus and the arena.
