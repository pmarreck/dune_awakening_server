Zantara is your early guide. His Communinet messages ("Zantara: Incoming Message...") cover the crash site, the first tradepost, getting a stillsuit, building a base, storms, spice and patrol ships (game text, `COMMUNINET_GP_ZANTARA_*`). His "Water from Blood" journey teaches blood harvesting and purification (game text). You briefly flew a Sardaukar ornithopter with him after the crash (game text, journey "Airborne!").

- Wanted poster: "ZANTARA, aka 'The Lion'... THE LAST OF THE FREMEN", one million Solaris (game text, lore fragment). The bounty contract "Zantara's Head" is issued in the name of Count Fenring, Imperial Spice Minister (game text).
- Act 2: he crashes your meeting with Ari in Arrakeen, then "Talk to Zantara in Harko Village" leads to the lab beneath Old Carthag, where a Tleilaxu Face Dancer compels you to kill him (game text). You keep "Zantara's Signet Ring" (game text).
- Afterwards his bat companion Noctua appears in Hagga Basin settlements with messages ("Noctua's Return"; game text).
- A boss named ZANTARA appears in the Chapter 4 "Forty Fears" level (game text, `NPC_COMBAT_NAMEDBOSS_FORTYFEARS_ZANTARA`); its nature is unverified.
