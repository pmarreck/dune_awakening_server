Each house sells its own gear only to members, gated by faction rank ("Requires Reputation Level {Level}"; game text). The game names Atreides and Harkonnen Quartermasters and "Rank 2" and "Rank 3" Atreides and Harkonnen vendors (game text, `NPC_NAMED_*`).

| NPC | Where | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Atreides Quartermaster | Residency Approach, Arrakeen; a second copy at Helius Gate | Scout Ornithopter Mk4 parts, Atreides armor and vehicle variants, Kirab/Harkonnen Traitor variants, swatches, furniture and building sets, 500 to 80,000 Solari; sold only to Atreides players | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Atreides_Quartermaster (2026-10-05) |
| Harkonnen Supply Clerk | Riftwatch | inventory not documented on the wiki | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Harkonnen_Supply_Clerk (2026-10-04) |
| Harkonnen Quartermaster | presumably Harko Village; unverified | name only | game text |

Joining a house is done through the recruiters at The Anvil (Anton Tolliver, Maxim Kazmir). Opposing-house cosmetics are sold by some city merchants at double price (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Faction; see Phin O'Garee and Ormuna Laaks). Exact rank thresholds per item are not documented (the wiki's rank column shows "?").
