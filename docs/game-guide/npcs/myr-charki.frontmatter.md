Myr Charki is the trader at Griffin's Reach Tradepost (game text). A scavenger contract says he "buys our scrap anyway" and calls him "Typical Harkonnen" (game text, `CONTRACT_DESCRIPTION_LONG_TRADEPOSTFUNNEL_SCAV_02`). He also issues the contract that sends you north: "NOTE: VEHICLE NEEDED FOR CROSSING TO THE NORTH" (game text).

Stock (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Myr_Charki, edited 2026-10-11; no prices listed): Maula clothing (breeches, bracers, boots, vest), Healkit, light and heavy darts, welding wire, Power Pack Mk1, mechanical parts, EMF Generator, Advanced Servoks, Particle Capacitor, Scrap Metal Knife, a Cutteray and the Hajra Literjon Mk1 schematic. He stands behind the bar, opposite the contract console (wiki). The wiki tags him Harkonnen-affiliated though "not Harkonnen himself".
