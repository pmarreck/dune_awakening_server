Kara Valk unlocks Trooper level 2, level 3 and the capstones. She is in Arrakeen (game text, Journey steps), in the Central Plaza and later the Salusan Bull bar per the wiki (wiki; unverified on this server). The final contract is "The Cleansing of Kara Valk" (game text).

- Story: you trade services for training and help her manage "her crippling addiction" (game text, journey completion). Contract items include medicine for withdrawal from "an unreliable servo-stim implant", a coded slaver servo-stim shipping manifest, and a master transmitter that can access any servo-stim implant (game text).
- Her first contract sends you to a slaver camp in Jabal Eifrit; one step has you kill a slaver commander for their transmitter (game text). Another asks you to find her smuggler contact Tuco in Harko Village, falling back on the black-market trader at Hannivar's, and return to Anoushka Hannivar (game text).
- The game's map-marker keys file her under a Hagga Basin region; that is where contracts send you, not where she stands (see `progression-and-skills.md`).
