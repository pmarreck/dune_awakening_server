To join House Atreides, talk to Lieutenant Anton Tolliver at The Anvil (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tradeposts, edited 2026-10-10; https://awakening.wiki/Anton_Tolliver, edited 2026-10-04). He sells nothing; he recruits and hands out War of Assassins espionage contracts.

- First contract "Test of Resourcefulness": bring Holtzman Shields and Artisan Kindjals, scavenged or fabricated (game text, `CONTRACTS_ATREIDES_FACTION1`).
- His rival across the room is the Harkonnen recruiter Maxim Kazmir; the two run contracts against each other (missing undercover agent, forged documents, the Skorda search) (wiki).
- A player who ran errands for Kazmir and wants to switch gets "Restoring Lt. Tolliver's Honor": recover the Spy Report Maxim obtained (game text).
- Later Atreides contacts: Andrea Ganan at Helius Gate, then Thufir Hawat in Arrakeen (wiki). Faction contracts raise standing up to rank 5 (patch notes 1.3.0.0; see `progression-and-skills.md`).

The game also has an NPC named "Cassini Brava Atreides Rep" (game text); where it stands is unverified.
