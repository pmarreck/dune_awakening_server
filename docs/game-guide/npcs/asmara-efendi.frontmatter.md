Asmara Efendi is the weapons merchant in the northeastern corner of Harko Village, north of the Imperial Consulate marker, in the alleys east of The Baron's Eye (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Asmara_Efendi, edited 2026-10-13).

Stock (wiki): Standard GRDA 44, Standard Karpov 38, Standard Disruptor M11, Standard Kindjal, Standard Sword, Standard Maula Pistol, darts, Healkit, and components: Armor Plating, Blade Parts, Gun Parts, Diamondine Dust, Carbide Scraps, Sandtrout Leathers. No access requirement is listed.
