The "Young Lord's Guide" calls deserters "among the most dangerous and desperate villains" on Arrakis; even the lowest has combat and ambush training, and veterans may be trained swordsmen or gunners (game text). The Hagga Rift journey notes "Deserter bases threaded into the walls" (game text).

Named bosses (game text): Colonel Keil (deserter contract chain 1); Grenadier, Heavy, Swordsman and "Marksman (Contract)" (chain 2, opened by Sub-Bashar Istivan Madis on Kazmir's recommendation); Cassian and Leon at Blushing Cavern, whose "Ironstorm" Vulcan GAU-92 and "Ifrit" flamethrower are trophies in a Landsraad false-flag mission. Both houses issue deserter contracts in the Rift, including blood samples and recovering intelligence from a testing station deep in the Rift (game text). "Seasoned" deserter variants carry a `DD6` key, probably the Deep Desert (inference). Deserters are also Landsraad kill targets in the Deep Desert (see `world-and-maps.md`).
