Jabal Eifrit is "Slaver Territory" (game text). Enemy types: Trapper, Scorcher, Sawbones, Sniper, Flayer and Boss (game text). Ariste's journal says the slavers of House Halil turned an abandoned spicing station at the Hand of Khidr into their command center (game text). Political pressure from the Neo-Carthag project has pushed slavers to increase their numbers (game text, "Young Lord's Guide").

They figure in the Glutton assassination, Kara Valk's servo-stim chain, Zayn de Witte's minimic film hunt and Tyg Rolsum's "extremely dangerous" slaver contracts (game text). Ore merchants also pay you to clear slavers near their Rift mining sites (game text, stranded-merchant contracts).
