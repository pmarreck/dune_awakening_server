The Guide speaks in the Trials of Aql: "Here wali faced the trials of Aql, proving their knowledge of the Fremen ways" and "We have to die a little, in order to awaken" (game text, cinematic subtitles). The trials are spread across Hagga Basin; altar carvings point to the next site (game text, `JOURNEY_MAP_Altar*`; see `progression-and-skills.md`).

Being eaten by a sandworm starts a journey: "I awoke with a strange melody in my head. I followed the melody to an odd man... who spoke to me of the Shai-Hulud" (game text). Whether that odd man is the Guide is unverified.
