Mysa Tarill and The O'odham are "Maas Kharet Territory" (game text). An agent's report to Piter de Vries says a sandworm died breaking into the Harkonnen power plant at Mysa Tarill and its preserved carcass became the cult's holy relic (game text). Nahla's writings say she became their Sayaadina, shaping a cult of "better Fremen" after failing to find the real ones (game text).

Enemy types: Initiate, Fanatic, Oppressor, Priestess, Sniper, Bloodletter, Zealot, Boss, and a sietch boss "Hand of the Prophet" (game text). Contracts describe them killing visitors to a testing station for the water in their bodies, abducting a pyon, and wrecking a miner's sandbike (game text). The Act 2 boss the Paranoid leads them from Mysa Tarill.
