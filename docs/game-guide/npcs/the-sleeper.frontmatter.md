The minimic film from the sietch holds a recording of "Alnaëm. The Sleeper", who claims to be the Lisan al-Gaib and to have had your mission, "Find the Fremen" (game text). The Jackal and the Paranoid also speak of that mission (game text).

Chapter 4 "Forty Fears": imprisoned by Fenring on the Orbital Bastion and freed by Scytale, you find nearly forty sarcophagi of your ghola siblings and learn you are the ghola of Tyros Reffa. You wake the Sleeper, who defeats Fenring, kills Scytale and stays behind on the doomed ship while you take an escape pod (game text). A boss named TYROS appears in that level (game text, `NPC_COMBAT_NAMEDBOSS_FORTYFEARS_TYROSREFFA`).
