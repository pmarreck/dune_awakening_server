Kahraman Reis stands in the alley behind the CHOAM Exchange building in Harko Village, to the left (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Kahraman_Reis, edited 2026-10-07).

Stock (wiki): Buggy Mk3 parts, Sandbike Mk2 and Mk3 parts, some Treadwheel parts, Small Vehicle Fuel Cell, Complex Machinery (`T2MachineComponent`), Military Power Regulator, Hydraulic Piston, and unique modules: Night Rider Sandbike Boost Mk2, Bigger Buggy Boot Mk3, Albatross Wing Module Mk5, Steady Assault Boost Module Mk5 (`OrnithopterMediumBoost_Unique_LessHeat_5`; item data). Arrakeen's counterpart is Beatriz Luna.
