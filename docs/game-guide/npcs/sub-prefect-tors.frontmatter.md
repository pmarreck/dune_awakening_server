Count Fenring suspects the Bene Tleilax in a series of Arrakeen killings and sends you to his chief investigator at the Imperial Consulate, Sub-Prefect Tors (game text, "Proces-Verbal"). The four journeys (game text):

| Journey | What you do | Key NPCs |
|---|---|---|
| Proces-Verbal | Find Osten Laithe in the Upper Salusan Bull, question witnesses, get a poison snooper "through Alastair Chim or Tidjii O'Garee", convince Hester Klimt he was murdered | Tors, Hester Klimt |
| Touch of Evil | Hester gives the location of Osten's workshop, inside a Sandflies base in the Eastern Shield Wall | Tors |
| From the Ashes | Find Phaedra Vye; follow her valet's note to a Deep Desert cave; ask Tidjii O'Garee what she knows | Tors, Tidjii O'Garee |
| The Janusian Faithful | Investigate a massacred banquet of nobles tied to Old Carthag loot; identify victims for Dhood Shazz; question the butler and Ambrose Lucifera; catch the assassin | Dhood Shazz, Ambrose Lucifera, Tors |

Dhood Shazz is the Sheriff of Arrakeen, upstairs in the banquet hall (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Dhood_Shazz, edited 2026-10-11); he gifts Phaedra Vye's mask after Proces-Verbal (game text, item description). Tors's standing position is not documented (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tors, edited 2026-10-04).
