The Lost Harvest DLC starts with the crashed spice harvester Mithra northwest of Griffin's Reach (game text; see `world-and-maps.md`). Keif Villari, "the Spice Miner's Guild Safety Warden", wants you to contact him once you have helped the survivors; the journey card is "Assist Keif Villari" (game text).

Contract givers among the survivors (game text, contract short descriptions): Chief Hanso (search Kirab camps for Spice Miner's Guild tags of abducted survivors), Lt. Drangh (comms repair), Quartermaster Hix (medical supplies). Named survivors include Dune Men Cass, Adkin and Gulati (game text).

The unique "Villari's Stillsuit" set (`Stillsuit_Unique_Efficient_06_*`; item data) is branded by CHOAM after his "ordeal in the Deep Desert" (game text).
