In Act 2 you recover the Ixian decoder from the Wreck of the Hephaestus "with help from none other than Feyd-Rautha Harkonnen - the na-baron himself", who was smuggled aboard in a container; the NPC appears as "Dashing Rogue (Feyd Rautha)" (game text). In Chapter 3 the "Dancer of Veils" in the illegal dueling ring "revealed himself to be Feyd Rautha" and saved you from the Face Dancer (game text, "Art of Kanly").
