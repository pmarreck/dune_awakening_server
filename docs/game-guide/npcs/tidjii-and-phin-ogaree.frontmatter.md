The O'Garees came to Arrakis from Hagal and stand just inside the Arrakeen landing pad, to the right (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tidjii_O%27Garee, edited 2026-10-07; https://awakening.wiki/Phin_O%27Garee, edited 2026-10-07).

- Tidjii: scrap trader. Blank Sinkchart (`BlankSinkChart`) 1,500 Solari, Range Finder 5,000, most components 750, Salvaged Metal 10 (wiki; ids item data). She also figures in Chapter 2: you can get the poison snooper through her, she issues a black-market trade receipt, and "Ask Tidjii O'Garee what she knows" is a step of "From the Ashes" (game text).
- Phin: black-market merchant. Harkonnen vehicle variants, furniture and building sets, swatches and salvage components, for Solari or House Scrip. Atreides players pay double; he will not talk to Harkonnen-aligned players (wiki).
