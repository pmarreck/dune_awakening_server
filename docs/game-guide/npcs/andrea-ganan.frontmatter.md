Andrea Ganan is a Mentat serving Thufir Hawat, found at Helius Gate (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Andrea_Ganan, edited 2026-10-09; the page names both the Eastern and Western Shield Wall). Contract giver for "The Trail of the Red Scorpion", "Secrets of the Stone Sentinel", "The War Profiteer", "The Master of Assassins", "Investigate the Sandflies", "A Spy Among Spies" and "Interrogate Tripp Yates" (wiki). Contract issuer text "Andrea Ganan" (game text).

"A Spy Among Spies" is the defection path for Harkonnen players: eliminate the spies Simone von Konig planted around Helius Gate (game text, `CONTRACT_FACTIONBETRAYALSIMONE`). Thufir Hawat's first dialogue lets you deliver a message from her (wiki).
