Cyprian Io holds the post of Imperial Planetologist through family ties to Shaddam IV and sits in an alcove at the eastern corner of the Salusan Bull's upper floor (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Cyprian_Io, edited 2026-10-11). Derek Chinara's "Adhering to Hierarchy" contract requires talking to him about Liet-Kynes's notes; he refuses (wiki). He also issues the short contract "I Spy a Planetologist", signed "-Cyprian Io" (game text, `CONTRACT_PLANETOLOGISTFUNNEL`). Orilis Io is a separate NPC (game text).
