Ant Nostrum sells weapons from a desk in the first vendor alcove of Arrakeen's southern district (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Ant_Nostrum, edited 2026-10-05).

Prices (wiki, Solari): unique schematics Kharet Viper 59,000 (`UniqueRapier_02_Schematic`), Fivefinger's Tripleshot Rifle 87,000 (`LongRifle_Unique_LargeMag_05_Schematic`), Starburst 90,000 (`UniqueScattergun_Fire_05_Schematic`) (ids: item data); standard rifles, sidearms, blades and scatterguns about 2,700 to 4,380; Healkit 20; darts; caliber compressors, armor plating, gun and blade parts; a Shaddam IV portrait placeable.
