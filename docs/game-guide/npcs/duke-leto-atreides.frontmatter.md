Duke Leto summons Atreides players "for an audience at the Arrakeen Residence" in Chapter 3 (game text, `JOURNEY_B1C3_FACTION_ATRE_0`). His rank-20 contract "The Last Beacon" sends you after Captain Moser's ambushed unit and a captured intelligence asset; the journey lets you rescue Atreides captives at the cost of the asset (game text). The contract short description names him as issuer (game text).
