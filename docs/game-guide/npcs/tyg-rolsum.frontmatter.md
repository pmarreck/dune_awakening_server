Tyg "Smiley" Rolsum runs the main counter at The Anvil (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tyg_Rolsum, edited 2026-10-07). Contracts carry his name; one slaver contract is stamped "NOTE: EXTREMELY DANGEROUS AREA. PURSUE TASK AT YOUR OWN RISK. — TYG ROLSUM" (game text, `CONTRACT_SLAVERS3_CONTRACT1_LONGDESC`).

Listed stock includes the Night Rider Sandbike Boost Mk2 (`SandbikeBoost_Unique_LessHeat_2`, schematic `Schematic_UniqueBikeBoost`; item data), Scipio's Bloodbag and the Buoyant Reaper Mk2 (wiki; no prices given). The Anvil also holds the Atreides and Harkonnen recruiters and a House Tseida representative (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tradeposts, edited 2026-10-10).
