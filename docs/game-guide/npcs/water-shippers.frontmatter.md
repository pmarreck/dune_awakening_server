A Harkonnen dossier: the Water Shippers control most water concessions through dynastic holdings, "including the Bewts and the Yafengs", with ice quarries at the polar cap and dew fields in the lowlands; their weakness is labor (game text). Journey "Wind Pass and the Old Quarry": at the Old Quarry the Bewts work with Tleilaxu designs to reanimate dead workers; at Wind Pass the Yafengs make fatal errors expanding production (game text).

- Troop types: Bewt and Yafeng Aquarian, Dowser, Bearer, Harrier; Polar Guard and Polar Guard Elite with the same roles (game text).
- Named: Lingar Bewt; Dr. Jalanta, whose journal at the Old Quarry describes reanimating dead maula (game text); Yafeng head of operations Ksenia Yafeng; Deputy Operations Lead Kade Rin and Chief Engineer Ren Qaoli, kill targets of the Landsraad mission "The Price of Water" (game text).
- Act 2: you poison a Water Shipper's cistern at his mansion outside Arrakeen (game text).
