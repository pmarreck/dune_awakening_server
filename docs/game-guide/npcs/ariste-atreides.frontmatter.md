Ari explores the abandoned sietch with you in Act 1 ("Explore the sietch with Ari"), takes the encoded minimic film and reveals herself as "a member of one of the Great Houses" (game text). The journals in the Dunipedia are "From the Journal of Ariste Atreides" (game text).

- Act 2: she sends you to look for an Ixian decoder (Black Market Trader in Harko, then the Wreck of the Hephaestus) and meets you on the second floor of the Salusan Bull in Arrakeen to decode the film (game text).
- Chapter 2 "The Bloodline": meet her at the Salusan Bull, present her token at the Western Palace Barricade, sneak through the Archives under the Arrakeen Palace, pass a Bene Gesserit test and give a blood sample (game text, `JOURNEY_CH2_BLOODLINE_*`).
