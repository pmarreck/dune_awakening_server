To join House Harkonnen, talk to Levenbrech Maxim Kazmir at The Anvil (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tradeposts, edited 2026-10-10; https://awakening.wiki/Maxim_Kazmir, edited 2026-10-07). Proving loyalty unlocks the Harkonnen vendors (wiki).

- First contract "Test of Acquisition": bring fifty steel bars (game text, `CONTRACTS_HARKONNEN_FACTION1`).
- Other contracts he issues include planting a falsified communication log and "Fruits of Your Labor", hunting Atreides deserters (game text, contract short descriptions). The wiki adds forging a document against Anton Tolliver, blackmail, recovering stolen intelligence, experimental missile parts and questioning Katrina Chloros.
- His defection contract for Atreides players, "Betray the Atreides", has you intercept Anton Tolliver's messenger, steal the letter and "Humiliate Anton" (game text, `CONTRACT_FACTIONBETRAYALANTON`; the wiki lists this contract under Simone Von Konig, so the giver is uncertain).
- Sub-Bashar Istivan Madis's deserter contract opens: "You are reading this because you have received the recommendation of Levenbrech Kazmir" (game text).

The game also names a "Czagu Lavro Harkonnen Rep" (game text); placement unverified.
