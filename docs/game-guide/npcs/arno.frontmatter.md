Talk to Arno at Pinnacle Station and complete "SWORDMASTER: BASICS: Checking the Post" to open the Swordmaster tree (game text; verified on this server). Later Swordmaster levels and capstones come from Seron Varlin in Harko Village (game text).

Pinnacle Station is filed under Hagga Rift in the game's keys and under Jabal Eifrit Al-gharb on the wiki (see `world-and-maps.md`). On the wiki's description of the station, Arno stands to the right of the merchant Traj Axxari and his enforcer Gloos Bohannon (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Traj_Axxari, edited 2026-10-11).
