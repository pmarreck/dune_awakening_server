Derek Chinara trains Planetologist at every stage. His camp sits "above Imperial Testing Station No. 2" in "the rocky clefts above the Blight" (game text). The first contract is "PLANETOLOGIST: Basics: Minimic Film Recovery" and the last of nine is "The Final Piece" (game text).

- Unlock: finish the task inside Testing Station No. 2 and bring him the item. You can talk to him first to receive the task, or finish the station first and hand the item in (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Derek_Chinara, edited 2026-10-02).
- Advanced chain (wiki, same page): Buried Archives, Adhering to Hierarchy, Science Unlocked, The Search for Kynes, A Reasonable Routine, Clues from the Past, The Planetologist Report, The Final Piece. "Adhering to Hierarchy" sends you to Cyprian Io in Arrakeen.
- He moves to other locations during the chain (wiki). The journey summary: you explored Testing Stations for him, saved him "from more than one danger" and retraced the work of Liet-Kynes (game text, `JOURNEY_TRAINER_PLANETOLOGIST_1_DESC_COMPLETE`).
- Planetologist is not a starting class (game text; see `progression-and-skills.md`).
