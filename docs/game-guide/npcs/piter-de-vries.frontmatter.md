Piter de Vries resides in Harko Village and assigns contracts that test your loyalty; once you pass, he escorts you to Glossu Rabban to make your loyalty official (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Piter_de_Vries, edited 2026-09-29). Contract issuer "Piter de Vries" exists in the game text.

Defection contracts on both sides involve him (game text, see `npcs/thufir-hawat`). Rabban's rank-20 contract uses "Piter's neurotoxin bomb" (game text, "Rabban's Revenge").
