After a Sardaukar interrogation, Fenring tells you that you carry a hypno-ligation (a compulsion) and that you share an interest in finding its source; he sends you to Elara Tuek (game text, Act 2 cards).

- He commissions the Glutton, Paranoid, Jackal and Water Shipper assassinations; each victim shows a glowing sub-dermal mark like yours (game text).
- Chapter 2 opens with "Called to a Count": Elara says Fenring wants to see you; he then sends you to his chief investigator in Arrakeen (game text).
- Chapter 4: on Zanovar you are "ambushed by Sardaukar forces led by Count Fenring and captured", and you wake imprisoned on the Sardaukar Orbital Bastion; the Sleeper "soundly defeated Count Fenring" there (game text).
- The "Zantara's Head" bounty contract carries his name (game text). Seron Varlin's final contract challenges him under Kanly (game text). His wife Lady Margot Fenring is also an NPC (game text).
