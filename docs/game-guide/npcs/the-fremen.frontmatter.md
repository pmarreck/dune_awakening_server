Your opening mission from the Bene Gesserit is "Find the Fremen" (game text). Zantara is billed as "the last of the Fremen" (game text), the Maas Kharet are a cult imitating them, and Sietch Ta'lab and other sietches are map locations (game text). Stilgar appears in Chapter 4, with NPC variants named for a drop pod and for Sietch Tabr; Jamis and "Fremen Troop" also exist as NPCs (game text). The Fremen thread journeys are "The Test of Aql", the Trials of Aql and "The Footsteps of the Fremen" (game text; see `progression-and-skills.md`).
