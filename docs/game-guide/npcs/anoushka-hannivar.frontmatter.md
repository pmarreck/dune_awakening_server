Anoushka Hannivar runs Hannivar's, the Harko Village tavern (game text district name "Hannivar's tavern"; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Anoushka_Hannivar, edited 2026-10-05). From the landing bay go straight to New Court Way and enter the building on the left; she is at the main bar (wiki).

Stock and prices (wiki): Melange Spiced Liquor 1,000 Solari, Sapho Juice (`SaphoJuice`, item data) 2,500, Cup of Water (`WaterPack_Consumable`) 100, Imperial Casual Top and Shoes 10,000 each, Giedi Casual Shirt, Pants and Shoes 1,000 each.

Kara Valk's chain returns you to her ("Return to Anoushka Hannivar in Harko Village"; game text). Hannivar's is also where Chapter 2 has you meet Elara Tuek (game text).
