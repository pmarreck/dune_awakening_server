Seron Varlin unlocks Swordmaster level 2, level 3 and the capstones; the last contract is "The Last Stand of Seron Varlin" (game text). He is in Harko Village (game text, Journey steps), at Beast's Bend per the wiki (wiki; unverified on this server).

- Story: blind and harassed in Harko's back alleys, he sends you after the stolen hilt of his Ginaz sword. You search Hannivar's for clues and find a contract, issued by Elara Tuek, hiring Sandflies to "punish" him (game text, `CONTRACT_SWORDMASTER2_CT1`). He seeks Tleilaxu metal eyes through the black-market trader and the Tleilaxu trader (game text, `CT3`). The chain ends with a letter "Addressed to Count Hasimir Fenring, invoking the laws of Kanly" (game text).
- You learn "the secrets of forging Damasteel" (game text, journey completion).
