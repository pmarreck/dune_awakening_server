These are the five "Challenge Mode" Testing Stations of Chapter 3, repeatable up to difficulty 100 (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-testing-station-89-radiation-walkthrough-and-boss-guide, 2026-02-09, pre-1.5). The Chapter 3 journey "Scaling Up" ("the dungeons and deep places on Arrakis") fits them (game text). Lieutenant Aman: dodge his slow projectiles by strafing at range, stay out of the radioactive pits (two in phase 2, up to seven in phase 5), and kill the adds that spawn every 25 to 30 seconds and throw Collapse Grenades (same guide).

Lore from shigawire reels (game text): Aman's group hunts lost Atreides atomics they blame for the radiation; Masano experiments with hard-to-extinguish fire "for the movement"; Takulki wants to copy a technology he found; Burrbridge is dosing himself and talks to plants; Lemenger tries to control reanimated subjects. Station locations on the map were not found; unverified.
