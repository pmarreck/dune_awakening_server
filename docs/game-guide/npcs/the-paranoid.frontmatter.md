The Paranoid is a cult leader who "has taken over the power station of Mysa Tarill" (game text). The plan: kill her four Chosen, each carrying one of the five codes to her pentashield, then use a Shield Dissembler on the last pentashield to enter her place of contemplation (game text, Act 2 card and tasks). The Chosen are named the Chosen of the Fire, Body, Tooth and Breath (game text, NPC names); the tasks name Fire, Body and Tooth. "The Paranoid's Contemplation Chamber" is a map location filed under The O'odham (game text). She speaks of a mission to find the Fremen and shows the glowing tattoo when she dies (game text).

Instanced level since 1.5 (patch notes 1.5).
