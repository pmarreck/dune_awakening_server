The Black Market Trader works out of Hannivar's in Harko Village: Kara Valk's contract points you to "the black market trader in Hannivar's" (game text, `CONTRACT_TROOPER2_CT2_LONGDESC`). A shigawire reel adds: for contraband "speak with the barkeeper at a place called Hannivar's. You know which drink to order." (game text).

- Story: after the sietch, Ari suggests you buy an Ixian decryption device from a Black Market Trader in Harko Village (game text, journey "Black Market Tech").
- Trainer quests: Seron Varlin asks you to "Ask the black market trader about Tleilaxu Eyes" (game text).
- Item: the "Black Market K-28 Lasgun" is a unique lasgun (game text, item name); its seller is unverified.
