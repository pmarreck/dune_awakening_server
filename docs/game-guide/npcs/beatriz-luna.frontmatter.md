Beatriz Luna sells vehicle parts at a desk to the left in Arrakeen's entryway (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Beatriz_Luna, edited 2026-10-05).

Prices (wiki, Solari): Steady Assault Boost Module Mk5 220,000 (the most expensive item), Albatross Wing Module Mk5 55,000, Sandbike Tread Mk2 4,200, Complex Machinery 500; also unique vehicle schematics, one- and four-man groundcar parts and a Desert Mechanic Bike Variant. Harko's counterpart is Kahraman Reis.
