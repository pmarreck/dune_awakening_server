Jocasta Cleo unlocks Bene Gesserit level 2, level 3 and the capstones; the last contract is "The Rogue Bene Gesserit" (game text). She is in Harko Village (game text, Journey steps), in a back alley near the Atreides vendor at The Baron's Eye per the wiki (wiki; unverified on this server).

- Story: you investigate how the Missionaria Protectiva shaped ancient Fremen spirituality (game text, journey completion). Contract items include Fremen religious texts from the Hagga Basin, a summary of Fremen tribes and bloodlines, and Pardot Kynes's ecological lexicon (game text, `CONTRACT_CONTRACTBG*`). One step: "Question the clientele at Hannivar's" (game text).
- A community guide names Imperial Testing Station No. 71 (The O'odham) as the boss-and-dungeon stop of a Bene Gesserit trainer quest (community, https://itemlevel.net/dune-awakening-bene-gesserit-trainer-location-quest-guide/ via search summary; unverified).
