Simone Von Konig is on the first platform of Riftwatch, directly below the House Moritani representative (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Simone_Von_Konig, edited 2026-10-11). Contracts listed there: "The Coup", "Betray the Atreides", "A Secret Delivery", "Investigate the Sandflies", "Interrogate Tripp Yates" (wiki).

Defection contract for Atreides players at this tier (inferred from its wording): prove yourself by stealing "a rare and deliciously lethal poison" from an Atreides transport (game text, `CONTRACT_FACTIONBETRAYALANDREA`, steps "Intercept the Atreides transport", "Kill the ambushers", "Steal the poison vial"). The Atreides side hunts her spies in "A Spy Among Spies" (game text).
