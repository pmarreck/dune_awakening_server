PAL ("Paladin AI" in chat, "PALADIN" as an NPC name; game text) is a thinking-machine combat trainer at the bottom of Imperial Testing Station No. 163, inside the Forgotten Testing Station, reached through the "Once Upon a Time" journey of the Lost Harvest DLC (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/PAL, edited 2026-10-11).

Its terminal needs a passcode, found in a room to the left of its projection; without it you get "Unauthorized access. Passcode required." (wiki, same page). The wiki lists it under Trainers, but what it teaches is not stated there; unverified. Testing Station No. 163 is in The O'odham (game text, see `world-and-maps.md`).
