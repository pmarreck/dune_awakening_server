Samin Moro gives "MENTAT: BASICS: First Blood", which unlocks the Mentat tree (game text). He is at Riftwatch, the Harkonnen fortress in the Hagga Rift (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Samin_Moro, edited 2026-10-04; basic trainers verified on this server).

The advanced Mentat trainer is Zayn de Witte in Arrakeen (game text). Riftwatch also holds Simone Von Konig and the Harkonnen Supply Clerk (wiki).
