Ranya Sorvane is the Harko Village scrap dealer, on the left of the main street between the landing zone and Hannivar's (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Ranya_Sorvane, edited 2026-10-05).

Stock (wiki): Salvaged Metal, Armor Plating, Range Finder (`T3MarksmanComponent`; item data), Blade Parts, Gun Parts, EMF Generator, Micro-sandwich Fabric, Advanced Servoks, Particle Capacitor, Plasteel Microflora Fiber, Mechanical Parts. Arrakeen's scrap trader is Tidjii O'Garee.
