Every Landsraad house has a representative marked on the map ("House {Name} Representative"; game text). Complete Landsraad missions for a house and claim its tiered rewards "from the corresponding House Representative at any time" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Landsraad, edited 2026-10-08).

Houses with a representative map marker (game text, `Map_Legend_Entry_HouseRepresentative_*`): Alexin, Argosaz, Dyvetz, Ecaz, Hagal, Hurata, Imota, Kenola, Lindaren, Maros, Mikarrol, Moritani, Mutelli, Novebruns, Richese, Sor, Spinnette, Taligari, Thorvald, Tseida, Varota, Vernius, Wallach, Wayku, Wydras (25).

Known placements:
- House Tseida representative at The Anvil (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tradeposts, edited 2026-10-10).
- Havadisa Argosaz outside Griffin's Reach Tradepost; House Argosaz belongs to the five-house "Cartel of Gaiety" exploration bloc, and its exclusive reward is a swatch (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Havadisa_Argosaz, edited 2026-10-04).
- House Moritani representative on the first platform of Riftwatch (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Simone_Von_Konig, edited 2026-10-11).

The wiki's representative list (names only): Amadi Ikeni, Amarigo Mutelli, Asher Vandor, Barnabas Brightmorn, Chayson Vernius, Daryna Danylo, Dax Reyder, Desmond Mercer, Dren Silar, Graem Candis, Havadisa Argosaz, Heldabor Deseo, Ishago Imota, Juno Ezura, Luciana, Lupino Ord, Mara Bleak, Marwan Coel, Pennethorne Larkspur, Theo Skordi, Theodoric Ein, Thusnelda, Vera Vogel, Zephyrine Tuma, Zillah (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/NPCs, edited 2026-07-27). The other placements were not found; the wiki shows them only on a map image.
