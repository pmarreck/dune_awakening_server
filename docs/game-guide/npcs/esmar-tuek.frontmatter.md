Esmar Tuek leads the Smugglers. In Chapter 3 "Outside the Law" you fight through Fallen Light, the Smugglers' secret headquarters in a crashed Heighliner, and help him defeat "the Face Dancer impersonating him" (game text). A boss named "The Minotaur" carries Esmar's id (`NPC_BOSS_MINOTAUR_ESMAR_TUEK`), and a "Mounted Minotaur Mask" placeable is the "Outside the Law Memento" (game text), so the Minotaur is very likely that impostor fight (inference).

Chapter 4 "Dangerous Allies": Esmar sends you to his son Staban Tuek at a Smuggler base overrun by mercenaries and Sardaukar; you retrieve a shigawire reel while Staban sets off explosives (game text). Thufir Hawat's dossier calls Esmar "a refined smuggler" who "sells to all, kneels to none" (game text).
