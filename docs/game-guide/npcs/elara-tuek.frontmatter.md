Elara Tuek is Count Fenring's contact (game text). Her internal NPC id is `NPC_NAMED_THE_SPIDER`, and journey task keys refer to her as "the Spider" (game text keys). Chapter 2 sends you to "Talk to Elara Tuek at Hannivar's" in Harko Village (game text).

- Act 2: "Accept one of the assassinations offered by Elara Tuek": the Glutton (slaver), the Paranoid (Maas Kharet leader), the Jackal (Arrakeen beggar) and a Water Shipper in his mansion near Arrakeen (game text).
- Chapter 3: she tracks a Face Dancer to an illegal dueling ring near Neo Carthag and later sends you to the Smugglers' base Fallen Light (game text).
- Chapter 4: daughter of Esmar Tuek; she plays Staban's shigawire reel ("The truth lies buried on Zanovar") and turns out to be the pilot who flies you to Zanovar (game text).
- Seron Varlin's chain uncovers a contract she issued to Sandflies to "punish" him (game text).
- Lost Harvest DLC: you work for her and Keif Villari in Harko Village (game text; see `world-and-maps.md`).
