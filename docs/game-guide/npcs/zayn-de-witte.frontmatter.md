Zayn de Witte unlocks Mentat level 2, level 3 and the capstones; his final contract is "Untwisted Questions" (game text). He is in Arrakeen; contracts end "Deliver the Minimic Film to Zayn in Arrakeen" (game text). The wiki puts him in Arrakeen's lower bar (wiki; unverified on this server).

- Story: "The Twisted Mentat Zayne employed me in his doomed obsession to impress Thufir Hawat" (game text, journey completion).
- His contracts send you to the Hagga Rift for deserter tissue samples and minimic film, to a slaver base for film, and to the Tleilaxu trader at Pinnacle Station about "the Tleilaxu twisting of the mind" (game text, `CONTRACT_MENTAT2_*`).
