# Swords (long blades)

Quick answer: the sword is longer and heavier than a kindjal and uses sweeping attacks that "help deal with multiple foes"; it can penetrate shields with slow-blade attacks (game text). Its parry staggers in an area, useful against groups (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Weapons, edited 2026-09-17). Long Blade Damage (Swordmaster) boosts swords and rapiers (game text).

## Craftable line (names game text, ids item data)

Standard Sword (`CHOAMSword`), Artisan Sword (`CHOAMSword_0`), House Sword (`CHOAMSword_1`), Adept Sword (`CHOAMSword_2`), Regis Sword (`CHOAMSword_3`). The Vermillius Gap journey sums it up: "The sword meets multiple enemies blade-to-blade" (game text).

## Unique swords (effects game text)

| Family | Names (tier) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Pulse-swords | Pseudo Pulse-Sword, Shock-sword, Spark-sword, Jolt-sword (`UniqueSword_04`), Replica Pulse-sword | Small stamina boost when you parry. Jolt-sword is a reward of the Swordmaster finale contract "The Last Stand of Seron Varlin" (community, https://selphie1999gaming.com/game-guides/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-unique-weapons-how-to-obtain-all-unique-weapon-schematics/, June 2025, pre-1.5). |
| Block-bleed swords | Sword of Seven Sorrows (4), Bite Back Blade (5), Spike Hilt Sword (6) | Lower block stamina cost; bleed attackers on a successful block. |
| Damasteel Sword | Unique, "favored by masters of the Ginaz school" | Tied to a Seron Varlin contract (game text, contract item "forged to Seron's specifications"). |
| Branding Blade | Chapter 4 unique | Burn buildup with every hit. |
| Aramsham's Caress (`SardaukarCaptain_Sword`) | Sardaukar | Very low stamina per swing; for long fights. |
| Asterion's End (`MTX_B1C3_Minotaur_Memento_Variant`) | Minotaur memento | "Brutally heavy blade"; the `MTX` id suggests a cosmetic/store or memento variant (item data; unverified). |

Atreides Shortsword and Sardaukar Blade ("a longer sword used for self-defense. Can penetrate shields") also exist (game text; `SardaukarBlade` in item data); how players get them is unverified. The Filmic Archive DLC adds a Sardaukar Oathblade (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
