# Trooper gunner build

Quick answer: the Trooper is the "Offense & Demolition" school (game text): guns and heavy weapons, grenades and fields, the Shigawire Claw, and suspensor-belt aerial play. A 1.5 guide calls a solo Trooper the forgiving, flexible choice and the Grenadier the easy early-game build (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds, 2026-10-11).

## Kit (names and effects game text; tree from the wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Trooper)

| Kind | Picks | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Ability | Shigawire Claw (starting ability) | Pull yourself to a point; also staggers opponents. |
| Ability | Explosive Grenade, Collapse Grenade, Assault Seeker, Attractor Field | Area damage, grouping and stun, direct seeker, redirect shots to a marked enemy. |
| Ability | Suspensor Blast, Gravity Field, Anti-gravity Field, Energy Capsule | Knockback blast; slow and power drain; lift; ranged damage and accuracy buff. |
| Technique | Battle Hardened, Heavy Weapon Agility, Center of Mass, Death from Above, Suspensor Dash | Lower Trooper cooldowns; move faster with heavy weapons; body and shield damage up, headshots down; damage while airborne; mid-air dash. |
| Passive | Ranged Damage, Heavy Weapon Damage, Scattergun Damage, Disruptor Damage, Gunsmith, Field Maintenance, Suspensor Efficiency | |
| Prescient | Reflexive Reload | Grenades and mines reload your weapon instantly. |

## Gear

- Weapons: Disruptor M11 for shields, GRDA 44 or Drillshot FK7 close, Vulcan GAU-92, flamethrower, Pyrocket, missile launcher or lasgun as the heavy (game text names these as heavy weapons).
- Armor: the Trooper school set is the only heavy school set (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-archetype-armor-sets-and-how-to-unlock-them); a Full Suspensor Belt for crossing open ground (community, banrado.de).

## Builds named by guides

- Solo Trooper: Tactical Tech plus Suspensor Training; Claw for escape and staggers (banrado.de).
- Grenadier Nomad: cooldown-based explosives and mobility; clears early zones without relying on ammo or precise parries (banrado.de).
- Trooper plus Mentat group pair: Trooper brings grenades and flank control, Mentat marks targets and holds ground with turrets (banrado.de).
- A PvE guide mixes Trooper with Planetologist and Mentat picks (community, https://pecsandbox.com/dune-awakening-trooper-best-pve-build-guide/, pre-1.5).
