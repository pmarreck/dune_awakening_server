# Drillshot FK7 (burrow-dart shotguns)

Quick answer: the Drillshot FK7 is a double-barreled, mid-range "anti-shield" shotgun. Its burrow darts stick to an active shield and drill through after a short time, then damage the target behind it (game text). The wiki adds that the target can avoid it by dodging (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Weapons, edited 2026-09-17).

## Lines (names game text, ids item data)

Smuggler Elephant Gun (`D_SmugShot1`, deprecated), House Drillshot FK7 (`SmugShot3`), Adept (`SmugShot4`), Regis (`SmugShot5`). The FK7 arrives with the aluminum-tier O'odham journey: its burrowing darts open "a new dimension to shielded combat" (game text).

## Uniques (effects game text)

| Name | Effect |
|---|---|
| House / Adept / Regis Burst Drillshot | Larger capacity; charges up to fire a two-dart burst of burrow darts. |
| Penetrator (`Shotgun_Unique_Blood_06`) | Deep-drilling darts that pass slowly through a shield, then keep drilling to cause bleeding wounds. |
| Ambition | Two slugs in each barrel. |

## Augments (game text)

Drillshot FK7 Tactical Adjuster, Amplifier, Optimizer, Sprayer, Quickloader, Spray-and-Pray; Scattergun Rampage-Enhancement also fits since 1.5 (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).

Scattergun Damage (Trooper) applies to the Drillshot FK7 (game text).
