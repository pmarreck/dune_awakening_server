# Specializations and combat traits

Quick answer: Specializations are five tracks (Crafting, Gathering, Exploration, Combat, Sabotage) leveled by Landsraad missions of the matching category. Each level gives an automatic bonus, and Traits are bought with Spice Melange (game text). For fighting, the Combat track gives skill points, health, stamina, damage and mitigation; Sabotage gives headshot damage and damage to staggered targets; Crafting gives augment slots (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Specialization, edited 2026-10-03). General rules are in [../progression-and-skills.md](../progression-and-skills.md#specializations).

## Combat track (wiki)

- Automatic per level: +1% Damage Bonus (up to +100% at level 100) and +0.5% Damage Mitigation (up to +50%).
- Traits: Skill Points at many levels (+54 total by 100), Max Stamina Increase (+55 total by 95), Max Health Increase (+55 total by 91), and a cosmetic Combatant's Helm Variant at 75.
- Trait costs run from 2 Spice Melange early to 40 at level 100; each track totals 44,182 XP and 750 Spice to level 100 (wiki; costs unverified in game).

## Sabotage track (wiki)

- Automatic per level: +0.5% Damage to Staggered Targets (+50% at 100; the page's own intro says +29.5%, so the figure is uncertain).
- Combat-relevant traits: Head Hunter (+5% headshot damage at levels 1, 10, 27, 50, 74, 100), Discreet (enemy detection range), Hard to Spot (recognition speed), Shadow and Infiltrator (scanning), Explosive (+100% explosive barrel damage), "What's Theirs is Mine" (extra loot from corpses).

## Crafting track

Ranged, Melee and Garment Augmentation Limit traits and augment-quality traits; see [augments](augments.frontmatter.md).

The server setting "Specialization Experience" multiplies specialization XP (game text). Trait names such as Max Health Increase, Head Hunter, Wall Crawler and the augmentation limits are game text.
