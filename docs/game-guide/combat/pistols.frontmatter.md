# Pistols (Maula, Rafiq Snubnose, deadshot)

Quick answer: the Maula Pistol is the light, short-range starter sidearm you craft in the first journey; the Rafiq Snubnose is the high-caliber pistol with more punch and a slower rate of fire (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Weapons). Pistol Damage (Mentat passive) boosts both (game text).

## Craftable lines (names game text, ids item data)

| Line | Tiers |
|---|---|
| Maula Pistol (light darts) | Improvised `ChoamSda1`, Maula Pistol `ChoamSda2`, Standard `ChoamSda3`, Artisan, House, Adept, Regis `ChoamSda7` |
| Rafiq Snubnose ("high caliber pistol") | Rafiq Snubnose Pistol `HarkHeavyPistol1`, Artisan `HarkHeavyPistol2`, House `HarkHeavyPistol3`, Adept `HarkHeavyPistol4`, Regis `HarkHeavyPistol5` |

The Jabal Eifrit journey notes the Spitdart and Snubnose "deliver far more punch than previous weapons" at the steel tier (game text).

## Unique pistols (effects game text)

| Family | Names | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Spring Maulas ("Way of the ...") | Fallen, Wanderer, Lost, Fighter, Desert, Misr | Automatic spring reloads darts without releasing the trigger (full-auto Maula). |
| Dart spitters | Shadrath's Edge (Al), Taqwa's Double (Dural), Shishakli's Bite (Plast) | Fire two darts at once, less accurate; Shadrath's punctures Sardaukar armor. |
| Deadshot | Cope (Dural), Seethe (`HeavyPistol_Unique_Headshot_06`, Plast) | More accuracy and headshot damage, small clip. |
| Heavy disruptor pistols | Artisan / House / Adept / Regis Disruptor Pistol | See [disruptors](disruptors.frontmatter.md). |
| Chapter 4 | Hell-Fury Pistol (burn buildup on hit), Taligari Viper | |
| Story | Ari's Pistol (accuracy while prescient) | |

Seethe is a recommended pick for the Testing Station 89 and 24 bosses (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_89, edited 2026-10-05).

## Augments (game text)

Maula: Sprayer, Disruptive Coating, Expander, Barrel Extender, Tactical Enhancer, Antipersonnel Rounds. Snubnose: Heavy Kit, Disruptive Coating, Quickdraw Assembly, Expander, Sprayer, Marksman. Older weapon "mods" also exist in text: Maula Pistol Coater (poison), Rafiq Snubnose Explosive Gel (game text; whether obtainable in 1.5 is unverified).
