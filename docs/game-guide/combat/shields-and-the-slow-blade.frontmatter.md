# Shields and the slow blade

Quick answer: a shielded target takes almost no damage from fast blows and darts. Stagger it (three light attacks or a parry), then hold attack for a heavy "slow blade" attack, which passes through the shield; or hurt it with poison or fire, or pour concentrated fire into it (disruptors do this best) until the shield drops (game text, tutorials "Shielded NPCs" and "Heavy and Slow Blade Attacks").

## How a shield behaves

- You toggle a Holtzman Shield on manually. It deflects darts and blades at the cost of power from your Power Pack, and it can short-circuit if too many projectiles hit at once (game text).
- It blocks your own outgoing shots too, so it switches off whenever you fire or throw something and switches back on after its refresh time. After it breaks, it comes back once the power pack has recharged (game text). The wiki adds that a fully drained shield cannot be reactivated until the pack is full again (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Holtzman_Shield, edited 2026-09-19).
- An active shield on open sand drives sandworms mad and draws them much faster; do not use it there (game text).
- Blades can still stagger a shielded character, and constant fire such as a Vulcan's slows the target (wiki, same page).
- Explosions: shrapnel cannot pierce a shield, but a grenade's shockwave still damages a shielded enemy (game text, Explosive Grenade). Rockets are only partly mitigated (wiki, same page).
- Never fire a lasgun into a shield. In lore the result is a nuclear blast; in game, imported lasguns carry a safety override that shuts them off when aimed at a shield (game text).

## Ways through a shield

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Slow blade (heavy attack) | "Heavy attacks are slow, break block and cause stagger if they land" and penetrate shields; staggered enemies are more vulnerable to them (game text). Since 1.5 a heavy attack does the same damage as a shield-penetrating attack (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/). |
| Poison and fire | Pass through shields (game text). Poison rapiers release gas "capable of passing through a shield"; poison long rifles burst on contact with a shield and the poison "wafts through" (game text). |
| Disruptors | Fire variable-velocity darts that find gaps, then pulse the shield's power; "if enough disruptor darts penetrate, a shield can be taken offline" (game text). See [disruptors](disruptors.frontmatter.md). |
| Burrow darts | Drillshot FK7 darts stick to a shield and penetrate after a short time (game text). See [drillshot shotguns](drillshot-shotguns.frontmatter.md). |
| Concentrated fire | Enough hits overwhelm and short-circuit the shield (game text). Weapons list a separate "Shield Damage" stat (game text, item stats). |
| Dual Blades flurry | Holding attack starts frenzied hits that "partially bypass shields" (game text). |
| Abilities | Expunge is a melee area attack that bypasses shields; Hunter-Seekers act as a burrow dart against a shield; Shield Overcharge (Mentat, prescient) makes ranged hits drain the target's pack faster (game text). |
| EMP rifle | The unique Experimental Lightning Gun fires EMP blasts that disrupt shields (game text). |

## 1.5 changes

- Shield damage in PvP reduced by 33% (patch notes 1.5).
- Endgame NPCs scale their shield power, shield regeneration and damage to shields with Testing Station difficulty (game text, dungeon scaling dialog).
