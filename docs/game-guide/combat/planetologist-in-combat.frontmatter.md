# Planetologist in combat

Quick answer: the Planetologist tree is almost all gathering, travel and vehicle passives, so it is not a combat school. Its fight-relevant pieces are Suspensor Pad (launch yourself and targets into the air), Overcharge and Rerouting (bigger, faster-recharging power pack, which keeps shields and lasguns running), Mountaineer and Conservation of Energy (game text; tree from the wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Planetologist, see [../progression-and-skills.md](../progression-and-skills.md)).

- Planetologist is not a starting class; it opens at Derek Chinara's camp (game text).
- The school armor set unpacks into unique stillsuits (game text), useful for water rather than fighting. A pre-1.5 guide says its cooldown bonus only helps Suspensor Pad (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-archetype-armor-sets-and-how-to-unlock-them).
- The Testing Station 152 boss is a Planetologist (Planetologist Takulki) with a disruptor pistol, Suspensor Blast and Stunner, not a Planetologist-tree kit (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_152).
- Typical use: spend points here for crafting and harvesting, then put combat points into one fighting school; see [combining skill trees](combining-skill-trees.frontmatter.md). A Trooper PvE guide takes some Planetologist picks (community, https://pecsandbox.com/dune-awakening-trooper-best-pve-build-guide/, pre-1.5).
