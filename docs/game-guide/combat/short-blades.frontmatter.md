# Short blades (kindjals, dirks, knives)

Quick answer: short blades are the fast, short-reach melee weapons, with a forgiving parry window. The craftable lines are the Atreides Kindjal and the Harkonnen Dirk; both "can penetrate shields" with slow-blade attacks (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Weapons). Short Blade Damage (Bene Gesserit passive) boosts them (game text; wiki).

## Craftable lines (names game text, ids item data)

| Line | Tiers |
|---|---|
| Atreides Kindjal ("curved short blade") | Kindjal `Kindjal`, Standard `Kindjal_0`, Artisan `Kindjal_1`, House `Kindjal_2`, Adept `Kindjal_3`, Regis `Kindjal_4` |
| Harkonnen Dirk ("short dagger used for self-defense") | Artisan Dirk `Dirk`, House `Dirk_0`, Adept `Dirk_1`, Regis `Dirk_3` (one more rung, "Dirk Mark III", is unnamed in text) |
| Scrap Metal Knife (`ScrapMetalKnife`) | Makeshift starter, crafted in the tutorial to cut a membrane (game text) |
| Unfixed Crysknife (`Crysknife`) | Made from a sandworm's tooth; "very brittle. Cannot leave the owner's person" (game text). How it is obtained is unverified. |

The copper Kindjal deals 7.18 per hit at attack speed 139 and is crafted from 4 Copper Ingot, 3 Plasteel Microflora Fiber and 3 Plant Fiber (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Kindjal, edited 2026-10-05; numbers may predate 1.5 balance).
