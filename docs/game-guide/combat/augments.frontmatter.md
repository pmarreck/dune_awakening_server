# Augments and augment slots

Quick answer: only plastanium (tier 6) Unique weapons and armor take augments. You craft an augment from a schematic (dropped in Testing Stations) plus Regis Components, then attach it at an Augmentation Station. Augments are permanent and each costs the item maximum durability (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Augmentation). Slots come from the Crafting specialization's Augmentation Limit traits.

## Slots: Crafting specialization traits (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Crafting, edited 2026-10-03)

| Trait | Levels | Each gives |
|---|---|---|
| Ranged Augmentation Limit | 1, 31, 87 | +1 ranged slot |
| Melee Augmentation Limit | 3, 30, 88 | +1 melee slot |
| Garment Augmentation Limit | 10, 42 | +1 armor slot |
| Augment Fabrication Mastery | 20, 33, 55, 78, 96 | Better chance of high stat rolls |
| Augment Cost Reduction | 25, 59, 90 | -10% augment crafting cost |

Trait names and descriptions are game text ("Allows additional augments to be slotted onto ranged weapons", and so on). Whether an item has any slot before the first trait is unverified; the Chapter 3 journey has you buy the Ranged Augmentation Limit trait before your first augment (game text).

## Making and attaching

- Schematics drop from Testing Stations; recycling unwanted ones gives Augmentation Schematic Patterns (game text). Higher difficulty gives higher grades (game text, scaling dialog). 1.5 grade bands: Grade 3 not after difficulty 20, Grade 4 rarer from 27, Grade 5 from 20 (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Regis Components come from Offworld Regis Compounds bought from the Landsraad crafting vendor and fabricated at the Advanced Survival Fabricator (game text, journey "Mnemonic Devices"). One guide prices a compound at 550 House Scrip and says one craft yields 10 components (community, https://method.gg/dune-awakening/augmentations-in-dune-awakening-how-to-unlock-how-they-work-more, February 2026, pre-1.5).
- Augments are fabricated at the Advanced Weapons Fabricator (weapons) or Advanced Garment Fabricator (armor) (game text; community above).
- Augmentation Station: 1.5 removed Spice Melange from its build cost (patch notes 1.5).
- Each augment lowers the item's maximum durability; the wiki says by 2% (wiki). The item needs enough durability to accept one (game text).
- 1.5 made augment stat rolls a bell curve: better rolls more often, same odds of the maximum (patch notes 1.5).
- Grade example: Garment Reinforcement gives +2 to 4% armor at Grade 1 up to +17 to 20% at Grade 5 (wiki).

## Augment types (names and effects game text)

- Any ranged weapon: Heavy Caliber Upgrade (damage), House Heavy Caliber Upgrade, Disruptive Coating (shield damage), Tactical Enhancer (headshots), Precision Barrel Adjuster (accuracy), Barrel Extender (range), Quick-release Trigger (fire rate), Recoil Adjuster, Quickloader (reload), Capacity Expander (ammo, more volume).
- Any melee weapon: Blade Sharpener (damage), Blade Grip Adjuster (attack stamina), Blade Flexi-Coating (block stamina), Aggressive / Defensive Grip Adjuster, Lightweight Adjuster, Heavy Blade Adjuster, Blade Optimizer, Edge Optimizer, Heavy Metal Blade Coating (much more damage, much heavier; Grade 3+ in 1.5), Blade Blood Grooves (lighter, cheaper stamina, less damage).
- Weapon-specific: Karpov 38, JABAL Spitdart, Maula, Rafiq Snubnose, GRDA 44, Drillshot FK7, Disruptor M11, Vulcan GAU-92, Lasgun, Flamethrower, Pyrocket, Missile Launcher; see each weapon entry.
- Garment: one resistance up, another down. Concussive Dampening (concussive up, energy down), Concussive Redirection (concussive up, dart down), Woven Reinforcement (blade up; poison and fire down), Penetrative Reinforcement (dart up, blade down), Energy Dispersing Padding (energy up, concussive down), Curative-infused Padding (poison up, fire down), Energy Resistant Weave (energy up, poison down), Detoxifying Fabrics (poison up, energy down), Flame-resistant Fabrics (fire up, poison down), Blade-warding Weave (blade up, dart down), Dart-proof Latticing (dart up, more volume), Garment Reinforcement (armor), Desert-tested Weave (heat protection), Radiation Absorbing Fabrics and Radiation Shielded Latticing.
- Universal: Protective Coating, which removes durability loss on defeat (Grade 5 sets it to 0, wiki).
