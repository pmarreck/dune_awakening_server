# Disruptors (Disruptor M11 and heavy disruptor pistols)

Quick answer: disruptors are the dedicated shield breakers. A Holtzman shield stops fast projectiles, so disruptors fire darts at varied speeds to find gaps; once inside, each dart pulses the shield's power, and enough of them take the shield offline (game text). The tutorial names them "more effective at disabling shields than others" (game text). Disruptor Damage (Trooper passive) boosts them (game text).

## Craftable line (names game text, ids item data)

Savoy & Mirt M11 "rapid fire disruptor": Standard Disruptor M11 (`AtreSmg2`), Artisan (`AtreSmg3`), House (`AtreSmg4`), Adept (`AtreSmg5`), Regis (`AtreSmg6`). The Vermillius Gap journey has you craft the Standard M11; "The Disruptor overpowers shielded foes" (game text).

## Augments (game text)

Disruptor M11 Precision Tuning, Quickloader, Shield Breaker (more shield damage and range, slower fire), Sprayer, Amplifier, Heavy Kit, Personnel-Buster (more critical damage and fire rate, less shield damage). Disruptive Coating raises shield damage on any ranged weapon.

The Filmic Archive DLC adds a "Benediction Disruptor" (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/). A 1.5 guide calls disruptors the ranged counter to Swordmaster melee in PvP (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds).
