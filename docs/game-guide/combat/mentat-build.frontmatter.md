# Mentat build

Quick answer: the Mentat is "Recon & Strategy" (game text): ranged and headshot damage, weak points, poison tools, and deployables (The Sentinel turret, Hunter-Seeker, decoys, Shield Wall). Guides use it as a long-range sniper for clearing camps and as a group "anchor" that holds ground with turrets (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds, 2026-10-11).

## Kit (names and effects game text; tree from the wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Mentat)

| Branch | Picks |
|---|---|
| Mental Calculus | The Sentinel (starting ability, motion-tracking dart turret); Exploit Weakness (reveal and shoot a weak point), Marksman (headshots); Ranged, Rifle and Pistol Damage; Shield Overcharge (prescient). |
| Assassination | Hunter-Seeker, Stunner (1.5: cooldown 25 to 15 s, 15 stamina, patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/), Poison Mine, Poison Capsule; Poison Tooth; Assassin's Shot (back shots), Headshot Damage. |
| Tactician | Shield Wall, Solido Decoy, Gravity Mine, Anti-gravity Mine, Source of Power; Iron Will (resist the Voice). |

Unplaced names that fit the Mentat's toys (game text, tree unverified): Overdrive (seekers), Feedback Routines and Emergency Decoys (decoys), Squadron Alpha and Omega (+1 seeker each), Precise Calibration, Mesh Network, Organized Lines, Legion, Planned Recurrence, Mentat Efficiency, Battlefield Calculation, Enemy Analysis, Weapons Projection, Weak-Point Focus.

## Builds named by guides

- Sniper Mentat: scoped poison rifles, Marksman or Exploit Weakness (+20% headshots and +30% from weak spots per banrado.de), clear from range (community).
- Mentat anchor (group): The Sentinel, Shield Wall, Source of Power; Thufir's Best or the Regis Tripleshot Repeating Rifle (banrado.de).
- Mentat plus Trooper: marks and turrets plus grenades and flank control (banrado.de).

Armor: the Tactical Coat of the Mentat school set (game text); light armor generally. See [rifles](rifles.frontmatter.md) and [headshots](headshots-and-weak-points.frontmatter.md).
