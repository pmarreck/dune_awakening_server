# Rifles (Karpov 38, JABAL Spitdart, long rifles)

Quick answer: the Karpov 38 is the gas-powered automatic assault rifle for medium range; the JABAL Spitdart is a rapid-cycling scoped rifle whose darts poison targets, for long range (game text). Rifle Damage (Mentat) names both (game text).

## Craftable lines (names game text, ids item data)

| Line | Tiers |
|---|---|
| Karpov 38 | Karpov 38 `HarkAr2`, Standard `HarkAr3`, Artisan, House, Adept, Regis `HarkAr7` |
| JABAL Spitdart | JABAL Spitdart Rifle, Artisan `SmugDmr3`, House `SmugDmr4`, Adept `SmugDmr5`, Regis `SmugDmr6` |

The Spitdart applies its own "Spitdart Poison" debuff (game text). It does not take Light Darts (verified on this server, 2026-10-26, Adept `SmugDmr5`), whatever some community guides say; Heavy Darts (`HeavyAmmo`) are the likely ammo but are unverified.

## Augments (game text)

Karpov 38: Pressurizer, Dart Sprayer, Heavy Kit, Focuser, Precision Kit, Sniper Barrel (range and accuracy, slower reload). Spitdart: Tactical Enhancer, Range Adjuster, Amplifier, Disruptive Coating, Expander, Toxicity Amplifier (more poison, less shield damage), Focuser.

Thufir's Best and the Regis Tripleshot are the rifles a 1.5 group guide names for a Mentat anchor (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds, 2026-10-11).
