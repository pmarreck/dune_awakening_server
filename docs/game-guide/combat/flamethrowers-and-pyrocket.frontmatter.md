# Flamethrowers and the Pyrocket

Quick answer: fire passes through shields ("Holtzman shields provide very little protection against being slowly cooked"), so flamethrowers are strong against shielded enemies; they burn Incendiary Gel (`Napalm`) and apply burn over time (game text). The Pyrocket, added after launch, lobs balls of ignited gel that explode in a large radius (game text; Funcom, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/).

## Weapons (names game text, ids item data)

| Name | Notes |
|---|---|
| Scorchbolt (`Flamethrower_Prototype`) | Garoti portable flamethrower; Unique-tagged. Sandflies NPCs use it. |
| Glasser (`Flamethrower_Prototype_2`) | Mark II Scorchbolt. |
| Shaitan's Tongue (`UniqueFlameThrower`), Vaporizer (`UniqueFlameThrower_02`) | Higher gel capacity, more intense flame. |
| Pyrocket (`Fireballer_1`), Duraluminum Pyrocket (`Fireballer_2`), Plastanium Pyrocket (`Fireballer_3`) | Fireballs detonate on impact for area damage; the gel clings and burns. |

Molten-dart scatterguns and the burning dirks and swords also deal fire; see [scatterguns](scatterguns.frontmatter.md) and [short blades](short-blades.frontmatter.md).

## Augments (game text)

Flamethrower Amplifier, Superheater (faster spray, worse burns, less range), Nozzle Extension, Quickloader, Decimator (huge shield damage, slower fire). Pyrocket Supercharger, Quickloader, Decimator (huge shield damage, smaller magazine).

Heavy Weapon Damage and Heavy Weapon Agility (Trooper) list the flamethrower (game text). Masano the Burned (Testing Station 136) uses Pyrocket fireballs; see [boss tactics](pve-boss-tactics.frontmatter.md). The Static Compactor clears fire clouds (game text).
