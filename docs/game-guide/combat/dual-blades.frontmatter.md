# Dual Blades

Quick answer: Dual Blades pair a kindjal with a slip-tip knife. Holding the attack button starts a sequence of frenzied hits that "partially bypass shields" (game text). Funcom added them after launch, alongside the Pyrocket (Funcom, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/).

## Getting them

| Item (id) | Notes |
|---|---|
| Adept Dual Blades (`DualBlades_1`) | Duraluminum tier by the name ladder (inferred). |
| Regis Dual Blades (`DualBlades_2`) | Schematic unlocks after the Chapter 3 journey "A Deadly Poison" and costs 40 Intel; crafted at the Advanced Weapons Fabricator from 25 Plastanium Ingot, 12 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 3 Plant Fiber, 12 Plasteel Composite Blade Parts, 7 Diamondine Dust and 20 Spice Melange plus 440 mL water (wiki, https://www.awakening.wiki/Regis_Dual_Blades, edited 2026-09-05, pre-1.5 costs). |
| Feyd's Spare Blades (`DualBlades_Memento`) | "A set of Feyd-Rautha's dual blades" (game text). Source unverified. |
| Burning Blades (`UniqueDualBlades_6`) | Heated blades that tear and scorch, designed by Masano of the Sandflies (game text), the Testing Station 136 boss. |
| Leech's Maw (`B1C4_Unique_DualBlades1`) | Chapter 4 unique: every hit drains the target's power (game text). The 1.5.15.0 test notes give it the same journey requirement as the other unique dual blades (patch notes 1.5.15.0, https://duneawakening.com/news/public-test-client-patch-1-5-15-0/). |

"A Deadly Poison" is the Art of Kanly arena fight against Sinnu the Poisoner (game text).

## Combat notes

- Item and id names are game text and item data. Damage passives: Short Blade Damage and Blade Damage likely apply (inference from the kindjal/slip-tip makeup; unverified).
- 1.5: parry window for dual blades 0.25 to 0.33 s, 10 stamina per parry; flurries no longer end a target's Second Wind early (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Wiki: fast sweeping attacks that pressure shields but may need many hits against heavy defenses (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Weapons). A guide recommends dual blades for clearing summoned suboids in Testing Station 24 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_24, edited 2026-10-10).
