# Lasguns

Quick answer: lasguns exist, as endgame unique heavy weapons. They use power-pack energy instead of darts, are "slow to fire ... extremely powerful", and cut out automatically if aimed at a Holtzman shield (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Lasgun). Use them on unshielded targets, vehicles and structures (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Weapons).

## The two lasguns

| Item (id) | How to get |
|---|---|
| Arhun K-28 Lasgun (`ChoamLg1`) | Reward of the "Cutting Edge" contract chain from the Crossroads Tradepost board: hand in a new Cutteray Mk6, fetch an amplifier from the Wreck of the Tartarus (radiation) and a modulator from the Wreck of the Delphis, then kill four Maas Kharet assassins; about 64.2 damage per shot (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/how-to-get-a-lasgun-in-dune-awakening-cutting-edge-quest-reward, July 2025, pre-1.5). A pre-1.5 list also places its schematic in Testing Stations 37 and 185 (community, https://selphie1999gaming.com/game-guides/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-unique-weapons-how-to-obtain-all-unique-weapon-schematics/). |
| Black Market K-28 Lasgun (`ChoamLg2`) | Unique plastanium schematic, crafted at the Advanced Weapons Fabricator with Thermo-responsive Ray Amplifiers (method.gg above, about 84.7 damage per shot, pre-1.5). Now a 1.4% schematic drop from Testing Station 195 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_195, edited 2026-10-09). |

The K-28's lore: a lasgun hitting a hot shield leveled Carthag, so imported lasguns carry a shield-detection safety; the Black Market version is "just a regular lasgun with the serial numbers filed off" and keeps the safety (game text).

## Skills and augments

Heavy Weapon Damage and Heavy Weapon Agility (Trooper) name the lasgun (game text). Augments: Lasgun Focuser, Extender, Harmonizer (lower power drain, faster beam), Amplifier, Concentrator (game text). Power-pack tier sets how efficiently it fires (wiki); see [shields and power packs](holtzman-shields-and-power-packs.frontmatter.md).
