# Blocking and parrying

Quick answer: hold the aim/block button to block. Time the block to the instant the enemy's swing lands and you parry instead, which staggers the attacker and gives back some stamina (game text). Update 1.5 widened the parry window for swords, daggers and dual blades from 0.25 s to 0.33 s, and a successful parry with those weapons returns 10 stamina (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).

## Details

- Block is "[HOLD] Block"; the same input parries ("Aim/Block/Parry" in controls) (game text).
- Heavy (slow) attacks break a block (game text), so a blocking enemy is opened with a heavy attack and a heavy swing coming at you is better dodged than blocked.
- Blocking costs stamina; each melee weapon lists a Block Stamina Cost (game text).
- 1.5: holding right-click after a successful parry returns you to blocking (patch notes 1.5).
- The 1.5.1.0 test notes gave 25 stamina per parry; the shipped value is 10 (patch notes 1.5 versus PTC 1.5.1.0). See [stamina](stamina-in-combat.frontmatter.md).
- Rapiers parry differently: their opening attack is itself a parry. Attack parries and steps forward; aim parries and steps back (game text, Atreides Rapier). The wiki says the rapier's window differs from every other melee weapon (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Rapier, edited 2026-03-20, pre-1.5).

## Skills, gear and augments that use parry or block

| Name | Effect (game text) |
|---|---|
| Foil (Swordmaster ability) | Parry the next melee attack and counter; the target deals less damage for a while. |
| Retaliate (Swordmaster ability) | Parry the next melee attack and riposte with a bleeding strike. |
| Precise Parry (Swordmaster, prescient) | Your parries lower the enemy's damage mitigation. |
| Bindu Dodge (Bene Gesserit, prescient) | After a successful parry, dart and blade attacks pass through you. |
| Prescient Edge (unique rapier) | Bleeds the opponent on every successful parry. |
| Shock-sword, Spark-sword, Replica Pulse-sword | A small stamina boost when you parry. |
| Sword of Seven Sorrows, Bite Back Blade, Spike Hilt Sword | Lower block stamina cost and bleed an attacker on a successful block. |
| Blade Flexi-Coating, Defensive Grip Adjuster, Blade Optimizer (augments) | Lower block stamina cost, with trade-offs. |

Wiki melee notes (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Weapons, edited 2026-09-17): kindjals have "a large, forgiving parry window"; swords parry with an area stagger that works against groups.
