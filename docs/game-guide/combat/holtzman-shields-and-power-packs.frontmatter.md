# Holtzman shield and power pack gear

Quick answer: craft a Holtzman Shield after researching it (the Vermillius Gap journey walks you through the Mk2, the Jabal Eifrit one the Mk3) and pair it with a Power Pack; the shield drains the pack with every deflection (game text). Mechanics are in [shields and the slow blade](shields-and-the-slow-blade.frontmatter.md).

## Power packs (game text; ids item data)

Improvised Power Pack, then Mk1 (`PowerPack5`) to Mk6, plus Accelerator Power Pack (`PowerPack_Unique_Capacity_06`, extra capacity) and Purifier Power Pack (poison and radiation mitigation by id). Packs recharge when not in use (game text). Skills: Overcharge (more capacity) and Rerouting (faster recharge) (Planetologist, game text); Source of Power (Mentat) is a deployable generator that recharges allies' power (game text). The Accelerator pack is suggested for Swordmaster PvP to keep the shield up against burst (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds) and drops in Testing Station 89 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_89).

Things that drain your power in a fight: shield deflections, Gravity Field and Gravity Mine, Leech's Maw, Static Needle, Reverse Flow decoy discharges, Proximity Discharge, electrified floors in Testing Station 152 (game text; wiki).

## Suspensor belts

Suspensor belts use Holtzman fields too and also anger sandworms (game text). Combat-relevant: Sentinel Belt (unique, id says damage reduction), Elohim-Class Suspensor Belt (unique, durability), The Emperor's Wings Mk1 to Mk6 (glide) (game text). Trooper's Suspensor Training tree and Death from Above build on being airborne (game text).
