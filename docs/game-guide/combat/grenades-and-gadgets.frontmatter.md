# Grenades, mines and gadgets

Quick answer: there are no grenade items to craft in 1.5. Grenades, mines and seekers are abilities you learn in a skill tree and equip in an ability slot (game text). Grenade schematics in the item text are marked "NOTUSED" (game text). Explosive Grenade and Collapse Grenade are Trooper skills; mines and seekers are mostly Mentat (wiki tree pages, see [../progression-and-skills.md](../progression-and-skills.md)).

## Throwables and fields (effects game text; school per wiki)

| Ability | School | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Explosive Grenade | Trooper | Timed shrapnel grenade; shrapnel cannot pierce a shield but the shockwave still hurts a shielded enemy. |
| Collapse Grenade | Trooper | Pulls everyone in a sphere together and briefly stuns them; follow with area attacks. |
| Gravity Field | Trooper | Sphere that slows enemies, drains their power and slows projectiles. |
| Anti-gravity Field | Trooper | Sphere that lifts enemies or prevents fall damage. |
| Assault Seeker | Trooper | Flies straight and explodes on contact, or on a second press. |
| Attractor Field | Trooper | Attach to an enemy; shots into the field redirect to their center of mass. Wiki boss pages recommend it against Masano and Takulki. |
| Suspensor Blast | Trooper | Explodes your belt's field around you: damage, knockback, slow. Hold to charge. |
| Poison Mine | Mentat | Remote mine releasing a poison spore cloud that stacks poison. |
| Poison Capsule | Mentat | Dart that bursts into a poison cloud. |
| Gravity Mine / Anti-gravity Mine | Mentat | Remote versions of the two fields. |
| Hunter-Seeker | Mentat | Piloted assassination dart; you are vulnerable while flying it. |
| The Sentinel | Mentat | Deployed motion-tracking dart turret. |
| Shield Wall | Mentat | Half-height shield cover that slows enemies passing through. |
| Solido Decoy | Mentat | Projected copy of you to draw fire. |
| Stunner | Mentat | Sedative dart that stuns. |

Other names exist without wiki placement: Impact Frag Grenade, Explosive Mine, Stabilization Bomb, Remote Stabilization Mine, Trophy Seeker (intercepts slow projectiles) (game text).

## Synergies (game text)

Reflexive Reload (Trooper, prescient): throwing a grenade or dropping a mine instantly reloads your weapon. Battle Hardened (Trooper) shortens Trooper cooldowns. Viscous Concoctions, Enhanced Dispersal, Heavy Aerosols and Powered Sprays make clouds bigger or longer. Precise Calibration extends seeker range; Squadron Alpha and Omega raise the seeker cap.

1.5 removed the Suspensor Grenade from most NPCs and shortened NPC Suspensor Blast range (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
