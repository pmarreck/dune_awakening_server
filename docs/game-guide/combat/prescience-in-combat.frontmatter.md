# Prescience in combat

Quick answer: eating or drinking spice, or standing in spice fields, builds toward the Prescient state. While prescient you get a third ability slot, shorter ability cooldowns, more damage, more passive healing, poison tolerance and enemy info (game text). Afterwards comes Withdrawal, and rising Tolerance makes the next trip harder; see [../survival-combat-crafting.md](../survival-combat-crafting.md#spice).

## Skills that need Prescience (game text)

| Skill | School (wiki) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Precise Parry | Swordmaster | Your parries lower the enemy's damage mitigation. |
| Prescient Strike | Swordmaster | After any Swordmaster ability, your next strike hits twice; the second hit does 70% of the first. |
| Bindu Dodge | Bene Gesserit | After a successful parry, dart and blade attacks pass through you. |
| Screech | Bene Gesserit | Voice staggers those around your registered target. |
| Shield Overcharge | Mentat | Ranged hits on a shield reveal a weak spot that drains the target's power pack faster. |
| Reflexive Reload | Trooper | Throwing a grenade or dropping a mine instantly reloads your weapon. |
| Heat Management | Planetologist | Less vehicle heat buildup. |

School placement is from the wiki's tree pages (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Swordmaster and siblings, edited 2026-09 to 2026-10).

## Capstone-style skills with a prescient bonus (game text; trees unconfirmed)

Weak-Point Focus (allies can use your weak point), Overdrive (seekers stronger), Feedback Routines (decoys pulse to reveal life signs), Spice Infused Poisons (more poison damage), Spice Medicine (stronger heal-over-time), Spiced Death (Anigolah minions refresh and enrage). Overdrive and Feedback Routines fit the Mentat's seekers and decoys; the rest are unplaced (see [../progression-and-skills.md](../progression-and-skills.md)).

## Uniques

Ari's Pistol, a story Maula Pistol, "greatly increases accuracy while Prescient" (game text).
