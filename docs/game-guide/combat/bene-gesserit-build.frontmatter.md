# Bene Gesserit build

Quick answer: the Bene Gesserit is "Physical Mastery & Manipulation" (game text): Voice abilities that pull, freeze or blind enemies, Weirding Way strikes and sprints, and Body Control passives for health and poison resistance. Voice abilities need a moment to "register" the target first (game text). A 1.5 guide builds a burst "Bladedancer" for solo PvE and Testing Stations around Ignore and Stop (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds, 2026-10-11).

## Kit (names and effects game text; tree from the wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Bene_Gesserit)

| Kind | Picks | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | Compel (starting ability) | Pull a target to you. 1.5: 3 levels, cooldown 30 to 20 s, 15 stamina (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/). |
| Voice | Stop | Freeze enemies in a cone. Same 1.5 numbers as Compel. 1.5.15.0 test notes block the victim's stamina regeneration during the stun (patch notes 1.5.15.0). |
| Voice | Ignore | One target treats you as invisible. 15 stamina in 1.5. |
| Weirding Way | Weirding Step | Stun one enemy and appear behind it. 1.5: cooldown 25/20/15 s, 15 stamina. |
| Weirding Way | Bindu Sprint, Prana-Bindu Strikes | Very fast sprint; a staggering flurry. |
| Body Control | Litany Against Fear | Mitigation and stagger resistance for you and allies. |
| Technique | Manipulate Instability, Rapid Register, Prana-Bindu Stability, Metabolize Poison, Trauma Recovery | Bonus damage on staggered or stunned targets; faster Voice; less poise damage; poison reduction; better healing. |
| Passive | Blade Damage, Short Blade Damage, Voice Training, Vitality, Self-Healing, Poison Tolerance, Sun Tolerance, Recovery | |
| Prescient | Bindu Dodge, Screech | After a parry, attacks pass through you; Voice staggers around the target. |

## Notes

- Counter: Iron Will (Mentat) resists lesser Voice users at a stamina cost, after which the target is briefly vulnerable to Voice (game text).
- Weapons: the passives favor short blades (kindjal, dirk); the guide adds a ranged weapon for bosses (banrado.de). Armor: Robe of the Sisterhood school set (game text).
- Doctor Lemenger, the Testing Station 24 boss, fights with a short blade and a Bene Gesserit kit (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_24).
- Common pairing: Swordmaster melee with Bene Gesserit defensive picks; see [combining skill trees](combining-skill-trees.frontmatter.md).
