# Melee attacks, poise and stagger

Quick answer: light attacks are fast, and three in a row stagger the target; heavy attacks are slow, break blocks, stagger on hit and pierce shields. A staggered enemy is open to extra damage, especially a slow blade (game text, tutorials).

## Attacks

- Light attack ("Slash"): quick; "Multiple light attacks in succession will stagger the target" (game text). The new-player tutorial asks for a 3-hit combo (game text).
- Heavy attack: hold the attack button. Heavy attacks are slow, break block and cause stagger if they land; they are the slow blade that gets through shields (game text). In 1.5 they deal the same damage as shield-penetrating attacks (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Sprint attacks and lunges exist; the Swordmaster technique Reckless Lunge and passive Confidence reward them with damage mitigation (game text).
- Each weapon card lists Damage Per Hit, Attack Speed, Attack Stamina Cost and Block Stamina Cost (game text, item stats). Weight-changing augments change stamina costs (game text); see [augments](augments.frontmatter.md).
- 1.5 tuned melee soft-lock: it now engages only while attacking or being hit by an NPC, with new settings "Melee Proximity Auto Lock" and "Melee Target Mouse Aim Selection" (patch notes 1.5).

## Poise and stagger

- Hits deal Poise Damage alongside health damage; when poise runs out you are Staggered (game text, stat "Poise Damage"; skill Solid Stance "You can take more poise damage before being staggered").
- To leave a stagger, dash in any direction. Staggers caused by abilities cost more stamina to dash out of than a normal dash (game text, tutorials "Being Staggered" and "Being Staggered from Abilities").
- Ways to resist: Solid Stance and Litany Against Fear (more stagger resistance), Prana-Bindu Stability and Thrive on Danger (less poise damage) (game text). Stats on gear read "Light / Medium / Heavy Stagger Reduction" (game text).
- Ways to exploit: Manipulate Instability (Bene Gesserit) adds damage to stunned, staggered or knocked-back enemies (game text). The Sabotage specialization raises "Damage to Staggered Targets" by 0.5% per level, +50% at level 100 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Specialization, edited 2026-10-03).

## Enemies and stagger (1.5)

Copper to aluminum enemies can dash out of a stagger once; duraluminum and plastanium enemies can dash out of basic staggers twice and out of ability staggers once (patch notes 1.5). NPC combos got clearer tells before attacks (Funcom, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/).
