# School armor sets

Quick answer: each school has a unique armor set, earned by finishing that school's trainer contract chain and then craftable at the Advanced Garment Fabricator (Funcom, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/; community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-archetype-armor-sets-and-how-to-unlock-them, September 2025). The sets cut that school's ability cooldowns; there are no cross-piece "set bonus" strings in the game text.

| School | Chest piece (game text) | Type | Container item (game text) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swordmaster | Tunic of Ginaz | light per the container and guide; chest id says heavy | Swordmaster Light Armor Set |
| Bene Gesserit | Robe of the Sisterhood ("awarded to those who undertake advanced Bene Gesserit training") | light | Bene Gesserit Armor Set |
| Mentat | Tactical Coat of the Mentat | light | Mentat Light Armor Set |
| Planetologist | Planetologist light armor coat / stillsuits | light (container unpacks "Unique stillsuits") | Planetologist Light Armor Set |
| Trooper | Trooper Heavy Armor Tactical Coat | heavy | Trooper Armor Set |

## Stats (community, September 2025, pre-1.5; verify in game)

All five are duraluminum (Mk5). Light sets: armor 786, -7% cooldowns for their school, -30% dash stamina cost, +40% blade mitigation, 3.0 heat protection. Trooper heavy set: armor 1,417, -7% cooldowns, +25% dash cost, -40% blade mitigation, +20% dart and concussive mitigation (method.gg above). Abilities the guide says benefit: Swordmaster (Knee Charge, Crippling Strike, Eye of the Storm), Bene Gesserit (Compel, Bindu Sprint, Weirding Step), Mentat (Gravity Mine, The Sentinel), Trooper (Shigawire Claw, Explosive Grenade, Gravity Field), Planetologist (Suspensor Pad).

## Unlock

Finish the trainer chain: basic trainer, then advanced trainer, ending with "The Last Stand of Seron Varlin", "The Rogue Bene Gesserit", "The Cleansing of Kara Valk", "Untwisted Questions" or "The Final Piece" (contract names game text; trainers in [../progression-and-skills.md](../progression-and-skills.md#trainers)). Trainer cosmetic sets (Bene Gesserit Vestments, Mentat Habiliment, Planetologist Garment, Trooper Chestpiece) also exist (game text).
