# Unique weapons and where they come from

Quick answer: a unique weapon is crafted from a schematic you find; each craft uses one Imperial Permit, found schematics grant permits, and permits can also be bought with Intel (game text). Plastanium (tier 6) uniques come from Testing Stations in Grades 1 to 5 (game text; Funcom, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/). Green map markers (shipwrecks) hold weapon and armor parts (game text).

## Testing Station drops (wiki pages edited October 2026, current for 1.5)

| Station (boss) | Weapons in the drop list |
|---|---|
| No. 136, fire (Masano the Burned) | The Ancient Way, Perforator, Halleck's Pick |
| No. 89, radiation (Lieutenant Aman) | Penetrator, Regis Burst Drillshot, Ambition, Way of the Misr, Shishakli's Bite |
| No. 195, poison (Burrbridge) | Halleck's Pick, Dunewatcher, The Ancient Way (11.8% each); Seethe, Sardaukar Intimidator, A Dart for Every Man, Regis Disruptor Pistol (6.0% each); Black Market K-28 Lasgun (1.4%) |
| No. 152, electric (Planetologist Takulki) | Experimental Lightning Gun, Regis Tripleshot Repeating Rifle |
| No. 24, darkness (Doctor Lemenger) | Feyd's Drinker, Cauterizer |

Sources: https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_136, _89, _195, _152, _24 (wiki). Boss names are game text. Higher difficulty raises grade odds; Grade 3 stops after difficulty 20, Grade 4 thins out from 27, Grade 5 starts at 20 (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).

## Contract and story rewards

- Jolt-sword: "The Last Stand of Seron Varlin" (Swordmaster finale); Mendek's Rattle: "The Cleansing of Kara Valk" (Trooper finale); Thufir's Best: "Making a Martyr" and "Untwisted Questions" (Mentat); Adept Disruptor Pistol: "The Rogue Bene Gesserit" (community, https://selphie1999gaming.com/game-guides/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-unique-weapons-how-to-obtain-all-unique-weapon-schematics/, June 2025, pre-1.5; contract names match game text).
- Chapter 4 uniques (Eel's Tooth, Serpent's Fang, Leech's Maw, Prescient Edge, Branding Blade, Hell-Fury Pistol, The Angry Adder, Spitting Cobra, The Hateful Barker, Blind Fury) have journey requirements (game text item keys `B1C4_*`; patch notes 1.5.15.0).
- Regis Dual Blades: Chapter 3 journey "A Deadly Poison" (wiki, https://www.awakening.wiki/Regis_Dual_Blades).
- Arhun K-28 Lasgun: "Cutting Edge" contract chain; see [lasguns](lasguns.frontmatter.md).
- Vendors: Legion Tattoo and Seb's Kisser schematics via Smiley Tyg at the Anvil (game text, lore); Fivefinger's Tripleshot and Shaitan's Tongue reported from Arrakeen and Harkonnen vendors (community, pre-1.5).

## World locations (pre-1.5 list)

The same June 2025 list places 86 unique weapons at shipwrecks, Imperial Testing Stations (ITS) in Hagga Basin and the Deep Desert (selphie1999gaming above). Update 1.5 moved plastanium weapon and armor blueprints into the new Testing Stations (Funcom, announcement above), so its "Deep Desert" entries for tier 6 items are likely stale. Use the per-family entries ([short blades](short-blades.frontmatter.md), [swords](swords.frontmatter.md), [rifles](rifles.frontmatter.md), [pistols](pistols.frontmatter.md), [disruptors](disruptors.frontmatter.md)) for each unique's effect.

## Picks named by 1.5-era sources

Seethe, Dunewatcher and the Regis Disruptor Pistol for long-range boss work (wiki, Testing Station 89); Thufir's Best and the Regis Tripleshot for a Mentat (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds); Jolt-sword for a Swordmaster (community, https://www.bisecthosting.com/blog/dune-awakening-storm-swordmaster-build-best-weapons-armor-skills-talents, date not checked).
