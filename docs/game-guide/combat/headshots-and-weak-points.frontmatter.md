# Headshots, weak points and backstabs

Quick answer: head hits with ranged weapons deal extra damage, and much of the game's "critical" damage means headshots or revealed weak points. The Mentat tree, the Sabotage specialization and several augments build on it (game text).

## Mechanics (game text unless noted)

- Headshots: the passive Headshot Damage and the technique Marksman raise headshot damage. A 1.5-era guide puts Marksman at +20% (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds, 2026-10-11).
- Body shots: Center of Mass (Trooper technique) raises damage to the body and to shields but lowers headshot damage. Bodyshot Damage is a separate passive name in the text.
- Weak points: Exploit Weakness (Mentat technique) reveals a weak point when you damage an unshielded enemy with a ranged weapon; shooting it raises the damage of later attacks (not ranged area attacks). Only one weak point shows at a time. A guide gives +30% (community, banrado.de above).
- Weak-Point Focus (prescient capstone, tree unconfirmed): after looking at a target a moment you see a weak point; while prescient your allies can see and use it too.
- Shield Overcharge (Mentat, prescient): ranged hits on a shield reveal a vulnerability; hitting it drains the target's power pack faster.
- Back shots: Assassin's Shot (Mentat) adds damage when shooting enemies in the back.
- Staggered targets: Manipulate Instability (Bene Gesserit) and the Sabotage track's "Damage to Staggered Targets" (up to +50% at level 100; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Specialization).

## Gear

- Uniques: Cope and Seethe (deadshot pistols) have higher accuracy and "bonus damage for headshots" with small clips.
- Augments: Tactical Enhancer (headshot damage, any ranged weapon); weapon-specific ones such as Karpov 38 Precision Kit, JABAL Spitdart Tactical Enhancer, Maula Pistol Tactical Enhancer, GRDA 44 Tactical Amplifier, Drillshot FK7 Tactical Adjuster raise headshot damage with trade-offs. Maula Pistol Sprayer lowers headshot damage for fire rate. Some augments mention "critical damage" (Disruptor M11 Personnel-Buster, Maula Pistol Antipersonnel Rounds), which appears to mean weak-spot hits (Karpov 38 Precision Kit text: "critical damage to weak spots").
- Sabotage track: "Head Hunter" trait, +5% headshot damage at levels 1, 10, 27, 50, 74 and 100 per the wiki table (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Specialization, edited 2026-10-03).

Whether NPC armor or helmets reduce headshot bonuses is unverified.
