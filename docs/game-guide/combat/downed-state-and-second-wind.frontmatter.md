# Downed state (DBNO) and Second Wind

Quick answer: at zero health you are usually downed rather than killed. Allies can revive you (it costs water, "Insufficient Water to Revive"), you can try to self-revive ("Try To Keep Going"), or you can "Surrender to the Desert" (game text). Death drops are covered in [../survival-combat-crafting.md](../survival-combat-crafting.md).

## Details

- 1.5 gives extended invulnerability frames when entering and recovering from the downed state (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Second Wind: if you take 40 points of health damage during Second Wind it ends and returns all your stamina; Dual Blade flurries no longer end a target's Second Wind early (patch notes 1.5). What triggers Second Wind is not described in game text (unverified).
- 1.5.15.0 (test client) clears negative status effects when you go down (patch notes 1.5.15.0, https://duneawakening.com/news/public-test-client-patch-1-5-15-0/); unverified on this server's build.

## Skills tied to going down (game text; tree placement unverified for some)

| Skill | Effect |
|---|---|
| Recovery Assistance | Bonus stamina when you recover from DBNO. |
| Proximity Discharge | Recovering from DBNO releases an electric pulse that incapacitates foes and drains their power. |
| Possfeign | Voluntarily enter DBNO for momentary invulnerability, then self-revive very quickly. |
| Poison Tooth (Mentat) | When you are killed in combat, a poison cloud damages everyone nearby. |
| Emergency Decoys / Tactical Repositioning | Dash out of the Incapacitated state to spawn a decoy, or swap places with a nearby decoy and recover. |
| For Master! | Your Anigolah minions enrage when you go down. |
