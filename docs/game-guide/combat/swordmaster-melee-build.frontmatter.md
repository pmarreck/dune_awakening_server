# Swordmaster melee build

Quick answer: the Swordmaster is the close-quarters melee school: Knee Charge to close the gap, Crippling Strike and Deflection for control and defense, Dance of Blades to chain kills, and passives for blade damage and poise. A 1.5-era guide rates Swordmaster melee as the PvP top tier (community, https://banrado.de/en/dune-awakening-best-builds, 2026-10-11). Skill names and effects below are game text; tree placement is from the wiki (https://awakening.wiki/Swordmaster).

## Kit

| Kind | Picks | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Ability | Knee Charge (starting ability) | Charge and severely stagger; costs stamina. |
| Ability | Crippling Strike | Slows and raises dash cost of targets. 1.5: 3 levels, cooldown 20 to 15 s, 15 stamina, can now be parried or blocked (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/). |
| Ability | Deflection | Stance that deflects darts while stamina lasts. 1.5: cooldown 8 to 4 s, 2.5 stamina/s, per-deflection cost 3 falling to 2 at level 3 (patch notes 1.5). |
| Ability | Eye of the Storm, Foil, Retaliate, Inspiration | Spinning area attack; parry-counters (numbing or bleeding); group melee damage buff (+25% at level 1 for 14 s per community guides cited by banrado.de). |
| Technique | Dance of Blades | A melee kill empowers your next melee attack; called non-negotiable for PvE (community, https://pecsandbox.com/dune-awakening-swordmaster-best-pve-build-guide/, pre-1.5). |
| Technique | Disciplined Breathing, Thrive on Danger, Reckless Lunge | Stamina recovery; low-health sustain; mitigation after sprint attacks. |
| Passive | Long Blade Damage, Blade Damage, Solid Stance, Confidence, Bleed Tolerance, General/Desert Conditioning, Optimized Hydration | Damage, poise, stamina. |
| Prescient | Precise Parry, Prescient Strike | Parries shred mitigation; next strike after an ability hits twice (second at 70%). |

## Gear

- Weapons: sword for groups, rapier for duels, dual blades for fast pressure on shields (game text; wiki). Jolt-sword is a common late pick (community, https://www.bisecthosting.com/blog/dune-awakening-storm-swordmaster-build-best-weapons-armor-skills-talents). Carry a disruptor or scattergun for ranged openers (community, banrado.de).
- Armor: the Swordmaster school set (Tunic of Ginaz), light armor for cheaper dashes, or the Combat Exoskeleton chest for +10% melee damage (game text). Accelerator Power Pack keeps the shield up in PvP (community, banrado.de).

## Play pattern

Open with Knee Charge (stagger), light combo, then a heavy slow-blade into the staggered, shielded target; parry their swings (0.33 s window, +10 stamina since 1.5); dash out of staggers; keep stamina for the dash. See [melee attacks](melee-attacks-and-stagger.frontmatter.md).

## Common pairings (community)

- With Bene Gesserit for survivability (Litany Against Fear, Prana-Bindu Stability) and Voice control (community, pecsandbox.com above; https://vortexgaming.io/en/postdetail/664930).
- With a Trooper mobility tool: Shigawire Claw for gap closing and staggers (community, banrado.de; game8, https://game8.co/games/Dune-Awakening/archives/532357).
- Hybrid opener for PvE and PvP: Shigawire Claw, Knee Charge, disruptor or scattergun to soften, melee to finish (community, banrado.de).
