# Dashing and dodging

Quick answer: the dodge in Dune: Awakening is the Dash, a short burst in the direction you hold. It costs stamina, it is how you escape a stagger ("Direction+Dash"), and since 1.5 it passes through other characters, so you can escape when cornered (game text; patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).

## Details

- Dash: "Quickly move a short distance" (game text). Dashing, blocking and other stamina actions scale with the server's stamina setting (game text, server setting "PlayerStaminaDrain"); this server runs it at 0.8 (verified on this server, see [../survival-combat-crafting.md](../survival-combat-crafting.md)).
- Out of a stagger: dash in any direction. Ability staggers cost more stamina to dash out of (game text).
- Dash cost depends on armor. Item cards show "Dash Stamina Cost" (game text). Light armor lowers it, heavy armor raises it: a community comparison of the duraluminum school sets lists -30% for the light sets and +25% for the Trooper heavy set (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-archetype-armor-sets-and-how-to-unlock-them, September 2025, pre-1.5).
- Uniques that lower dash cost: the Crew Chief's dashed stillsuit tops ("reduces the stamina cost of dashing") and Compression-Stim / Stim-Leggings (game text).
- Crippling Strike (Swordmaster) slows the victim and makes their dashes cost more stamina; it can be parried or blocked since 1.5 (game text; patch notes 1.5).
- Suspensor Dash (Trooper technique) lets you dash in mid-air while suspended (game text).
- Emergency Decoys and Tactical Repositioning (Mentat) trigger when you dash out of the Incapacitated state (game text).
- Enemies dash too: higher-tier NPCs dash more and can dash out of staggers (patch notes 1.5).
