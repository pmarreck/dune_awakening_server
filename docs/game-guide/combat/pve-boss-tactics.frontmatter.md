# PvE boss tactics (Testing Stations and story bosses)

Quick answer: the endgame bosses live in six repeatable Overland Map dungeons, five Imperial Testing Stations and The Old Quarry, run solo or in a group at a Challenge Level you pick (game text; Funcom, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/). Each has an environmental theme; bring the matching protection, kill adds fast and learn the phase changes. Boss names are game text; tactics come from the community wiki pages edited in October 2026.

## Difficulty and scaling

- The dialog shows enemy health, damage, damage to shields, shield power and regeneration, environmental modifiers, recommended tier plus grade, and loot grades; enemy count scales with party size; higher levels unlock after clearing the previous one, and some cap by faction rank (game text).
- 1.5: difficulty 30 now gives +1,750% enemy health and damage (previously about +1,700% at 20), then +50% per level; boss bonus health is lower in 3 and 4 player groups and 4-player fights have fewer adds; bosses are immune before the fight starts and show health bars (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- A pre-1.5 guide says rewards stop improving at level 40 of 100 (community, https://method.gg/dune-awakening/augmentations-in-dune-awakening-how-to-unlock-how-they-work-more); unverified for 1.5.

## The bosses

| Station (theme) | Boss | Key mechanics and tactics |
|---|---|---|
| No. 136 (fire) | Masano the Burned | Fireball salvos; airborne phase with about 20 Pyrocket balls and shielded melee adds; homing missile in phase 3; in phase 4 the floor ignites, stay off the ground. Shoot the explosive canisters early and the bombers on the ceiling to knock him out of the air; Attractor Field helps (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_136). |
| No. 89 (radiation) | Lieutenant Aman | Spams Collapse Grenades; adds every 25 to 30 s; more radioactive pits each phase. Burst with a scattergun early, dash out of collapse fields, kill adds at range, hug the edge late. Bring a Radiation Suit Mk6 and Iodine Pills, and long-range weapons such as Seethe, Dunewatcher or the Regis Disruptor Pistol (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_89). |
| No. 195 (poison) | Burrbridge | Swordmaster-style (Knee Charge, Eye of the Storm, both dodgeable); poison spores pop near you through shields and stagger; Poison Mines; phase 4 Poison Nova on the outer ring, so fight in the center. Clear spores first, kill the shielded add fast; ranged is easier than melee; Healkits remove poison (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_195). |
| No. 152 (electric) | Planetologist Takulki | Disruptor pistol (3 charged shots then reload), Suspensor Blast, Stunner, no adds; sweeping electric walls, then electrified floor sections that never repeat the last two. Use his reload to heal and hit; melee staggers him but invites Suspensor Blast; Compel, Ignore, Attractor Field and Deflection recommended; Forge set for low levels (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_152). |
| No. 24 (darkness) | Doctor Lemenger | Short blade and Bene Gesserit kit: shield-piercing bolt, teleport behind you, an area debuff that drains energy when you dash; vanishes twice. Fight just outside his reach, hit the green light switches to reveal and stun him, use fast melee such as dual blades on summoned suboids (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Testing_Station_24). |
| The Old Quarry | Dr. Jalanta (community name; her journal is game text) | Three health brackets with a brief invulnerability between; she summons suboids and eats them to heal. Kill suboids before they glow orange and are consumed (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/The_Old_Quarry). 1.5 lengthened her anti-air and suspended-travel cooldowns (patch notes 1.5). |

## Story and contract bosses

Named bosses in game text include The Glutton, The Paranoid, Commander Aramsham, Zantara and Tyros Reffa (Forty Fears), the Art of Kanly arena fighters (Brute the Scrapper, Aren the Exile, The Master of Chains, Thrax the Flamer, Sinnu the Poisoner, Face Dancer Assassin), The Minotaur, Lazaro Fein, Colonel Keil and the Hand of the Prophet (game text). 1.5 made Echoes of the Past, The Glutton, The Paranoid and The Jackal instanced (Funcom announcement above). Per-boss tactics for these are not yet researched here.

## General advice (sourced above)

- Bring Healkits for cleansing, the right environmental protection (radiation suit, fire or energy augments), and a long-range option.
- Kill adds before they snowball; many bosses heal from or shield behind them.
- Ranged weapons with shield damage (disruptors) or poison help against shielded adds; see [shields](shields-and-the-slow-blade.frontmatter.md).
