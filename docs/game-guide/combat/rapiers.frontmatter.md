# Rapiers

Quick answer: the rapier is a long blade built around parrying. Its opening attack parries: press attack to parry and step forward, aim to parry and step back. It has reach and raw damage, penetrates shields, and suits one-on-one duels more than groups (game text, Atreides Rapier; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Rapier). Funcom reworked it into "a parrying weapon" in 1.5 (Funcom, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/).

## Lines (names game text, ids item data)

House Rapier (`Rapier`, aluminum), Adept Rapier (`Rapier_0`, duraluminum), Regis Rapier (`Rapier_3`, plastanium). Tiers per the wiki, which gives damage from 28.5 (House) to 115.4 (Regis) (wiki, edited 2026-03-20, before the 1.5 rework; treat numbers as stale). Rapiers start at the aluminum tier; the O'odham late-game journey introduces them (game text).

## Behavior (wiki, pre-1.5)

- Blocking is replaced by a parry that also pulls you backward.
- Primary attack opens with a parry, then a flurry of stabs; forward stabs lunge for extra reach.
- Almost no area attacks; the parry window differs from other melee weapons.
- 1.5's 0.25 to 0.33 s parry-window change named swords, daggers and dual blades, not rapiers (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
