# What 1.5 changed for the endgame

1.5 launched on PC on 2026-09-17 (see the version history in [../economy-social-versions.md](../economy-social-versions.md)). Sources: patch notes 1.5.3.0 and hotfixes (https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/) and the 1.5 announcement (https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/), unless noted.

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Dungeon scaling | Old difficulty-20 scaling (+1,700% enemy health and damage) moved to difficulty 30 (+1,750%), +50% per level beyond |
| Loot grades | Grade 3 no longer drops above difficulty 20; Grade 5 from difficulty 20 at a lower rate; Grade 4 less often from 27 |
| Bosses | Less bonus health for 3 and 4 player groups; immune before the fight starts; fewer adds for groups of 4 in some fights; health bars on story bosses |
| New dungeons | Six new testing stations with unique bosses, five repeatable from the Overland map; Echoes of the Past, The Glutton, The Paranoid and The Jackal became instanced |
| Augments | Stat rolls on a bell curve; Heavy Metal Blade Coating now Grade 3+; Exchange schematic grade selector |
| Melange | Removed from Advanced Garment, Survival, Vehicle and Weapons Fabricators, Medium Chemical Refinery and Augmentation Station build costs |
| Material costs | Aluminum, Duraluminum, Plastanium and early graded Plastanium components 10 to 25% cheaper |
| Landsraad | Single-player simulates rival guilds; fix for players stuck at rank 4; "Convergence" and "Sample Recovery" missions disabled; fix for missions granting items twice |
| Deep Desert | Single-player Deep Desert never resets and the storm no longer instantly kills; fix for the 50% building discount not applying (1.5.3.1) |
| Journeys | "Harvest Spice" step fixed; Sandcrawler step completes only when fully built |
| Protections | Base Reconstruction Tool (3 snapshots), base recycling, 30-day vehicle recovery, no base taxes, death durability penalty 5% to 2% |
| Story | Book One concluded (The Great Convention Part III); retroactive rewards for old missions |
| New content | Smuggler's Run, Blushing Caverns and other locations; Pyrocket and Dual Blades; reworked Rapier; instanced PvP chests |
| DLC | Filmic Archive (Sardaukar sets), released 2026-09-22 |
| Self-hosted | Settings through `.ini` files; 1.5.3.1 added `UserServerCustomSettings.ini`; 1.5.3.4 noted custom settings need `DifficultyLevel=Custom` |

Later: 1.5.3.5 (2026-09-30) fixed fabricator and refinery water issues and added mirrored Sardaukar pieces; 1.5.3.6 (2026-10-06) made BattlEye optional on private servers (see the version history).
