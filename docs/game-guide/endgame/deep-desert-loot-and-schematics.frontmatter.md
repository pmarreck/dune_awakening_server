# Where Mk6 and Unique schematics drop

Unique and Mk6 schematics mostly come from Deep Desert points of interest (testing stations, wrecks and downed ships), whose loot tables rotate weekly with the reset (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/deep-desert-companion, page labeled "13th October to 20th October", checked 2026-10-13). Graded Unique Mk6 weapons and armor and augment schematics come from the Overland challenge testing stations (game text; see [challenge-testing-stations](challenge-testing-stations.frontmatter.md)).

## Sources

| Source | What it gives | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Desert testing stations | Many Mk6 uniques (the method.gg tracker lists stations such as The Forgotten Cells, The Furnace, The Museum, The Control Room, with vehicle parts like Bigger Buggy Boot Mk6). Testing stations in general are "the primary source" of Advanced Servoks and Particle Capacitors and often hold damaged utility tools and vehicle modules. | community, method.gg; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Imperial_Testing_Stations (2026-09-26) |
| Deep Desert wrecks and downed ships | Mk6 weapons and modules; downed ships "often at higher rates than wrecks" | community, method.gg |
| Row A sites | Mk5 loot tables (the Shield Wall row) | community, method.gg |
| Overland challenge testing stations | Grade 1 to 5 Plastanium Unique weapons and armor and augment schematics, better with difficulty | game text; patch notes 1.5.3.0 |
| Landsraad crafting vendor | Paid in House Scrip; "specialized components required to craft high quality items and augmentations"; limited stock, weekly restock | game text |
| Landsraad "Import Rights" decrees | Open a Tool, Armor, Weapon or Vehicle vendor for the term and make one extra Mk6 blueprint available per week | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Landsraad (2026-10-08) |
| Specialized components by map marker | Green markers (shipwrecks): weapon and armor parts; blue (testing stations): vehicle and tech parts; orange (moisture-sealed caves): hydration and gathering gear | game text |
| Exchange | Weapon schematics now have a grade selector | patch notes 1.5.3.0 |

## Notes

- PvP Deep Desert tables include PvP-only items (for example the Black Market K-28 Lasgun and the Bulwark armor set) and different drop rates; method.gg lists Young Sparky Mk6 at under 1% in PvE and 7% in PvP (community, method.gg). On a world without PvP these items may be unobtainable from Deep Desert loot (inference).
- Specific sector lists in older guides (for example "Sandbike Engine Mk6 in a buried testing station at F-8") are pre-1.5 and change weekly (community, https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=264356); do not rely on them.
- Recycling an unwanted Augmentation Schematic gives Augmentation Schematic Patterns (game text).
- Crafting specialization trait "Ghost Data" gives a chance to duplicate a schematic, +15% at level 100 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Specializations, 2026-10-03).
