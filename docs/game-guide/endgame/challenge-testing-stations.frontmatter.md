# Challenge testing stations

The Overland map's instanced Imperial Testing Stations are the repeatable endgame dungeons. You pick a Challenge Level at the entrance ("Attune to Palm Lock"), and higher levels raise enemy health, damage and shields, hazard damage and the loot grade (game text). Clearing them is how you get Grade 1 to 5 Unique Mk6 gear and augment schematics (game text; community, boostroom 2026-09-29).

## Stations

| Station | Theme | Boss (community) | Community difficulty rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imperial Testing Station No. 136 | Fire: "136 was sealed for a reason" | Masano the Burned | Easy to medium, good solo |
| Imperial Testing Station No. 152 | Electricity: "the air is charged" | not recorded here | Easy to medium, no adds |
| Imperial Testing Station No. 195 | Poison: "The garden keeps what it's given" | Burrbridge | Hard |
| Imperial Testing Station No. 89 | Radiation | Lieutenant Aman | Very hard, growing waves of adds |
| Imperial Testing Station No. 24 | Darkness: "the heart of 24 is pure dark" | not recorded here | not recorded |
| The Old Quarry | Water Shipper quarry near the Polar Sink | Doctor Jalanta | not recorded |

Station names and flavor lines are game text (`UI/DungeonScalingDialog_*`). Bosses and ratings: community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/testing-stations and the per-station method.gg guides (for example https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-testing-station-136-fire-walkthrough-and-boss-guide), written before 1.5. Method.gg calls the first five "Challenge Mode" stations from Chapter 3, repeatable "up to 100" levels (community, pre-1.5). The 1.5 announcement speaks of six new stations, five repeatable from the Overland map (1.5 announcement); which six is not spelled out.

## Difficulty and its gate

- You must clear a level before the next unlocks ("Complete previous difficulty"), and your faction rank caps the level ("Current Max Rank", "Increase your Faction rank") (game text). See [landsraad](landsraad.frontmatter.md) for ranks.
- Scaling since 1.5.3.0: the old difficulty-20 scaling (enemy health and damage +1,700%) now sits at difficulty 30 (+1,750%), and each level above 30 adds 50% (patch notes 1.5.3.0, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Enemy count adjusts to party size; bonus boss health for 3 and 4 player groups was reduced in 1.5 (game text; patch notes 1.5.3.0). The dialog can show "Party recommended" and a recommended tier plus augment grade (game text).
- Each run records a personal best level and time (game text).

## Loot by difficulty (patch notes 1.5.3.0)

- Grade 3 items and augmentations stop dropping above difficulty 20.
- Grade 5 items and augmentations begin at difficulty 20, at a lower rate than before.
- Grade 4 drops become less frequent from difficulty 27.
- The scaling dialog lists "Reward Drop Rates" for Ungraded through Grade 5 weapons and armor and augmentations (game text).

Augmentation Schematics "can be acquired by completing dungeons on the Overland map, after receiving proper training from a faction" (game text). Endgame bosses are immune to damage before the fight starts (patch notes 1.5.3.0).
