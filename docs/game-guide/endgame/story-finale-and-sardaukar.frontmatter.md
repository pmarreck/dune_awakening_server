# Story finale and Sardaukar content

1.5 completes Book One: "The Great Convention Part III and the final Chapter close out Book One", and the whole story can now be played start to finish (1.5 announcement, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/). The finale takes you offworld to Zanovar and onto a Sardaukar Orbital Bastion (game text). Spoilers follow.

## Chapter 4 journeys (game text)

| Journey (likely order; not confirmed) | What happens |
|---|---|
| Dangerous Allies | Wait for Elara Tuek's call at a watersealed base, talk to Esmar Tuek in Fallen Light, then fight through a Smuggler base in the Deep Desert overrun by mercenaries and Sardaukar to get a shigawire reel from Staban Tuek |
| Prelude to a Journey | The reel says "The truth lies buried on Zanovar"; gather a bribe of Spice Melange and meet the pilot at the Arrakeen Spaceport |
| The Tragedy of Zanovar | Fly offworld through a Sardaukar blockade to Zanovar, search the ruins, and get captured by Sardaukar under Count Fenring |
| Forty Fears | Wake on a Sardaukar Orbital Bastion, discover ghola sarcophagi, recover your identity in a spice dream, free the Sleeper, escape |
| A Fall from Heaven | Your escape pod crashes on Arrakis; the Fremen Stilgar cuts you out and tells you not to seek the Fremen further |
| The Great Convention — Part III | The Book One conclusion |

The spice bribe is a contract: "we're going to need an emperor's ransom in spice if you want to get past the planetary blockade" (game text); the amount is a `{number}` placeholder in the text (unverified). The maps are `CB_Story_DestroyedZanovar` ("Arrakeen Spaceport") and `CB_Story_OrbitalMonitor` ("Sardaukar Orbital Bastion") (game text; see [../world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md)). Patch 1.5.15.0 (test client) made the Chapter 3 and 4 journeys award XP (patch notes, cited in the progression chapter).

Existing story missions gained retroactive rewards in 1.5, such as swatches, weapon and armor variants and decorations (community, https://boostroom.com/blog/dune-awakening-update-15-guide-new-endgame-progression-major-changes, 2026-09-29).

## After the story

The story has no post-credits loop of its own; the repeatable endgame is the Landsraad, Specializations, challenge testing stations and the Deep Desert (community, boostroom; see [index](index.frontmatter.md)). The free Polar Cap update was announced for later in 2026 (1.5 announcement).

## Sardaukar (Filmic Archive DLC)

The Filmic Archive DLC (9.99, released 2026-09-22) adds three Sardaukar armor sets, Sardaukar weapons and emotes, and a 73-piece Sardaukar building set with 17 decorations (1.5 announcement; see [../economy-social-versions.md](../economy-social-versions.md) and [../bases-and-building.md](../bases-and-building.md)). It is cosmetic and building content, not a progression gate (inferred from the announcement's contents). Sardaukar also appear as enemies in story content (game text).
