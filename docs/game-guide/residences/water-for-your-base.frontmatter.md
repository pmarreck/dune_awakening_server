# Water for your base

Quick answer: three ways, and good sites give you at least two. Windtraps make water over time but need Filters and power; deathstills and blood purifiers turn corpses and blood into water, so build near enemy camps; dew reapers harvest primrose fields at dawn into literjons you carry home (game text). Store it in cisterns linked to the base's water circuit.

## Siting for water

- Near enemy camps: steady corpses for deathstills and blood for purifiers (community, pre-1.5, method.gg "when to move" guide; itemlevel).
- Near primrose fields: 2025 guides name the Western Vermillius Gap and the edge of Jabal Eifrit Al-gharb as rich in primrose, and the Eastern Shield Wall plateaus as poor (community, pre-1.5, keengamer and itemlevel URLs in [base-sites-by-region](base-sites-by-region.frontmatter.md)). Do not build on top of a field; it blocks harvesting (community, pre-1.5).
- Large groups should settle near a big dew field because they burn much more water (community, pre-1.5, method.gg).

## Storage

Water Cistern, Medium and Large, plus Small and Medium Insulated Water Cisterns; plain cisterns are "Fragile" and the circuit shows an "Evaporation Rate" (game text). The Large Water Cistern holds 100,000 (wiki). 1.5.15 fixed offline water generation being too high or too low (patch notes 1.5.15.0).
