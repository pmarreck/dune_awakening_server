# Base layout tips

Quick answer: put a garage at the entrance with storage right beside it, keep crafting stations together in one wing with two-story rooms for the big refineries, run one central staircase, and leave open ground for windtraps and turbines. Stay inside your claim box, which has a ceiling. Mechanics and numbers are in [../bases-and-building.md](../bases-and-building.md).

## Vehicles

- Garage Door pieces exist for most sets, Large Garage Doors for CHOAM Facility, Atreides and Harkonnen (game text; patch notes 1.3.0.0). The vehicle console shows "Unsheltered" / "At Risk" when parking is not good enough (game text).
- Park inside your own claim: vehicles outside it decay "even if they are sheltered" (game text). Co-owned vehicles no longer decay inside a friendly claim (patch notes 1.3.0.0).
- Backups fail if vehicles are inside the structure (game text), so a garage you can drive out of quickly saves trouble when moving the base.
- Ornithopters: players roof over a landing pad so the thopter counts as sheltered (community; mechanics unverified). A Landing Pad Building Set exists (game text: Floor, Floor Corner, Wall Support, Triangle Wall Support); how it is obtained is unverified.

## Storage and crafting

- Put storage containers next to vehicle entrances for quick unloading (community, pre-1.5, https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-build-the-perfect-base-dune-awakening/).
- Storage containers link to the base by storage circuits, so stations draw from them (game text); a container's inventory source can be set to pull from vehicles in the Sub-Fief (community, same guide).
- Group fabricators and refineries in one wing. The Medium Spice Refinery and Large Ore Refinery need a room two tiles high (community, same guide).
- One central staircase connecting all floors; avoid maze layouts (community, same guide).
- The Medium Storage Container holds 4,000 volume since 1.5 (patch notes 1.5).

## Power and water

- Wind turbines and windtraps want open air: the directional turbine needs an unsheltered spot (wiki), and windtraps too (bases chapter). Since 1.5.15 a placeable's info card states its shelter requirement (patch notes 1.5.15.0). Roof terraces work well for this (inference).
- Generators: Fuel-Powered (75), Omnidirectional turbine (150), Directional turbine (350), Spice-Powered (1000) (wiki figures, via the bases chapter). Bases have up to five circuits (game text).
- Pentashields cost 3 power per tile in one 2025 guide (community, pre-1.5, keengamer).

## Claim box

- The plain Sub-Fief Console reaches only about 13.8 m above the console; the Advanced console reaches 28.16 m at level 1 and 130.56 m at level 6 (verified on this server, docs/admin.md). Plan towers and landing pads within that.
- Placeables can no longer be put above the height limit since 1.5.15 (patch notes 1.5.15.0).
- Claim extra land early so neighbors cannot box you in (community, pre-1.5, keengamer).
