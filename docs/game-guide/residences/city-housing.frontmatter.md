# Housing in Arrakeen and Harko Village

Quick answer: there is no purchasable or rentable housing in either city. Your home is a base you build on land you claim with a Sub-Fief Console, in Hagga Basin or (temporarily) the Deep Desert (game text; see [../bases-and-building.md](../bases-and-building.md)).

Evidence:

- The build 25610213 string tables have no housing, rent, apartment-purchase or lease UI strings. "Residency" and "apartment" appear only in story content: the Arrakeen Residency is Duke Leto's seat, where quests send you ("Report at once to the Arrakeen Residence"), and Phaedra Vye's apartment in the upper Central Plaza is a Chapter 2 crime scene (game text).
- The wiki's Arrakeen and Harko Village pages list vendors, trainers, bankers and story NPCs but no housing (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Arrakeen, 2026-10-01; https://awakening.wiki/Harko_Village, 2026-09-29).
- Cities are "KANLY - GUILD PEACE [NO COMBAT]" zones (game text) and claims cannot be placed in blocked regions such as NPC sites and tradeposts (official blog 2025, via the bases chapter).

What players do instead:

- Keep a base close to a tradepost with a thopter pilot, so the city is one taxi ride away (500 Solari to a social hub since 1.5; patch notes 1.5.1.0 PTC). See [base-sites-by-region](base-sites-by-region.frontmatter.md).
- Bank Solari in the city: Solari in your backpack drop on death (game text), banked Solari do not.
- Use furniture and decoration sets to make a base feel like a residence; see [building-aesthetics](building-aesthetics.frontmatter.md).
