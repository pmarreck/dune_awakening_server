# Notable base ideas

Quick answer: the bases players talk about most are cliff or plateau eyries reachable only by ornithopter, rock-island hideouts in the Vermillius Gap, and a crafting bunker by the Rift's erythrite. Ideas below are labeled as community advice or as inference from terrain and rules; none is an official "best".

| Idea | Where | Why it works | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plateau eyrie | Eastern Shield Wall cliffs (for example near Testing Station No. 17), Jabal Eifrit Al-janub plateaus near No. 76 | Aluminum, carbon and granite around (basalt too on the Shield Wall); flying required; water scarce. Out of reach of ground traffic (inference) | community, pre-1.5, https://itemlevel.net/dune-awakening-top-5-best-base-locations-for-all-materials/ |
| Built into the cliff | Any rock face | Natural support and a less exposed silhouette; looks native to Arrakis | community, pre-1.5, https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/how-to-build-the-perfect-base-dune-awakening/ |
| Rock-island hideout | Vermillius Gap rock formations near The Anvil, or near the hammer rock | Iron country; the island cuts worm exposure to short hops; busy with other players | community, pre-1.5, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/where-to-build-your-base-dune-awakening-and-when-to-move; itemlevel (Hagga Basin) |
| Tradepost townhouse | On rock beside Griffin's Reach or The Anvil | No city housing exists, so this is the nearest thing to a home in town: taxi, contracts, trader and recruiters next door | inference; community advice for both sites as above |
| Rift crafting bunker | Hagga Rift trench or a bunker plateau | Only erythrite source, camps for blood; few other resources, so a satellite, not a main base | community, pre-1.5, keengamer and itemlevel |
| Faction compound | Near Helius Gate (Atreides) or Riftwatch (Harkonnen) | Quartermaster on site for your faction's set and furniture; wiki calls both prime real estate | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Helius_Gate, https://awakening.wiki/Riftwatch |
| Three-base chain | Starter (Hagga Basin South), mid (Vermillius Gap), forever home (Shield Wall) | Follows the resource tiers; free relocation in Hagga Basin South since 1.5 makes the starter disposable | community, pre-1.5, method.gg; patch notes 1.5.1.0 |
| Deep Desert staging rim | Northern edge of the Shield Wall or the western rim of the O'odham | Shortest flight to the Overland Map and Deep Desert, so less fuel | community, pre-1.5, https://itemlevel.net/best-base-locations-in-hagga-basin-which-to-choose-in-dune-awakening/ |
| Copy and recycle | Anywhere | Copy a favorite base with a Solido Replicator before moving; recycle an old one for 100% of materials via the Base Reconstruction Tool | game text; patch notes 1.3.15.0 (bases chapter) |

Respect other players: building on a primrose field, a tunnel or a common route blocks them (community, pre-1.5, keengamer; itemlevel).
