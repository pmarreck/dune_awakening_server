# Good base sites by region

Quick answer: build on rock, near the resources of your current tier, near a water source (primrose dew fields or enemy camps for blood), and within reach of a tradepost. Players commonly move through three bases: a starter near Griffin's Reach, a mid-game base in the Vermillius Gap or Jabal Eifrit, and a late "forever home" on the Shield Wall rim near the Deep Desert. The specific spots below come from 2025 community guides (pre-1.5) and Hagga Basin resource nodes move at each Coriolis storm (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Coriolis_Storms), so scout before committing.

You can hold three claims, at most two of them the small console (official blog 2025; wiki); this server's admin can change the cap (game text setting "Maximum Sub-fief Amount"). Moving is cheap in Hagga Basin South since 1.5: a Base Reconstruction placement there has no cooldown (patch notes 1.5.1.0); elsewhere a placed backup triggers a 7-day cooldown (game text). See [../bases-and-building.md](../bases-and-building.md).

## Rules that limit where you can build

- Not on open sand or quicksand; not over NPC sites, testing stations or tradeposts; not in blocked regions (game text; official blog 2025).
- The wiki says Advanced Sub-Fief Consoles cannot be placed in Hagga Basin South (wiki, Base_Reconstruction_Tool); not confirmed in game text.
- Claims are boxes: place the console high on a slope or you lose headroom (verified on this server; see the bases chapter).
- Don't build over primrose fields or tunnels other players use (community, pre-1.5, keengamer "perfect base" and itemlevel).
