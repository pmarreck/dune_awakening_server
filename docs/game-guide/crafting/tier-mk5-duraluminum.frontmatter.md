# Tier Mk5: duraluminum

Quick answer: duraluminum unlocks at 700 Intel spent; schematics cost about 30 Intel and vehicle assemblies 45 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). Duraluminum Ingot = 1 Aluminum Ingot + 4 Jasmium Crystal (Cutteray Mk4) + 500 mL in a Medium Ore Refinery. Mk5 is the top tier of the ordinary specialized fabricators.

- Gear names: "Adept" weapons (including Adept Dual Blades via the journey A Deadly Poison, and the Adept Missile Launcher Kit), Mercenary Scout and Heavy armor, Mercenary stillsuit, Radiation Suit Mk5, Holtzman Shield Mk5, Cutteray Mk5, Power Pack Mk5, Welding Torch Mk5, Iodine Pill, Large Windtrap, Spice Refinery, Sandbike, Buggy, Scout and Assault Ornithopter Mk5 (wiki research list). Game text lists Flamethrower and Lasgun in the duraluminum Combat subcategory.
- Typical costs (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Adept Disruptor M11 25 Duraluminum + 14 Mechanical Parts + 33 Silicone + 23 Gun Parts + 5 Light Caliber Compressor + 340 mL ([worked-example-adept-disruptor-mk5](worked-example-adept-disruptor-mk5.frontmatter.md)); Mercenary Heavy Chestplate 50 Duraluminum + 14 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 26 Silicone + 23 Armor Plating + 450 mL; full Buggy Mk5 358 Duraluminum, 88 Advanced Servoks, 149 Cobalt, 68 Armor Plating, 7 Hydraulic Piston, 7 Military Power Regulator and more.
- New this tier: Great House base parts (Hydraulic Piston for engines, Military Power Regulator for PSUs), Thermoelectric Cooler and Irradiated Slag for tools.
- 1.5 cut some duraluminum-tier component costs by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5); the numbers above predate that.
