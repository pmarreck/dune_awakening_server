# Unique items, schematics and Imperial Permits

Quick answer: unique (named) items are not unlocked with ordinary research. Each craft uses an Imperial Permit. Using a found schematic adds permits for that item, and permits can also be bought with Intel in the Research menu: "use {numintel} Intel to acquire {numcharges} Imperial Permit(s) for {itemname}" (game text; survival and progression chapters). Uniques add a spice-infused dust of their tier to the recipe (game text; wiki recipe data).

- The Research menu has a Uniques category and a "Maximum Permits Exceeded" limit; permits added show as "{n}x {item} Imperial Permits added to {fabricator}" (game text).
- Researching a schematic requires being watersealed at a base, city or tradepost (game text, survival chapter).
- Skill stats "Schematic Doubling Chance" and "Schematic Recovery Chance" exist (game text); values unverified.
- Tier markers: unique recipes use the same station as their tier (for example Sim's Cutter and Olef's Quickcutter at the Fabricator, The Forge armor at the Advanced Garment Fabricator) (wiki recipe data).
- Plastanium uniques come in Grades 1 to 5; only they take augments, at the [Augmentation Station](augmentation-station.frontmatter.md) (game text). Unwanted augment schematics recycle into Schematic Patterns (Grades 1 to 5), and a Crafting keystone lets the Advanced Survival Fabricator combine low-grade patterns into higher ones (game text).
- Typical unique costs (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Sinner's Bloodbag 16 Aluminum + 18 Micro-sandwich Fabric + 7 Insulated Fabric + 7 Plant Fiber + 12 Spice-infused Aluminum Dust + 255 mL; Halleck's Pick and Static Needle 35 Plastanium + 17 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 6 Plasteel Composite Blade Parts + 5 Carbide blade parts + 9 Spice-infused Plastanium Dust + 53 Melange + 3 Plant Fiber + 440 mL.
