# Stillsuit costs by tier

Quick answer: a stillsuit is four pieces (mask, body or garment, gloves, boots). Every tier uses Micro-sandwich Fabric; Silicone joins at Mk3, Stillsuit Tubing at Mk4, and Mk6 swaps tubing for Improved Watertube and adds Spice Melange (wiki recipe data). Numbers are wiki recipe data from before 1.5; Mk4-Mk6 costs may be 10 to 25% lower in 1.5, and this server halves fabrication costs (`CraftingCost=0.5`, server config). See [index](index.frontmatter.md). Stillsuit tops also cover the leg slot in 1.5 (patch notes 1.5).

| Set (tier) | Full set (mask + body + gloves + boots), computed | Body alone |
|---|---|---|
| Scavenger (Mk1) | 6 Micro-sandwich Fabric, 17 Plant Fiber | 3 MSF, 8 Plant Fiber |
| Kirab (Mk2) | 41 Iron, 35 MSF, 17 Plant Fiber | 18 Iron, 15 MSF, 7 Plant Fiber |
| Slaver (Mk3) | 32 Steel, 47 MSF, 33 Silicone, 310 mL | 14 Steel, 20 MSF, 14 Silicone, 135 mL |
| Native (Mk4) | 62 Aluminum, 82 MSF, 90 Silicone, 17 Stillsuit Tubing, 765 mL | 27 Aluminum, 40 MSF, 39 Silicone, 7 Tubing, 340 mL |
| Mercenary (Mk5) | 80 Duraluminum, 92 MSF, 179 Silicone, 23 Stillsuit Tubing, 1,520 mL | 35 Duraluminum, 40 MSF, 80 Silicone, 10 Tubing, 675 mL |
| CHOAM (Mk6) | 134 Plastanium, 122 MSF, 184 Silicone, 21 Improved Watertube, 107 Melange, 2,370 mL | 60 Plastanium, 54 MSF, 81 Silicone, 9 Improved Watertube, 54 Melange, 1,050 mL |

MSF = Micro-sandwich Fabric. Stations: Mk1-Mk2 Fabricator or Garment Fabricator; Mk3-Mk5 Garment Fabricator; Mk6 Advanced Garment Fabricator. Research: Scavenger 0 Intel, Kirab 5, Slaver 10, Native 20, Mercenary 30, CHOAM 40, all needing the journey "The First Trial of Aql" (https://awakening.wiki/Research). Unique suits (Hollower, Menol's, Kel's, Shadrath's, Maraqeb, Saturnine, Batigh, Imperial, Villari's) and hoods are schematics with permits (survival chapter).

Sources: https://awakening.wiki/Garment_Fabricator, https://awakening.wiki/Advanced_Garment_Fabricator, https://awakening.wiki/Fabricator.
