# Recycler

Quick answer: costs 30 Copper Ingot, draws 15 power and "disassembles an item, returning a portion of its component materials"; the amount depends on the item's condition (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Recycler, pre-1.5 data). Research: Copper tier, 2 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research).

- 20 slots, volume 100, health 1,250 (wiki).
- Failure messages: "Item type cannot be recycled", "Insufficient space for recycler output", "Insufficient power" (game text).
- Recycling unwanted augmentation schematics yields Schematic Patterns used to craft augmentations (game text).
- Some retired items say "Please recycle this item at the Recycler to get 100% of the invested crafting materials back" (game text, for example a removed CHOAM recon scanner module).
- Skill stat "Schematic Recovery Chance" exists for recycling unique schematics (game text); values unverified.
