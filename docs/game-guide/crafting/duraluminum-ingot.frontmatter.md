# Duraluminum Ingot

Quick answer: 1 Aluminum Ingot + Jasmium Crystal + 500 mL water: 4 Jasmium in the Medium Ore Refinery (5 s), 3 in the Large (4 s) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Duraluminum_Ingot). Jasmium needs a Cutteray Mk4 (survival chapter). Item id `DuraluminumRod` (item data).

- From raw ore: 7 Aluminum Ore + 4 Jasmium + 700 mL per ingot via the Medium refinery; 4 + 3 + 700 mL via the Large (computed).
- Used in 182 recipes (wiki): Mk5 gear, the Medium Chemical Refinery (150), Spice Refinery (160), Advanced Fremen Deathstill (240), and as a filler in two Mk6 conversions (Tri-Forged Hydraulic Piston, Irradiated Core) (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5; see [index](index.frontmatter.md)).
- 1.5 cut some duraluminum-tier costs by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5).
- Base vendor price 375; not dropped on death (wiki item data).
