# Silicone Block

Quick answer: Flour Sand + 50 mL water in a chemical refinery: 5 sand in the Small (15 s), 3 in the Medium (10 s) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Silicone_Block). "Flour sand refined into plastic" (game text). Item id `Silicone` (item data).

- Flour Sand is gathered from white dust plumes on open sand, best with a Static Compactor; "beware the worm" (game text journey).
- Ingredient in 347 recipes, the most of any refined material (wiki): Mk2+ guns, Mk3+ armor and stillsuits, every refinery and Advanced fabricator (90 to 1,080 each), lubricants and Plastone (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5; see [index](index.frontmatter.md)).
- Base vendor price 45 (wiki item data).
