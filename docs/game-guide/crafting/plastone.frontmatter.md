# Plastone

Quick answer: 200 Basalt Stone + 1 Silicone Block + 50 mL water gives 200 Plastone in a Small Ore Refinery (15 s) or 250 in a Medium (10 s); the Large needs only 175 Basalt for 250 (5 s) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Plastone). "An artificial composite of Silicone and Basalt used in sandstorm-resistant construction"; Basalt is the "Base material for plastone" (game text). Basalt needs a Cutteray Mk3 (survival chapter).

- Which building pieces use Plastone in 1.5 is not on the pages read; the CHOAM Shelter and Facility pieces listed cost Granite Stone (wiki). Unverified.
- Base vendor price 10 (wiki item data).
