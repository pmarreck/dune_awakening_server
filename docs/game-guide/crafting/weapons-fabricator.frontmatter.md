# Weapons Fabricator

Quick answer: built from 40 Steel Ingot and 30 Complex Machinery, draws 40 power and makes 109 weapon and ammo recipes up to Adept (Mk5, duraluminum), including many unique weapons. Regis (Mk6) weapons need the Advanced Weapons Fabricator (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Weapons_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: "Weapons Fabricator" set, Construction, Steel tier, 5 Intel; "A set including machinery for fabricating weapons and ammunition" (game text; wiki https://awakening.wiki/Research).
- Power: base power required (game text). 10 slots, health 1,250 (wiki).
- Covers: Kindjal, Dirk, Sword, Rapier, Maula Pistol, Rafiq Snubnose, Disruptor M11, Disruptor Pistol, Karpov 38, JABAL Spitdart, GRDA 44, Drillshot FK7 and Burst Drillshot, Vulcan GAU-92, the Arhun K-28 Lasgun, Rockets, Light and Heavy Darts, and uniques such as Thufir's Best and Shadrath's Edge (wiki recipe list). Costs: [weapon-costs](weapon-costs.frontmatter.md).
- Inputs climb with tier: Mechanical Parts and Gun Parts for guns, Plasteel Microflora Fiber and Blade Parts for blades, plus ingots, silicone and from Mk4 regional parts such as Light Caliber Compressors and Carbide Scraps (wiki).
