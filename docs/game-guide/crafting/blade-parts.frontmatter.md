# Blade Parts

Quick answer: "These can often be found on the bodies of those who favor the blade, or in their storage lockers"; tiered variants are "Found on Rusher enemies and in weapons lockers" (game text). Id `T1RusherComponent` (item data).

- Uses (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Standard Sword 6, Artisan 9, House 15, Adept 19.
- Mk6: Plasteel Composite Blade Parts = 3 Blade Parts + 2 Plasteel Plate + 1 Spice Melange; Carbide and Diamodine blade parts also take 1 Blade Parts each (wiki).
- Serrated, Sharpened, Keen and Advanced Blade Parts exist in game text, but recipes use only the base item ([component-families](component-families.frontmatter.md)).
