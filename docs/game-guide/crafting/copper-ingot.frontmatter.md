# Copper Ingot

Quick answer: refine Copper Ore (`AzuriteOre`, Cutteray Mk1) in any ore refinery: 4 ore in the Small, 3 in the Medium, 2 in the Large, no water (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Copper_Ingot). Item id `CopperBar`, stack 500 (item data).

- Used in 47 recipes (wiki): Mk1 gear, the Small Chemical Refinery (45), Recycler (30), Welding Torch Mk1 (6), Cutteray Mk1 (6 + 4 EMF Generator), Power Pack Mk1 (5 + 3 Particle Capacitor), Sandbike Chassis Mk1 (12 + 5 Advanced Servoks) (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5; see [index](index.frontmatter.md)).
- Also a filler in one Mk6 conversion: Thermo-Responsive Ray Amplifier takes 2 Copper Ingot (wiki).
- Base vendor price 20 Solari (wiki item data).
