# Medium Spice Refinery

Quick answer: 750 Spice Sand + 7,000 mL water gives 10 Spice Melange + 75 Spice Residue in 450 s, a quarter less sand per melange than the small refinery. Build cost 285 Plastanium Ingot, 225 Silicone Block, 135 Spice Melange, 100 Complex Machinery and 190 Cobalt Paste; power 350 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Medium_Spice_Refinery, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: Construction, Plastanium, 20 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research).
- "Processes Spice Sand into Spice Melange more efficiently than the Small Spice Refinery" (game text). 5 slots, health 2,500 (wiki).
- Not among the six placeables whose melange cost 1.5 removed (patch notes 1.5); its 135 melange may still apply.
