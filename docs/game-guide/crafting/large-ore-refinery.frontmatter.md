# Large Ore Refinery

Quick answer: "This ultra-efficient unit will produce higher yields from smaller quantities of raw materials" (game text). It needs about a third less ore than the Medium for aluminum and plastanium, but costs 380 Plastanium Ingot, 540 Silicone Block, 400 Spice Melange, 200 Complex Machinery, 745 Cobalt Paste and 40 Advanced Machinery to build (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Large_Ore_Refinery, edited 2026-09-12, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: Construction, Plastanium, 20 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). Power draw: not listed on the wiki (unverified).
- Recipes (wiki): Copper 2 ore, 3 s; Iron 3 ore + 25 mL, 5 s; Steel 1 Iron Ingot + 2 Carbon + 50 mL, 3 s; Aluminum 4 ore + 200 mL, 20 s; Duraluminum 1 Aluminum Ingot + 3 Jasmium + 500 mL, 4 s; Plastanium 4 Titanium + 1 Stravidium Fiber + 1,250 mL, 20 s; Plastone 175 Basalt + 1 Silicone + 50 mL gives 250, 5 s.
- 1.5 did not list it among the six placeables that lost their melange cost (patch notes 1.5), so the 400 melange may still apply, possibly reduced by the plastanium-tier pass. Check the build menu.
- Is it worth it? It saves 2 Titanium per plastanium ingot and 3 Aluminum Ore per aluminum ingot (33% and 43%). The 400 melange alone is 40,000 Spice Sand in a small spice refinery, so it pays off only for heavy plastanium or aluminum production (computed).
