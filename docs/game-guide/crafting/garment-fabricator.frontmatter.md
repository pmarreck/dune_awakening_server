# Garment Fabricator

Quick answer: built from 40 Steel Ingot and 30 Complex Machinery, draws 40 power and makes 205 garment recipes (light and heavy armor, stillsuits, radiation suits, uniques) up to Mk5 duraluminum. CHOAM (Mk6) sets need the Advanced Garment Fabricator (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Garment_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: Construction, Steel tier, 5 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). In game files it is the "Wearables" fabricator; the UI calls it Garment Fabricator (game text).
- Power: base power required (game text). 10 slots, health 1,250 (wiki).
- Sets by tier: Scavenger (Mk1), Kirab (Mk2), Slaver (Mk3), Duneman (Mk4), Mercenary (Mk5) armor; Scavenger, Kirab, Slaver, Native, Mercenary stillsuits (game text research groups). Costs: [armor-costs](armor-costs.frontmatter.md), [stillsuit-costs](stillsuit-costs.frontmatter.md).
- Inputs: Plasteel Microflora Fiber and Armor Plating for armor; Micro-sandwich Fabric, Silicone and from Mk4 Stillsuit Tubing for stillsuits (wiki).
