# Spice Refinery (small)

Quick answer: costs 160 Duraluminum Ingot, 130 Silicone Block, 80 Cobalt Paste and 70 Complex Machinery, draws 200 power and turns 100 Spice Sand + 750 mL water into 1 Spice Melange + 5 Spice Residue in 180 s (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Spice_Refinery, pre-1.5 data). The placeable is named "Spice Refinery"; its patent is "Small Spice Refinery Patent" (game text).

- Research tier: the wiki lists it at Duraluminum for 30 Intel (https://awakening.wiki/Research), while the game's Steel research subcategory text lists "Advanced Fabricators, Spice Refinery, Cutteray, Pentashield, and Building Set" (game text). Unverified which is current.
- 5 slots, health 1,250 (wiki). "Must be powered through additional base power installations" (game text).
- Spice Residue feeds Spice-infused Fuel Cells and lubricant; melange is sold or used in late crafting ("Can be sold or used in further crafting", game text).
