# Augmentation Station

Quick answer: applies a fabricated augmentation to a plastanium unique item. Build cost on the wiki is 75 Plastanium Ingot, 180 Silicone Block, 50 Complex Machinery, 15 Spice Melange, 75 Cobalt Paste and 10 Advanced Machinery; 1.5 removed the melange. It draws 50 power (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Augmentation_Station, added in 1.3.0, pre-1.5 data; patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).

- Research: Construction, Plastanium, 20 Intel (wiki); journey "Research the Augmentation Station" in the Chapter 3 augmentation steps, after fabricating a Regis Component and an augmentation (game text).
- Rules (game text): only plastanium-tier unique items take augments; augments cannot be removed and reduce maximum durability; slot limits rise with Melee, Ranged and Garment "Augmentation Limit" traits in the Crafting specialization.
- Augmentations are fabricated, not made here: weapon augments in the Advanced Weapons Fabricator (game text), garment augments reportedly in the Advanced Garment Fabricator (community, method.gg, unverified). They need 5 to 12 Regis Components by grade (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/how-to-get-offworld-regis-compound-and-regis-components-in-dune-awakening, pre-1.5, unverified).
