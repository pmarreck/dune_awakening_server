# Complex Machinery

Quick answer: "Ironically, these components are found in many older fabricators and refineries, but cannot themselves be easily fabricated" (game text). Loot it from old machinery and loot containers. Id `T2MachineComponent` (item data). Ingredient in 25 recipes (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Complex_Machinery).

- Station build costs (wiki, pre-1.5): each specialized fabricator 30; Medium Ore Refinery 50; Improved Blood Purifier 20; Fremen Deathstill 32; Medium Chemical Refinery 50; Spice Refinery 70; Advanced Fremen Deathstill 70; each Advanced fabricator 100; Medium Spice Refinery 100; Large Ore Refinery 200; Large Spice Refinery 350; Augmentation Station 50.
- Vehicle engines (wiki): Sandbike Engine Mk3 10, Buggy Engine Mk4 15, Mk5 21, Mk6 30.
- Three of them plus 2 Plasteel Plate and 1 Cobalt Paste make [Advanced Machinery](advanced-machinery.frontmatter.md).
- A full late base (four specialized and four Advanced fabricators, Medium Ore and Chemical refineries) needs about 620 Complex Machinery (computed from the station costs above).
