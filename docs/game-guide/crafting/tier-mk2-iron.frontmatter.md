# Tier Mk2: iron

Quick answer: iron unlocks at 22 Intel spent; most schematics cost 5 Intel and need the journey "Moving On" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). Iron Ingots come from 5 iron ore + 25 mL in the Small Ore Refinery. Everything is still made in the general Fabricator.

- Gear names: "Standard" weapons (Standard Sword, Disruptor M11, Karpov 38, GRDA 44, Maula Pistol, Kindjal), Kirab Scout and Kirab Heavy armor, Kirab stillsuit, Cutteray Mk2, Power Pack Mk2, Holtzman Shield Mk2, Healkit Mk2, Sandbike Mk2 (wiki research list).
- Typical costs (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Standard Disruptor M11 8 Iron Ingot + 5 Mechanical Parts + 4 Silicone + 7 Gun Parts; Standard Sword 10 Iron + 4 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 3 Plant Fiber + 6 Blade Parts; Kirab Heavy Jerkin 15 Iron + 6 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 5 Plant Fiber + 5 Armor Plating; Kirab Stillsuit Body 18 Iron + 15 Micro-sandwich Fabric + 7 Plant Fiber; Sandbike Mk2 75 Iron + 28 Advanced Servoks + 7 Armor Plating + 13 Particle Capacitor.
- New this tier: enemy-dropped Gun Parts, Blade Parts and Armor Plating, and Silicone Blocks in guns.
- Stations researchable: Repair Station (40 Iron Ingot), Improved Blood Purifier, Advanced Construction Kit. Carbon and Erythrite need a Cutteray Mk2 (survival chapter), which opens steel and cobalt.
