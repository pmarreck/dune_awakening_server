# Tier Mk1: copper

Quick answer: copper unlocks after 19 Intel spent; schematics cost 0 to 3 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). Make Copper Ingots from copper ore (Cutteray Mk1) in the Small Ore Refinery, 4 ore each, and fabricate in the general Fabricator. Below copper sits the salvage tier: improvised gear from Salvaged Metal and Plant Fiber, researched for 0 to 2 Intel.

- Gear names: plain names (Kindjal, Maula Pistol, Karpov 38), Scavenger armor ("Scavenger Rags") and Scavenger stillsuit, Cutteray Mk1, Power Pack Mk1, Welding Torch Mk1, Sandbike Mk1, Literjon, Vehicle Backup Tool (game text research groups; wiki).
- Typical costs (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Kindjal 4 Copper Ingot + 3 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 3 Plant Fiber; Karpov 38 8 Copper + 2 Mechanical Parts; Scavenger Chestpiece 10 Copper + 3 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 8 Plant Fiber; Scavenger Stillsuit Body 3 Micro-sandwich Fabric + 8 Plant Fiber; whole Sandbike Mk1 43 Copper + 13 Advanced Servoks + 6 Particle Capacitor. No water in copper recipes.
- Stations: Fabricator (75 Salvaged Metal), Small Ore Refinery (90 Salvaged Metal), Small Chemical Refinery (45 Copper Ingot), Recycler (30 Copper Ingot).
- Research subcategory text: Construction "Chemical Refinery and Cutteray"; Combat "Battle Rifle and Armor"; Water "Stillsuit and Water Storage"; Exploration "Survey Probe Launcher, Ammunition, and Suspensor Belt"; Vehicles "CHOAM Sandbike and Vehicle Assembly Tool" (game text).
