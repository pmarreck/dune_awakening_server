# Large Spice Refinery

Quick answer: 10,000 Spice Sand + 75,000 mL water gives 200 Spice Melange + 1,000 Spice Residue in 2,700 s, half the sand and water per melange of the small refinery. It costs 950 Plastanium Ingot, 1,080 Silicone Block, 350 Complex Machinery, 1,000 Spice Melange, 1,110 Cobalt Paste and 55 Advanced Machinery, draws 500 power and has 20,000 health (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Large_Spice_Refinery, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: Construction, Plastanium, 40 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research).
- "Processes Spice Sand into Spice Melange more efficiently than other spice refineries" (game text).
- Not among the placeables whose melange cost 1.5 removed (patch notes 1.5).
- Guild-scale investment: its 1,000-melange build cost equals 100,000 Spice Sand through a small refinery (computed).
