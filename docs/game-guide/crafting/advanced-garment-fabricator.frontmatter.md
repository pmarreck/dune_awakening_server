# Advanced Garment Fabricator

Quick answer: makes CHOAM Scout and Heavy armor, CHOAM, Imperial and Villari's stillsuits, Pardot's Hood, Shaddam's Bladder, the Radiation Suit Mk6 and plastanium unique sets such as The Forge, Bulwark, Executor's and Pincushion, 57 recipes in the wiki's list. Armor augmentations are made here according to guides (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/augmentations-in-dune-awakening-how-to-unlock-how-they-work-more, unverified) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Advanced_Garment_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data).

- Build cost (wiki, pre-1.5): 140 Plastanium Ingot, 180 Silicone Block, 100 Complex Machinery, 150 Cobalt Paste, 115 Armor Plating, 45 Spice Melange. Before 1.5 each of the four Advanced fabricators also cost 45 Spice Melange; 1.5 removed that, and the plastanium-tier cost pass may have lowered the rest by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Power 150, 10 slots, health 2,500 (wiki). Research: Construction, Plastanium, 20 Intel (wiki).
- Examples (wiki): CHOAM Heavy Chestplate = 60 Plastanium, 18 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 27 Silicone, 12 Plasteel Composite Armor Plating, 36 Melange, 700 mL; CHOAM Stillsuit Body = 60 Plastanium, 54 Micro-sandwich Fabric, 81 Silicone, 9 Improved Watertube, 54 Melange, 1,050 mL. Radiation Suit Mk6 = 60 Plastanium, 54 Silicone, 9 Ballistic Weave Fabric, 54 Melange, 1,050 mL; 1.5 added Plasteel Microflora Fiber to it (patch notes 1.5), amount unverified.
