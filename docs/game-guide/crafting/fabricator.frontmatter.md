# Fabricator

Quick answer: the general Fabricator costs 75 Salvaged Metal, draws 10 power and makes 142 copper- and iron-tier recipes: early weapons, armor, stillsuits, tools, sandbike parts and ammo. For steel and above you need the specialized fabricators (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Fabricator, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: Construction, Salvage tier, 1 Intel, after the journey "Echoes of the Past" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). The early journey step "Constructing a Fabricator for a Stillsuit" asks you to research and build it (game text).
- Placement: near a Sub-Fief Console with the Construction Tool; "Requires power from a base power generator to operate" (game text).
- Fabricators queue several designs; finished orders go to the fabricator's own storage, and they need power (game text). Storage: 10 slots, volume 200; it can link to base storage and accept vehicle deposits (wiki infobox).
- Health 1,250 (wiki).
- What it makes: Mk1 and Mk2 (copper and iron) gear across all categories, plus Light Darts (5 Salvaged Metal for 30), Heavy Darts, the Construction Tool (20 Salvaged Metal), Welding Torch Mk1 (6 Copper Ingot), Cutteray Mk1 (6 Copper Ingot + 4 EMF Generator) and Mk2, Power Pack Mk1 and Mk2, Sandbike Mk1 and Mk2 parts, Scavenger and Kirab armor and stillsuits, and several copper and iron uniques (wiki recipe list).
- When to move on: the game's own journey says "The general fabricator has served me well, but as I progress into more exotic materials and items, it can no longer handle the load" and sends you to the specialized fabricators (game text). Everything it makes is also made by one of those (wiki recipe lists).
