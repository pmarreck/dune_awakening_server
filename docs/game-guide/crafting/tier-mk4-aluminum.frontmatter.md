# Tier Mk4: aluminum

Quick answer: aluminum unlocks at 300 Intel spent; schematics cost about 20 Intel and vehicle assemblies 30 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). Aluminum ore needs a Cutteray Mk3 and a Medium Ore Refinery (research 10 Intel; 125 Steel, 60 Cobalt, 50 Complex Machinery): 7 ore + 200 mL per ingot. Fabricate in the specialized fabricators.

- Gear names: "House" weapons (House Sword, Rapier, Disruptor M11, Karpov 38, Drillshot FK7, Vulcan GAU-92 and others), Duneman Scout and Heavy armor, Native stillsuit, Radiation Suit Mk4, Spice Mask, Cutteray Mk4, Power Pack Mk4, Healkit Mk4, Sandbike, Buggy and Scout Ornithopter Mk4, Treadwheel Mk4 (journey) (wiki research list).
- Typical costs (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): House Disruptor M11 18 Aluminum + 11 Mechanical Parts + 17 Silicone + 17 Gun Parts + 4 Light Caliber Compressor + 170 mL; Duneman Heavy Jacket 35 Aluminum + 11 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 13 Silicone + 14 Armor Plating + 225 mL; Native Stillsuit Body 27 Aluminum + 40 Micro-sandwich Fabric + 39 Silicone + 7 Stillsuit Tubing + 340 mL; full Buggy Mk4 271 Aluminum (see [worked-example-buggy-mk4](worked-example-buggy-mk4.frontmatter.md)); full Scout Ornithopter Mk4 241 Aluminum, 46 Advanced Servoks, 82 Cobalt, 22 Particle Capacitor and more ([vehicle-module-costs](vehicle-module-costs.frontmatter.md)).
- New this tier: regional components (Light Caliber Compressor, Carbide Scraps, Diamondine Dust, Sandtrout Leathers, Stillsuit Tubing, Industrial Pump, Insulated Fabric).
- 1.5 cut some aluminum-tier component costs by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/); the numbers above predate that.
