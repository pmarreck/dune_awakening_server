# Improved Blood Purifier

Quick answer: costs 50 Iron Ingot, 16 Silicone Block and 20 Complex Machinery, draws 35 power and also holds 1,000 mL; research it at the Iron tier for 5 Intel after the journey "Moving On" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Improved_Blood_Purifier and https://awakening.wiki/Research, pre-1.5 data).

- Health 2,500, double the basic purifier (wiki). How much faster it filters is not on the wiki or in game text (unverified); compare the Blood Filtration Rate on the two cards in the Research menu (game text field names).
