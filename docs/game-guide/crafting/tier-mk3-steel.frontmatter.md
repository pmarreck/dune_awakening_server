# Tier Mk3: steel

Quick answer: steel unlocks at 100 Intel spent; schematics cost about 10 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). Steel Ingot = 1 Iron Ingot + 4 Carbon Ore + 50 mL in the Small Ore Refinery. Steel is the tier where the general Fabricator stops: you need the Weapons, Garment, Survival and Vehicle Fabricators (40 Steel + 30 Complex Machinery each, 5 Intel each).

- Gear names: "Artisan" weapons (Artisan Sword, Dirk, Disruptor M11, Karpov 38, JABAL Spitdart, Rafiq Snubnose), Slaver Scout and Heavy armor, Slaver stillsuit, Cutteray Mk3, Power Pack Mk3, Welding Torch Mk3, Holtzman Shield Mk3, Sandbike Mk3, Buggy Mk3 (wiki research list).
- Typical costs (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Artisan Disruptor M11 9 Steel + 8 Mechanical Parts + 7 Silicone + 11 Gun Parts + 70 mL; Slaver Heavy Jerkin 14 Steel + 8 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 5 Silicone + 9 Armor Plating + 90 mL; Slaver Stillsuit Body 14 Steel + 20 Micro-sandwich Fabric + 14 Silicone + 135 mL; full Buggy Mk3 150 Steel + 90 Advanced Servoks + 81 Cobalt + 13 Armor Plating + 50 Particle Capacitor + 10 Complex Machinery.
- New this tier: water in recipes, Cobalt Paste in vehicles and tools, Ray Amplifiers in Cutterays.
- Also at steel: Staking Unit Set (10 Intel; 15 Steel + 15,000 Solari each), Pentashield Set, Wind Turbine Omnidirectional, Fremen Deathstill (journey), Windtrap (wiki).
