# Survival Fabricator

Quick answer: built from 40 Steel Ingot and 30 Complex Machinery, draws 40 power and makes 102 recipes: Cutterays, Power Packs, Welding Torches, blood and dew gear, literjons, compactors, suspensor belts, Holtzman shields, scanners, Stilltents, windtrap filters, welding wire, staking units and spice food, from Mk1 to Mk5 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Survival_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: Construction, Steel tier, 5 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). Journey: "Research the Survival Fabricator", then "Construct or interact with a Survival Fabricator" (game text).
- Placement and power: near a Sub-Fief Console, needs base power (game text). 10 slots, health 1,250 (wiki).
- Notable recipes (wiki): Staking Unit (15 Steel Ingot + 15,000 Solari), Cutteray Mk3 to Mk5, Power Pack Mk3 to Mk5, Welding Torch Mk3 and Mk5, Holtzman Shield Mk2, Mk3, Mk5, the Makeshift, Standard and Particulate windtrap filters (game text: filters are "crafted in a Survival Fabricator"), and welding wire in batches of 100. Costs are in [tool-costs](tool-costs.frontmatter.md).
- Mk6 versions of these tools are made in the [Advanced Survival Fabricator](advanced-survival-fabricator.frontmatter.md).
