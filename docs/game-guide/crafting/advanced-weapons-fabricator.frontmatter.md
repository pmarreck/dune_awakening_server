# Advanced Weapons Fabricator

Quick answer: makes the Regis (Mk6) version of every weapon, the Regis Missile Launcher and missiles, the Black Market K-28 Lasgun and plastanium uniques, 41 recipes in the wiki's list. Weapon augmentations are also fabricated here (journey step "Fabricate an augmentation for a ranged weapon in the Advanced Weapons Fabricator", game text) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Advanced_Weapons_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data).

- Build cost (wiki, pre-1.5): 140 Plastanium Ingot, 180 Silicone Block, 100 Complex Machinery, 150 Cobalt Paste, 40 Military Power Regulator, 45 Spice Melange. Before 1.5 each of the four Advanced fabricators also cost 45 Spice Melange; 1.5 removed that, and the plastanium-tier cost pass may have lowered the rest by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Power 150, 10 slots, health 2,500 (wiki). Research: "Advanced Weapons Fabricator" set, Construction, Plastanium, 20 Intel (game text name; wiki).
- Typical Regis recipe: Regis Disruptor M11 = 30 Plastanium Ingot, 18 Mechanical Parts, 34 Silicone Block, 12 Plasteel Composite Gun Parts, 8 Light Caliber Compressor, 25 Spice Melange, 530 mL (wiki). More in [weapon-costs](weapon-costs.frontmatter.md).
- Regis Components go into "all augmentations and high-grade weapon recipes" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Regis_Component).
