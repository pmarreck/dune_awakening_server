# Iron Ingot

Quick answer: refine Iron Ore (`MagnetiteOre`, Cutteray Mk1) with 25 mL water per ingot: 5 ore in the Small Ore Refinery (10 s), 4 in the Medium (7 s), 3 in the Large (5 s) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Iron_Ingot). Item id `IronBar` (item data).

- Every Steel Ingot consumes one Iron Ingot, so iron demand continues through Mk3 (wiki).
- Used in 69 recipes (wiki): Mk2 gear, Repair Station (40), Improved Blood Purifier (50), Cutteray Mk2 (10 + 7 EMF Generator), Power Pack Mk2 (8 + 5 Particle Capacitor), Sandbike Chassis Mk2 (20 + 8 Advanced Servoks) (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5; see [index](index.frontmatter.md)).
- Base vendor price 75 (wiki item data).
