# Medium Ore Refinery

Quick answer: the gate to Mk4 and above. It costs 125 Steel Ingot, 60 Cobalt Paste and 50 Complex Machinery, draws 45 power, and refines every ingot including aluminum, duraluminum and plastanium. Research it at the Aluminum tier for 10 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Medium_Ore_Refinery and https://awakening.wiki/Research, pre-1.5 data).

- Description: "A refinery capable of producing Aluminum and Duraluminum ingots from raw Aluminum and Jasmium ore" (game text). Titanium Ore says "Refine in a Medium Ore Refinery" (game text).
- 5 slots, volume 200, health 2,500 (wiki).
- Recipes (wiki): Copper 3 ore, 4 s; Iron 4 ore + 25 mL, 7 s; Steel 1 Iron Ingot + 3 Carbon + 50 mL, 4 s; Aluminum 7 ore + 200 mL, 30 s; Duraluminum 1 Aluminum Ingot + 4 Jasmium + 500 mL, 5 s; Plastanium 6 Titanium + 1 Stravidium Fiber + 1,250 mL, 30 s; Plastone 200 Basalt + 1 Silicone + 50 mL gives 250, 10 s.
- Its own cost is steel-tier material, so you can build it as soon as you have Complex Machinery (salvaged from old machinery; see [complex-machinery](complex-machinery.frontmatter.md)) and cobalt from a chemical refinery.
