# Small Ore Refinery

Quick answer: costs 90 Salvaged Metal, draws 15 power and "Processes raw Copper ore, Carbon ore, and Iron ore into Refined Resources. Can also create Steel ingots from Iron and Carbon" (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Small_Ore_Refinery, pre-1.5 data). It cannot make aluminum or anything above.

- Research: Construction, Salvage tier, 1 Intel, after "Echoes of the Past"; journey step "Research the Small Ore Refinery" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research; game text).
- 5 slots, volume 200, health 1,250 (wiki).
- Recipes (wiki): Copper Ingot 4 ore, 5 s; Iron Ingot 5 ore + 25 mL, 10 s; Steel Ingot 1 Iron Ingot + 4 Carbon Ore + 50 mL, 5 s; Plastone 200 Basalt + 1 Silicone + 50 mL gives 200, 15 s.
- Upgrade path: the Medium Ore Refinery saves a fifth to a quarter of the ore and adds aluminum, duraluminum and plastanium. See [refining-chains](refining-chains.frontmatter.md).
