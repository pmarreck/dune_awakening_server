# Regional loot components

Quick answer: from Mk3 onward many recipes add one named part that drops at a specific kind of site. None can be fabricated; each has a Mk6 counterpart made in the Advanced Survival Fabricator from two or three of them plus 2 Plasteel Plate.

| Component (game-text key) | Where (game text) | Used in (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5) | Mk6 counterpart |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ray Amplifier (`T3MiningGalleryComponent2`) | Crystal-mining cutteray gear, used "extensively to mine large parts of the Hagga Rift" | Cutteray Mk3-Mk5 (5, 7, 10), buggy cutterays, lasguns | Thermo-Responsive Ray Amplifier |
| Carbide Scraps (`T4MysaTarilComponent2`) | "closely guarded by the Maas Kharet in their most sacred site" (their home is the old power station of Mysa Tarill) | Mk4+ vehicle chassis (7, then 10, 15), some blades | Carbide blade parts |
| Diamondine Dust (`T4MysaTarilComponent1`) | same site as Carbide Scraps | Mk4+ hulls and rears (7), daggers, rapiers | Diamodine blade parts |
| Light Caliber Compressor (`T4HarkSpiceSiloComponent3`) | Sandflies outposts' stashes | Mk4+ light guns (House 4, Adept 5, Regis 8) | Fluted Light Caliber Compressor |
| Heavy Caliber Compressor (`T4HarkSpiceSiloComponent2`) | Sandflies outposts' stashes | Mk5 heavy guns, scatterguns, Vulcan | Fluted Heavy Caliber Compressor |
| Industrial Pump (`T4HarkSpiceSiloComponent1`) | Sandflies operations in Sentinel City | ornithopter wings, Mk4+ blood and dew tools; 50 for the Advanced Vehicle Fabricator | Fluid Efficient Industrial Pump |
| Sandtrout Leathers (`T4MaasKharetComponent1`) | Maas Kharet clothing | Mk4+ light armor and uniques, Radiation Suit Mk5 | Ballistic Weave Fabric |
| Stillsuit Tubing (`T4MaasKharetComponent2`) | Maas Kharet imitation Fremen tubing | Mk4-Mk5 stillsuits (7, 10) | Improved Watertube |
| Insulated Fabric (`T4PyonVillageComponent`) | O'odham pyon villages' old cottage industry | Decaliterjon, Large Blood Sack, Hajra Literjon Mk4-Mk5, stilltents | Atmospheric Filtered Fabric |
| Range Finder (`T3MarksmanComponent`) | "look for sharpshooters" | Mk5-Mk6 rocket launchers | Precision Range Finder |
| Thermoelectric Cooler (`T5DeepDesertShieldWallComponent`) | "usually found in the Deep Desert on the Shield Wall" | Cutteray Mk5-Mk6, compactors, buggy cutterays; 30 for the Advanced Survival Fabricator | none (used directly) |
| Irradiated Slag (`T5RadiatedCoreComponent`) | "Found in Radiated Core in the Sheol" | Power Pack Mk5 (12), Spice-infused Fuel Cell | Irradiated Core |

Other loot-only parts in game text: Calibrated Servok (Jabal Eifrit heavy equipment), Dart-grooved Barrel ("High-up bases in Shield Wall"), High-tensile Springs (Sheol), Station 15 Component, Off-world Medical Supplies (Healkit Mk4 and Mk6). No current wiki recipe uses the first four (wiki recipe data). The `HarkSpiceSilo` and `MysaTaril` parts of the keys hint at the source sites; the descriptions above are the authority.
