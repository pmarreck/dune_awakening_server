# Advanced Fremen Deathstill

Quick answer: costs 240 Duraluminum Ingot, 170 Silicone Block and 70 Complex Machinery, draws 350 power, and is researched at Duraluminum for 30 Intel with the journey "The Footsteps of the Fremen" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Advanced_Fremen_Deathstill and https://awakening.wiki/Research, pre-1.5 data).

- Health 2,500 (wiki). Its yield and speed advantage over the basic deathstill is not given on the wiki or in game text (unverified).
