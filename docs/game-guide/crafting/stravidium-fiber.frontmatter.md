# Stravidium Fiber

Quick answer: 3 Stravidium Mass + 100 mL water in a Medium Chemical Refinery, 10 s; each Plastanium Ingot consumes one (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Stravidium_Fiber). Ids `T6RefinedResourceB` (fiber) and `T6ResourceB` (mass) (item data). Stravidium Mass says "Refine in a Chemical Refinery" (game text); the wiki lists only the Medium one.

- Used in 2 recipes: the Medium and Large plastanium ingot recipes (wiki).
- Base vendor price 550 (wiki item data).
