# Plastanium Ingot

Quick answer: Titanium Ore + 1 Stravidium Fiber + 1,250 mL water: 6 Titanium in the Medium Ore Refinery (30 s) or 4 in the Large (20 s). The fiber comes from 3 Stravidium Mass + 100 mL in a Medium Chemical Refinery (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Plastanium_Ingot). Item ids: ingot `T6RefinedResourceA`, Titanium Ore `T6ResourceA`, Stravidium Mass `T6ResourceB`, fiber `T6RefinedResourceB` (item data). Its description: "Titanium ore refined with stravidium fiber" (game text).

- Prerequisites: Cutteray Mk5 for titanium and stravidium (survival chapter), and the Medium Chemical Refinery, a plastanium-tier research (20 Intel at 1,200 spent).
- From raw: 6 Titanium + 3 Stravidium Mass + 1,350 mL per ingot (Medium), 4 + 3 + 1,350 mL (Large) (computed).
- Used in 185 recipes (wiki): every Mk6 item (typically 30 to 100 each) and the Advanced fabricators (140 each), Large Ore Refinery (380), Medium Spice Refinery (285), Large Spice Refinery (950), Augmentation Station (75) (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5; see [index](index.frontmatter.md)).
- 1.5 cut some plastanium-tier costs by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5).
- Base vendor price 550 (wiki item data).
