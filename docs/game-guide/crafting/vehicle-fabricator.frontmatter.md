# Vehicle Fabricator

Quick answer: built from 40 Steel Ingot and 30 Complex Machinery, draws 40 power and makes 135 vehicle-module recipes from Mk1 to Mk5: sandbike, buggy, treadwheel, scout and assault ornithopter parts. Mk6 modules (and the Carrier Ornithopter and Sandcrawler, which exist only at Mk6) need the Advanced Vehicle Fabricator (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Vehicle_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: Construction, Steel tier, 5 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). Game text names it both "Vehicles Fabricator" and "Vehicle Fabricator".
- Power: base power required (game text). 10 slots, health 1,250 (wiki).
- Correction to the game's wording: the Advanced Vehicle Fabricator description says it makes "advanced vehicles like the Buggy or Ornithopter" (game text), but the wiki's recipe data put Buggy Mk3-Mk5 and Scout and Assault Ornithopter Mk4-Mk5 parts here, in the plain Vehicle Fabricator. Only Mk6 parts are in the advanced one (wiki).
- Main inputs: Advanced Servoks (chassis, hulls, treads), Particle Capacitors (engines, PSUs, boosters), Cobalt Paste, Complex Machinery (engines), Holtzman Actuators (boosters), Armor Plating (hulls), and from Mk4 Carbide Scraps and Diamondine Dust (wiki). Costs: [vehicle-module-costs](vehicle-module-costs.frontmatter.md).
