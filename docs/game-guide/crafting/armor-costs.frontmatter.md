# Armor costs by tier

Quick answer: armor takes ingots + Plasteel Microflora Fiber + Silicone (from Mk3); heavy armor adds Armor Plating, light (scout) armor adds Sandtrout Leathers from Mk4; Mk6 CHOAM armor swaps those for Plasteel Composite Armor Plating or Ballistic Weave Fabric and adds Spice Melange (wiki recipe data). Numbers are wiki recipe data from before 1.5; Mk4-Mk6 costs may be 10 to 25% lower in 1.5, and this server halves fabrication costs (`CraftingCost=0.5`, server config). See [index](index.frontmatter.md). The chest is the most expensive of five pieces (head, chest, legs, hands, feet).

## Heavy armor chest

| Set (tier) | Piece | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| Kirab (Mk2) | Kirab Heavy Jerkin | 15 Iron, 6 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 5 Plant Fiber, 5 Armor Plating |
| Slaver (Mk3) | Slaver Heavy Jerkin | 14 Steel, 8 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 5 Silicone, 9 Armor Plating, 90 mL |
| Duneman (Mk4) | Duneman Heavy Jacket | 35 Aluminum, 11 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 13 Silicone, 14 Armor Plating, 225 mL |
| Mercenary (Mk5) | Mercenary Heavy Chestplate | 50 Duraluminum, 14 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 26 Silicone, 23 Armor Plating, 450 mL |
| CHOAM (Mk6) | CHOAM Heavy Chestplate | 60 Plastanium, 18 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 27 Silicone, 12 Plasteel Composite Armor Plating, 36 Melange, 700 mL |

## Light (scout) armor chest

| Set (tier) | Piece | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| Scavenger (Mk1) | Scavenger Chestpiece | 10 Copper, 3 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 8 Plant Fiber |
| Kirab (Mk2) | Kirab Scout Jacket | 12 Iron, 7 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 5 Plant Fiber |
| Slaver (Mk3) | Slaver Scout Jacket | 14 Steel, 10 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 6 Silicone, 90 mL |
| Duneman (Mk4) | Duneman Scout Jacket | 25 Aluminum, 14 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 17 Silicone, 5 Sandtrout Leathers, 225 mL |
| Mercenary (Mk5) | Mercenary Scout Top | 32 Duraluminum, 18 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 35 Silicone, 7 Sandtrout Leathers, 450 mL |
| CHOAM (Mk6) | CHOAM Scout Chestplate | 40 Plastanium, 24 Plasteel Microflora Fiber, 36 Silicone, 6 Ballistic Weave Fabric, 36 Melange, 700 mL |

Stations: Mk1-Mk2 at the Fabricator or Garment Fabricator, Mk3-Mk5 at the Garment Fabricator, Mk6 at the Advanced Garment Fabricator. Research: Kirab sets 5 Intel each, Slaver 10, Duneman 20, Mercenary 30, CHOAM 40 (https://awakening.wiki/Research). Trainer sets (Bene Gesserit, Mentat, Planetologist, Swordmaster, Trooper) are unlocked through trainers (game text).

Sources: https://awakening.wiki/Garment_Fabricator, https://awakening.wiki/Advanced_Garment_Fabricator, https://awakening.wiki/Fabricator.
