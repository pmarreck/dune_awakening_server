# Repair Station

Quick answer: costs 40 Iron Ingot, draws 20 power and repairs personal gear using materials and the item's schematic; each repair permanently lowers the item's maximum durability (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Repair_Station, pre-1.5 data; game text). Research it in the Construction tab, Iron tier, 3 Intel, after "Moving On" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research; game text "Research the Repair Station in the Research menu's Construction tab").

- Vehicles are not repaired here; some items say "This item must be repaired with a welding torch" (game text).
- 5 slots, health 1,250 (wiki).
- The "CHOAM Refining Contract" Landsraad decree cuts repair costs (game text). Details on durability: survival chapter.
