# Plasteel Plate

Quick answer: "A crafting component for crafting plasteel crafting components. Can be found in the Deep Desert, or obtained from Landsraad rewards" (game text). Every Mk6 component conversion in the Advanced Survival Fabricator uses 2 of them (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Advanced_Survival_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data). Id `T6PlasteelComponent` (item data).

- It is the bottleneck of the plastanium tier for players who do not want to buy or loot finished Mk6 parts: for example a Buggy Engine Mk6 needs 9 Tri-Forged Hydraulic Pistons, which is 18 Plasteel Plate (computed from wiki data).
- Full conversion list: [plastanium-tier-components](plastanium-tier-components.frontmatter.md).
