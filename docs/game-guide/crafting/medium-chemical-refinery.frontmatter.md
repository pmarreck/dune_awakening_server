# Medium Chemical Refinery

Quick answer: required for plastanium, because only it makes Stravidium Fiber. Build cost on the wiki is 150 Duraluminum Ingot, 90 Silicone Block, 50 Complex Machinery and 35 Spice Melange; 1.5 removed the melange (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Medium_Chemical_Refinery, pre-1.5 data; patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/). It draws 350 power.

- Research: Construction, Plastanium tier, 20 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). This means you need 1,200 Intel spent before you can make your first plastanium ingot.
- "Can be used to refine resources with better efficiency" (game text). 5 slots, health 2,500 (wiki).
- Recipes (wiki): Stravidium Fiber 3 Stravidium Mass + 100 mL, 10 s; Silicone Block 3 Flour Sand + 50 mL, 10 s; Cobalt Paste 2 Erythrite + 75 mL, 10 s; Small / Medium / Large Vehicle Fuel Cell 20 / 40 (+15 mL) / 80 (+30 mL) Fuel Cells; Spice-infused Fuel Cell x10 30 Fuel Cell + 65 Spice Residue + 3 Irradiated Slag + 200 mL, 30 s (the fuel for the Spice-Powered Generator); lubricants.
- Spice-infused fuel is "made from spice residue in a Medium Chemical Refinery" (game text).
