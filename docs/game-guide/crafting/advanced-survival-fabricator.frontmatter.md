# Advanced Survival Fabricator

Quick answer: the hub of the plastanium tier. Besides Mk6 Cutterays, Power Packs, dew and blood gear, literjons, compactors and the Adaptive Holtzman Shield, it turns ordinary looted parts plus Plasteel Plate into the Mk6 components every other Advanced fabricator needs, and refines Offworld Regis Compound into Regis Components (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Advanced_Survival_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data; game text).

- Build cost (wiki, pre-1.5): 140 Plastanium Ingot, 180 Silicone Block, 100 Complex Machinery, 150 Cobalt Paste, 30 Thermoelectric Cooler, 45 Spice Melange. Before 1.5 each of the four Advanced fabricators also cost 45 Spice Melange; 1.5 removed that, and the plastanium-tier cost pass may have lowered the rest by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Power 150, 10 slots, health 2,500 (wiki). Research: Construction, Plastanium, 20 Intel (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research).
- Description: "capable of producing specialized products for survival, exploration, and mining" (game text).
- Component conversions, each 3 seconds and 50 mL water (wiki):

- The same Mk6 components also drop in the Deep Desert and come as Landsraad rewards (game text, for example Advanced Machinery and Ballistic Weave Fabric).
- A Crafting keystone "unlocks a recipe in the Advanced Survival Fabricator which combines lower quality Schematic Patterns to create higher quality patterns" (game text).
