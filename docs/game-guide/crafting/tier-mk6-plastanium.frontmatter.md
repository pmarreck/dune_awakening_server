# Tier Mk6: plastanium

Quick answer: plastanium unlocks at 1,200 Intel spent; schematics cost about 40 Intel, vehicle assemblies 60 and the plastanium stations 20 (40 for the Large Spice Refinery) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research). To make anything you need, in order: a Cutteray Mk5 for titanium and stravidium, a Medium Chemical Refinery for Stravidium Fiber, plastanium ingots from a Medium Ore Refinery, then the Advanced fabricator for the item category (140 Plastanium each, plus other materials). Most Mk6 items also need Spice Melange and Plasteel-based components.

- Gear names: "Regis" weapons and Regis Missile Launcher, CHOAM Scout and Heavy armor, CHOAM stillsuit, Radiation Suit Mk6, Healkit Mk6, Cutteray, Power Pack, Dew Reaper, Blood Extractor, Literjon Mk6, Massive Blood Sack, Omni Static Compactor, every vehicle at Mk6 including the Carrier Ornithopter and Sandcrawler (wiki research list).
- Typical costs (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Regis Sword 30 Plastanium + 15 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 3 Plant Fiber + 10 Plasteel Composite Blade Parts + 7 Carbide Scraps + 20 Melange + 440 mL; CHOAM Heavy Chestplate 60 Plastanium + 18 Plasteel Microflora Fiber + 27 Silicone + 12 Plasteel Composite Armor Plating + 36 Melange + 700 mL; full Buggy Mk6 435 Plastanium, 329 Melange, 126 Advanced Servoks, 273 Cobalt and more ([vehicle-module-costs](vehicle-module-costs.frontmatter.md)).
- Component pattern: Mk6 parts are Deep Desert loot, Landsraad rewards, or 2-3 lower parts + 2 Plasteel Plate in the Advanced Survival Fabricator ([plastanium-tier-components](plastanium-tier-components.frontmatter.md)).
- Unique plastanium items come in Grades 1 to 5 and are the only items that take augments (game text); see [uniques-and-imperial-permits](uniques-and-imperial-permits.frontmatter.md).
- 1.5 removed melange from the Advanced fabricators' build cost and cut some plastanium-tier and grade 1 to 3 component costs by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/); the numbers above predate that.
