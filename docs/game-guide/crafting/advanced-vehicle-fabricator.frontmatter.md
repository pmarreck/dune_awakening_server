# Advanced Vehicle Fabricator

Quick answer: makes all Mk6 vehicle modules: sandbike, buggy, scout, assault and carrier ornithopter, sandcrawler and the Regis spice container, 64 recipes in the wiki's list. Mk1-Mk5 modules come from the plain [Vehicle Fabricator](vehicle-fabricator.frontmatter.md) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Advanced_Vehicle_Fabricator, pre-1.5 data).

- Build cost (wiki, pre-1.5): 140 Plastanium Ingot, 180 Silicone Block, 100 Complex Machinery, 150 Cobalt Paste, 50 Industrial Pump, 45 Spice Melange. Before 1.5 each of the four Advanced fabricators also cost 45 Spice Melange; 1.5 removed that, and the plastanium-tier cost pass may have lowered the rest by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/).
- Power 150, 10 slots, health 2,500 (wiki). Research: Construction, Plastanium, 20 Intel (wiki).
- Mk6 modules need Spice Melange (about 54 to 55 per chassis or engine) and plastanium-tier parts such as Tri-Forged Hydraulic Piston (engines) and Advanced Machinery (unique engines, sandcrawler vacuum) (wiki). Plastanium vehicle modules need power regulators and hydraulic pistons "found in the Deep Desert, obtained from Landsraad rewards, or crafted in an Advanced Survival Fabricator" (game text, vehicles chapter).
- Examples (wiki): Buggy Chassis Mk6 = 85 Plastanium, 30 Advanced Servoks, 45 Cobalt, 55 Melange, 15 Carbide Scraps, 1,050 mL; Buggy Engine Mk6 = 60 Plastanium, 45 Cobalt, 18 Particle Capacitor, 30 Complex Machinery, 54 Melange, 9 Tri-Forged Hydraulic Piston, 1,050 mL. More in [vehicle-module-costs](vehicle-module-costs.frontmatter.md).
