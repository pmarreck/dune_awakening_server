# Research menu and Intel costs

Quick answer: open the Research menu and spend Intel to learn a schematic (a single design or a kit of several). Each material tier opens only after you have spent a total amount of Intel: 19 for copper, 22 iron, 100 steel, 300 aluminum, 700 duraluminum and 1,200 plastanium. Typical schematics cost about 3 Intel at copper, 5 at iron, 10 at steel, 20 at aluminum, 30 at duraluminum and 40 at plastanium; vehicle assemblies cost more (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research, edited 2026-10-06, after 1.5).

## How the menu works (game text)

- "The Research Menu contains designs for equipment and shows the location of necessary components. Use Intel points to acquire these designs." Researching unlocks recipes for fabrication.
- Categories: Essentials, Water Discipline, Combat, Construction, Exploration, Vehicles, plus Uniques, Suggested and augmentation subcategories (Melee, Ranged, Garment and Generic augmentations).
- Locked entries show "Requires Intel Spent: {current}/{required}", "Requires Level: {n}", or "Journey: {name}". A group shows "{learned} / {total}" items.
- Researching a kit ("Research Schematic") teaches every item in it ("You will gain knowledge of all the items above").
- Cards show the item's Components and Primary Source (where to find the looted parts), and for stations Power Cost or Power Generated.
- Unique items need Imperial Permits instead; see [uniques-and-imperial-permits](uniques-and-imperial-permits.frontmatter.md).

## How much Intel is enough

Adding up every schematic on the wiki's list gives 4,966 Intel (by tier: salvage 13, copper 33, iron 130, steel 325, aluminum 810, duraluminum 1,415, plastanium 2,240; computed 2026-10-13 from https://awakening.wiki/Research). Leveling to 125 gives 2,779 Intel (progression chapter, verified on this server), so a completionist needs about 2,200 more from outpost and Deep Desert Intel pickups, or must skip schematics. Treat the total as approximate: the wiki list may be incomplete. The Intel cap equals the total needed for all schematics (wiki; see progression-and-skills.md).

Journeys used as prerequisites include Getting Equipped, The Attitude of the Knife, Shelter and Shields, Water From Blood, Echoes of the Past, A Touch of Civilization, The Wreck of the Alcyon, Across the Gap (game text journey cards), plus Moving On, the Trials of Aql, The Footsteps of the Fremen, A Deadly Poison and Secrets of the Past (wiki).
