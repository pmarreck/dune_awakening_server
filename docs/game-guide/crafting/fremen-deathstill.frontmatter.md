# Fremen Deathstill

Quick answer: costs 60 Steel Ingot, 28 Silicone Block and 32 Complex Machinery, draws 200 power and stores 500 mL; it is free to research (0 Intel) but needs the journey "The Footsteps of the Fremen" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Fremen_Deathstill and https://awakening.wiki/Research, pre-1.5 data).

- Use: carry a Corpse ("Dead body that can be used in a Deathstill") to it and "Deposit Corpse"; the screen shows extraction time and total yield, and fails with "Insufficient water storage space" or "No Power" (game text).
- The late journey "Life from Death" has you research, build and use one after studying the ancient deathstill in the Sietch (game text).
