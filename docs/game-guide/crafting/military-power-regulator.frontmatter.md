# Military Power Regulator

Quick answer: "A component used to craft vehicle power units. The Great Houses have a stranglehold on this type of component on Arrakis and any who possess them must have forcibly wrested them from their control" (game text): take it from Great House sites. Id `T5FactionBaseComponent1` (item data). Ingredient in 5 recipes (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Military_Power_Regulator).

- Uses (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Sandbike PSU Mk5, Buggy PSU Mk5, Scout and Assault Ornithopter Generator Mk5, and 3 per Overclocked Power Regulator (Mk6). The Advanced Weapons Fabricator costs 40 to build (wiki).
- Base vendor price 350 (wiki item data).
