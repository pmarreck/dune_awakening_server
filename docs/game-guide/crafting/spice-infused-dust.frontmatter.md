# Spice-infused metal dusts

Quick answer: Spice-infused Copper, Iron, Steel, Aluminum, Duraluminum and Plastanium Dust are "A fabrication component used in unique items"; such metals are "locked away in their most secure containers" by the most powerful groups (game text). Ids `T1UniqueComponent` to `T6UniqueComponent` (item data).

- Typical amounts (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): 7 to 14 for Mk2-Mk5 uniques (Sinner's Bloodbag 12 aluminum dust, Maas Kharet Bloodbag 14 duraluminum dust), 9 to 65 for plastanium uniques (Static Needle 9, Bluddshot Buggy Engine Mk6 31, Sentinel Belt 65).
- Uniques also need Imperial Permits; see [uniques-and-imperial-permits](uniques-and-imperial-permits.frontmatter.md).
