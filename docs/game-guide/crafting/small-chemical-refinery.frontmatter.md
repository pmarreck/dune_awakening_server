# Small Chemical Refinery

Quick answer: costs 45 Copper Ingot, draws 15 power and refines "raw resources into Cobalt Ingots, Silicone Blocks, and Fuel Cell Packs" (game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Small_Chemical_Refinery, pre-1.5 data).

- Research: Construction, Salvage tier, 1 Intel, after the journey "Across the Gap" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Research); journey step "Research the Small Chemical Refinery" (game text). The copper research subcategory lists "Chemical Refinery" (game text).
- 5 slots, health 1,250 (wiki).
- Recipes (wiki): Silicone Block 5 Flour Sand + 50 mL, 15 s; Cobalt Paste 3 Erythrite + 75 mL, 15 s; Small Vehicle Fuel Cell 25 Fuel Cell, 15 s; Medium Sized Vehicle Fuel Cell 45 Fuel Cell + 15 mL, 20 s; Low-grade Lubricant x5 2 Fuel Cell + 1 Silicone + 4 mL, 20 s; Industrial-grade Lubricant x10 8 Fuel Cell + 4 Silicone + 5 Spice Residue + 15 mL, 40 s.
- It cannot make Stravidium Fiber or Large Vehicle Fuel Cells; those need the [Medium Chemical Refinery](medium-chemical-refinery.frontmatter.md).
