# Personal crafting and minor fabricators

Quick answer: the game distinguishes three verbs. "Crafting is done by the character, Fabricating is done by a fabricator, Constructing is done via the Construction Tool" (game text). The personal Crafting menu "can be used to craft basic items when exploring Arrakis"; designs are unlocked with Intel, and "a Fabricator is required to craft more complex items" (game text).

- The UI labels personal crafting "Personal Fabricator" (game text). Which items are personal-only is not listed on the wiki; salvage-tier items (Improvised Cutteray, Improvised Power Pack, Improvised Blood Extractor, Scrap Metal Knife, Healkits from Plant Fiber) are the usual candidates (game text names; wiki research list), unverified per item.
- **Construction Fabricator** ("Workstation for crafting buildings, placeables, deployables, and construction elements") and **Portable Fabricator** ("Device used for crafting items") exist as placeable names, but their item strings are prefixed `XX_NOTUSED` (game text) and the wiki has no pages for them. Treat both as unused in 1.5 (unverified).
- Polar Fabricator and Ice Refinery strings are placeholders (`PH_`), part of unreleased polar content (game text).
