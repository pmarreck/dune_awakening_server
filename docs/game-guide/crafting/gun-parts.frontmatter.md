# Gun Parts

Quick answer: "Key components for a wide variety of standard ranged weapons, often salvaged from the bodies of those who no longer have need for them" (game text). Tiered variants are "Found on Assault and Marksmen enemies" (game text). Id `T1AssaultComponent` (item data). Ingredient in 70 recipes (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Gun_Parts).

- Uses (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Standard Disruptor M11 7, Artisan 11, House 17, Adept 23; Standard Karpov 38 9, Artisan 14, House 22, Adept 29.
- Mk6: Plasteel Composite Gun Parts = 3 Gun Parts + 2 Plasteel Plate + 1 Spice Melange (wiki); Regis guns use 12 to 14 of those.
- Named tiers (Enhanced, Lightweight, Precision, Advanced Gun Parts) exist in game text but recipes use only the base item ([component-families](component-families.frontmatter.md)).
