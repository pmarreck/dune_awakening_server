# Tool costs by tier

Quick answer: Cutterays are ingots + EMF Generators (+ Cobalt and Ray Amplifiers from Mk3); Power Packs are ingots + Particle Capacitors (+ Cobalt from Mk3). Mk1-Mk2 come from the Fabricator or Survival Fabricator, Mk3-Mk5 from the Survival Fabricator, Mk6 from the Advanced Survival Fabricator (wiki recipe data). Numbers are wiki recipe data from before 1.5; Mk4-Mk6 costs may be 10 to 25% lower in 1.5, and this server halves fabrication costs (`CraftingCost=0.5`, server config). See [index](index.frontmatter.md).

| Tier | Cutteray | Power Pack |
|---|---|---|
| Mk1 | 6 Copper, 4 EMF Generator | 5 Copper, 3 Particle Capacitor |
| Mk2 | 10 Iron, 7 EMF | 8 Iron, 5 PC |
| Mk3 | 15 Steel, 10 EMF, 10 Cobalt, 5 Ray Amplifier, 90 mL | 9 Steel, 8 PC, 6 Cobalt, 70 mL |
| Mk4 | 25 Aluminum, 14 EMF, 12 Cobalt, 7 Ray Amplifier, 225 mL | 18 Aluminum, 11 PC, 9 Cobalt, 169 mL |
| Mk5 | 40 Duraluminum, 18 EMF, 16 Cobalt, 10 Ray Amplifier, 7 Thermoelectric Cooler, 450 mL | 12 Duraluminum, 11 PC, 12 Cobalt, 12 Irradiated Slag, 260 mL |
| Mk6 | 50 Plastanium, 30 EMF, 30 Cobalt, 6 Thermo-Responsive Ray Amplifier, 9 Thermoelectric Cooler, 27 Melange, 700 mL | 30 Plastanium, 18 PC, 23 Cobalt, 25 Irradiated Core, 27 Melange, 530 mL |
