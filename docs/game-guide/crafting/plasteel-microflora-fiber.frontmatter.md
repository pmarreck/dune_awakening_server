# Plasteel Microflora Fiber

Quick answer: "A fabrication component used in armor. Found in Great House ruins, such as Shipwrecks" (game text). It cannot be fabricated. Id `GreatHouseComponent1` (item data). Ingredient in 227 recipes, more than any other looted part (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Plasteel_Microflora_Fiber).

- Typical use (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Scavenger Chestpiece 3, Kirab Heavy Jerkin 6, Slaver Heavy Jerkin 8, Duneman Heavy Jacket 11, Mercenary Heavy Chestplate 14, CHOAM Heavy Chestplate 18; blades: Kindjal 3, Standard Sword 4, Artisan 12, House 25, Adept 12, Regis 15.
- 1.5 added it to the Radiation Suit Mk5 and Mk6 recipes (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/); amounts unverified.
- Game text also has regional variants of this family (names such as Enhanced, Efficient, Optimized, Charged, Advanced), each tied to a region from the Vermilius Gap to the Deep Desert, but their item ids carry the `D_` prefix used for deprecated items and no current recipe uses them (item data; wiki recipe data). In 1.5 one item serves every tier; see [component-families](component-families.frontmatter.md).
