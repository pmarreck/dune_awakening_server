# Micro-sandwich Fabric

Quick answer: "the multi-layered water recycling component used in Fremen equipment such as stillsuits"; find it in Fremen caves (game text). It cannot be fabricated. Id `FremenComponent2` (item data).

- Typical use (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5): Scavenger Stillsuit Body 3, Kirab 15, Slaver 20, Native 40, Mercenary 40, CHOAM 54; Medium Blood Sack 12; Large Blood Sack 16; Literjon 2 (survival chapter); Stilltent 18, Double-sealed Stilltent 40.
- 1.5 added it to the Massive Blood Sack, Literjon Mk6, Hajra Literjon Mk6 and The Baron's Bloodbag (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/dune-awakening-console-release-patch-notes/); the wiki recipes for those predate the change and omit it, amounts unverified.
- Game text also has regional variants of this family (names such as Enhanced, Efficient, Optimized, Charged, Advanced), each tied to a region from the Vermilius Gap to the Deep Desert, but their item ids carry the `D_` prefix used for deprecated items and no current recipe uses them (item data; wiki recipe data). In 1.5 one item serves every tier; see [component-families](component-families.frontmatter.md).
