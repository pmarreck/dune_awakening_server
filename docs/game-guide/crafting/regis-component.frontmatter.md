# Regis Component

Quick answer: 1 Offworld Regis Compound + 5 Silicone Block + 5 Cobalt Paste + 1,200 mL water makes 10 Regis Components in the Advanced Survival Fabricator (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Regis_Component). The compound is "An offworld compound that can be requisitioned via the Landsraad" and "Must be adapted to local environments via an Advanced Survival Fabricator before usage" (game text). Ids `T6LandsraadCraftedComponent` and `LandsraadComponent` (item data).

- Used in "all augmentations and high-grade weapon recipes" (wiki). A guide gives 5 to 12 per augmentation by grade (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/how-to-get-offworld-regis-compound-and-regis-components-in-dune-awakening, pre-1.5, unverified).
- The Chapter 3 augmentation journey starts with "Fabricate a Regis Component in the Advanced Survival Fabricator" (game text).
