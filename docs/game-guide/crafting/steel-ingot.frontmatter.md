# Steel Ingot

Quick answer: 1 Iron Ingot + Carbon Ore + 50 mL water in an ore refinery: 4 Carbon in the Small (5 s), 3 in the Medium (4 s), 2 in the Large (3 s) (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Steel_Ingot). Carbon Ore (`DolomiteRock`) needs a Cutteray Mk2 (survival chapter). Item id `SteelBar` (item data).

- Steel builds the mid-game infrastructure: each specialized fabricator (40), Medium Ore Refinery (125), Fremen Deathstill (60) and Staking Unit (15 + 15,000 Solari) (wiki) (wiki recipe data, pre-1.5; see [index](index.frontmatter.md)).
- Used in 104 recipes (wiki). Base vendor price 150 (wiki item data).
