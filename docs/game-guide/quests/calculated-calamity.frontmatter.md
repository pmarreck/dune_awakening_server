# Calculated Calamity

Quick answer: the first contract from the advanced Mentat trainer Zayn de Witte in Arrakeen. A rival syndicate, the Mobula gang, is the target. Start at The Anvil in the Eastern Vermillius Gap, interrogate two gang members, raid their base, learn of a second site called Chattel Passage, sabotage their generator, vehicle and comms console, and take their minimic film back to Zayn (game text, `CONTRACT_MENTAT2_CT1_*`).

## Conditions in order (game text)

1. Interrogate the Mobula Gang Member at the Anvil.
2. Interrogate the second Mobula Gang Member.
3. Enter the Mobula gang's base.
4. Search for information about other bases. A scroll reveals "an intel base at another site: Chattel Passage".
5. Sabotage generator.
6. Sabotage vehicle.
7. Enter Chattel Passage.
8. Find the Mobula Gang's Minimic Film.
9. Sabotage comms console.
10. Deliver the Minimic Film to Zayn in Arrakeen.

The order of the sabotage steps relative to the two bases is as listed in the string table; the game may let you do them in either order (unverified).

## Prerequisites

You need the basic Mentat contract "First Blood" done and to have asked about advanced training at Riftwatch, which points you to Zayn in Arrakeen (game text, journey "Discipline of the Mentat"). Getting to Arrakeen takes a tradepost ornithopter taxi or your own ornithopter (game text, "A Wider World").

## Where Chattel Passage is

The game text names Chattel Passage but not its region. Its exact location is unverified; the contract's map marker after step 4 should show it.

Next contract: "An Olive Branch" (Atreides) or "Underhanded Hand-Over" (Harkonnen); see [Discipline of the Mentat](mentat-training.frontmatter.md).
