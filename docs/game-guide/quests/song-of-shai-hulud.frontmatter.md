# The Song of Shai-Hulud

Quick answer: being eaten by a sandworm starts a short journey. You wake with "a strange melody" in your head; the steps are "Find the source of the Mysterious Singing" and "Talk to the stranger". The stranger is "an odd man ... who spoke to me of the Shai-Hulud, the Maker" (game text, `JOURNEY_SANDWORMRECOVERY_*`).

Where the singer stands and what, if anything, the conversation rewards are not in the game text read here (unverified). Losses from the worm death itself follow the normal death rules; see [survival-combat-crafting.md](../survival-combat-crafting.md).
