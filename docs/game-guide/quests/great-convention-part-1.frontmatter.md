# The Great Convention, Part I

Quick answer: after Old Carthag, Zantara's bat Noctua finds you, and Elara Tuek summons you to Count Fenring in Harko Village. He suspects the Bene Tleilax in a series of grisly murders and sends you to Arrakeen to work with his chief investigator, Sub-Prefect Tors. The chain is detective work: scan rooms for clues, question witnesses, follow leads to a Sandflies base and a Deep Desert cave, then a banquet where eleven nobles are dead. It ends with Ariste taking you into the Bene Gesserit archives beneath the Arrakeen palace (game text, `JOURNEY_CH2_*`; deck title "The Great Convention — Part I").

## Cards in order

Order from the wiki (https://awakening.wiki/Journeys, edited 2026-10-04); steps are game text.

| # | Card (in-game title) | Steps and places |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Call | Look for a sign in a Hagga Basin settlement; Noctua visits with a message you cannot yet understand. |
| 2 | CALLED TO A COUNT | Travel to Harko Village; talk to Elara Tuek at Hannivar's; talk to Count Fenring. |
| 3 | PROCES-VERBAL | Travel to Arrakeen; talk to the Chief Investigator at the Imperial Consulate; locate Osten Laithe in the Upper Salusan Bull; interrogate witnesses; acquire a poison snooper through Alastair Chim or Tidjii O'Garee; gain access to the private room and investigate it with the snooper; scan for clues; convince Hester Klimt that Osten was murdered; discuss findings with Tors. |
| 4 | TOUCH OF EVIL | Locate the Sandflies base in the Eastern Shield Wall, find and scan Osten's workshop (an Old Carthag schematic and a blackmail letter from Phaedra Vye), return to Tors in Arrakeen. |
| 5 | FROM THE ASHES | Enter Phaedra's apartment and scan for clues; search the valet's body and decipher his note; go to the location in the Deep Desert, find the cave entrance, search the cave and its secret room; ask Tidjii O'Garee what she knows; return to Tors. |
| 6 | THE JANUSIAN FAITHFUL | Enter the banquet; identify Ansel Virro, Agade Yimari and Roella Ramsby among the dead and report to Dhood Shazz; talk to the butler and the dying noble; interrogate Ambrose Lucifera; follow the assassin's trail and Tors. |
| 7 | THE BLOODLINE | Meet Ariste at the Salusan Bull; present Ariste's token at the Western Palace Barricade; sneak through the Upper Archives (find young Ariste's four etchings), distract the guards, enter the Lower Archives and look for hidden notes; pass the Bene Gesserit test; provide a blood sample in the breeding matrix; listen to the recording; leave the palace. |
| 8 | Noctua's Return | Look for Noctua in a Hagga Basin settlement; she delivers Zantara's last message through a distrans decoder. |

## Sticking points

- "From the Ashes" sends you into the Deep Desert. Since 1.5 the Deep Desert has both PvE and PvP instances (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/, 2026-09-17); bring a vehicle and storm awareness. See the world chapter for Deep Desert rules.
- If you lose your place in an investigation scene, the game's fallback steps are "Enter the private room in the Salusan Bull in Arrakeen", "Enter Phaedra's apartment in Arrakeen" and "Enter the banquet hall in Arrakeen to continue your journey" (game text, `JOURNEY_CH2_PROCESVERBAL_ALTERNATESTEP_1..3`).
- Many steps are "scan for clues": interactable clue objects (footprints, drag marks, spilled wine, a handprint and so on) each open a short note (game text, `DOCUMENT_JOURNEY_PROCESVERBAL_*`).
- Arrakeen and Harko Village are no-combat social hubs (game text); the investigation's fights happen in the field locations.
