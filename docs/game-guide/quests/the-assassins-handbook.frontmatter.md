# The Assassin's Handbook (Act 2)

Quick answer: Ari sends you to Harko Village for an Ixian decoder. Instead you are taken by the Sardaukar and handed to Count Hasimir Fenring, who says you carry a hypno-ligation (an implanted compulsion) and recruits you. His contact Elara Tuek in Harko Village gives you assassination targets. Between kills you raid the Wreck of the Hephaestus for the decoder, decode the film with Ari in Arrakeen, and follow its coordinates to a Tleilaxu lab under Old Carthag, where the chapter's big reveal happens (game text, `JOURNEY_ACT2_*`).

## Cards in order

Order from the wiki's Journeys page (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Journeys, edited 2026-10-04); steps are game text.

| # | Card | Steps and places |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Black Market Tech | Enter Harko Village; search for a Black Market Trader. The Sardaukar interrogate you and Fenring takes over; find Fenring's contact, Elara Tuek, and accept one of the two assassinations she offers ("The Glutton and the Paranoid. Which do you want to hear about?", dialogue). |
| 2 | The Paranoid | A Maas Kharet cult leader holding the power station of Mysa Tarill. Find the Chosen of the Maas Kharet and kill the Chosen of the Fire, Body, Tooth and Breath for their pentashield codes; use the Shield Dissembler on the last pentashield; kill her in the Place of Contemplation; exit. |
| 3 | The Glutton | A rising slaver leader in the Hagga Basin. Find and enter his dining room, poison his food with chaumas, exit. |
| 4 | The Next Target | Return to Elara Tuek in Harko Village and accept the second name (whichever of 2 or 3 you skipped). |
| 5 | Further Instructions | Return to Elara; return to Hagga Basin and wait; a spice dream ends with a ship falling from the sky and Ari calls about a crashed Ixian ship. |
| 6 | A Sign from Above | The Wreck of the Hephaestus (an overland story map): find a way into the cargo bay, find the smuggled noble's container, turn on auxiliary power, survive the suboid assault, switch off the emergency override, remove the fuse capacitor, restart the power nacelles, reach the bridge and take the Ixian Decoder, escape Gurney Halleck's trap, then reach your base or a city. Feyd-Rautha Harkonnen helps you (completion text). |
| 7 | New Targets | Receive Elara's transmission and return to Harko Village. |
| 8 | The Water Fat | A Water Shipper's mansion just outside Arrakeen, heavily guarded; sneak in, find his water storage, poison the cistern, escape the manor by ornithopter, reach your base or a city. |
| 9 | Last Target | Report to Elara; she assigns the final kill. |
| 10 | The Jackal | A beggar spreading sedition in the alleys of Arrakeen. Elara gives you a shigawire garrote that passes Arrakeen's scanners; find out how to talk to him, reach his alley, kill him, exit. |
| 11 | A Rendezvous with Ari | Meet Ari on the second floor of the Salusan Bull in Arrakeen. Zantara arrives; the decoder plays the film (a recording of "Alnaëm", the Sleeper). |
| 12 | Searching for Answers | Talk to Zantara in Harko Village; reach the lab under Old Carthag (an overland story map); find a way inside, explore, find Zantara deeper in, escape the Tleilaxu facility, reach your base or a city. |
| 13 | Epilogue | "An End to Many Things": wait to hear from Ariste. |

Card 6's title is matched to the Hephaestus card by its place in the wiki order; the card keys for it are `ACT2_HEPHAESTUS` (game text).

## Tips and sticking points

- You choose the order of the first two kills; both must be done (game text, cards 1 and 4).
- Several missions are separate maps reached from the overland map: Wreck of the Hephaestus, Water Shipper's Mansion, Ruins of Old Carthag (game text; see [world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md)). On this server those maps boot on demand, so the first trip can load slowly (verified on this server, see world chapter).
- Update 1.5 made The Glutton, The Paranoid and The Jackal (and the older "Echoes of the Past" mission) instanced, so there is no queue on busy servers (patch notes 1.5, https://duneawakening.com/news/announcement/the-new-dune-awakening-is-here/, 2026-09-17).
- If the Old Carthag step stalls, the game's fallback text is "Talk to Zantara in Harko Village to continue your journey" (game text, `JOURNEY_ACT2_OLDCARTHAG_ALTERNATESTEP`).
- Spoiler level kept practical: the Old Carthag ending changes your understanding of your character and removes one companion; the next chapter picks up from it.

## Rewards

The 1.5 update added rewards to existing story missions, including swatches, weapon and armor variants and decorations, granted retroactively (patch notes 1.5, same URL). Specific items per mission are not listed in the game text read here (unverified).
