# Mysteries of the Bene Gesserit

Quick answer: Sister Mesa at Helius Gate gives "BENE GESSERIT: BASICS: The Missing Pieces": recover two Missionaria Protectiva texts from abandoned Fremen ruins in the O'odham. That opens the tree. Advanced training is with Jocasta Cleo in Harko Village (a back alley near the Atreides vendor, per the wiki), five contracts ending in "The Rogue Bene Gesserit" (game text, `JOURNEY_TRAINER_BENEGESSERIT_1_*`; wiki per [progression-and-skills.md](../progression-and-skills.md#trainers)).

## Basic: The Missing Pieces (Sister Mesa, Helius Gate)

Lead-in notice "Skills of the Sisterhood" ("Ask for Sister Mesa at Helius Gate") (game text). Helius Gate is the Atreides fortress on the Shield Wall (world chapter).

Conditions (game text): find the text "Lisan al-Gaib and ..." and the text "The Mahdi's Blade" in Fremen ruins in the O'odham, and deliver both to Sister Mesa. A third document, "On Witches", is in the same contract's item list (game text).

Helius Gate and the O'odham are northern regions, so this is usually the last of the four starting-class trees a player can reach (inference from geography).

## Advanced: Jocasta Cleo (Harko Village)

| Contract | Conditions (game text) |
|---|---|
| Sacred Records | Search the depths of Mirzabah and recover the Bloodline Records; find the path up the Stone Sentinel and recover the List of Naibs; deliver both. |
| The Missionaria Protectiva | Search for the Panoplia Propheticus fragment, pick up the trail with your Scanner, go to the House of Ilm, recover the fragment, deliver it. |
| The Impact of Ecology | Enter Imperial Testing Station No. 71 and take Kynes's Lexicon of Ecology; find out what happened to Jocasta (question the clientele at Hannivar's, go to an ambush site in the O'odham, clear it, find her hiding nearby); hand the book over in Harko Village. |
| Secrets of the Syndicate | Investigate the Syndicate, find their flight logs, kill the Syndicate Underlords and their leader. |
| The Rogue Bene Gesserit | Practice the Voice (Compel), recite the Litany Against Fear, confront Lady Margot. |

The Rogue Bene Gesserit needs the Compel ability, the Bene Gesserit starting ability (game text, character creation; see the progression chapter).
