# Prowess of the Swordmaster

Quick answer: find Arno at Pinnacle Station and complete "SWORDMASTER: BASICS: Checking the Post", a tour of three dead-drop sites. That opens the tree. Advanced training is with Seron Varlin in Harko Village (Beast's Bend per the wiki) through five contracts ending in "The Last Stand of Seron Varlin" (game text, `JOURNEY_TRAINER_SWORDMASTER_1_*`; wiki per [world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md)).

## Basic: Checking the Post (Arno, Pinnacle Station)

Lead-in notice "Honing of the Blade" ("I am Arno, and you may find me at Pinnacle Station") (game text). Arno is in hiding and needs his dead drops checked for messages (game text, contract description). Conditions (game text):

1. Find Devil's Eye Cavern (northeast Jabal Eifrit Al-Sharq).
2. Find a way into the wreck of the Pallas (Western Vermillius Gap).
3. Enter Imperial Testing Station No. 76 (Jabal Eifrit Al-janub).
4. Report back to Arno at Pinnacle Station.

Region placements are from the world chapter and journey text (game text). Pinnacle Station's region is filed under the Hagga Rift in game data and under Jabal Eifrit Al-gharb by the wiki (see [world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md)).

## Advanced: Seron Varlin (Harko Village)

| Contract | Conditions (game text) |
|---|---|
| A Tormented Soul | Search Hannivar's for clues; question Elara Tuek about the contract; report to Seron. |
| (A Vengeful Soul) | Enter the Sandfly base at Western Comms; kill Sandflies and their leader; take Seron's sword hilt and return it. This contract is not listed among the journey's required steps. |
| Piercing Eyes | Ask the black market trader about Tleilaxu eyes; ask the Tleilaxu trader; complete "Lab Cleanup" (eliminate the Tleilaxu "experiments"); receive the eyes and deliver them to Seron. |
| Art of the Sword | Practice Knee Charge, Eye of the Storm and Crippling Strike; return to Seron. |
| Forged in Dishonor | Introduce yourself to Duncan Idaho and learn Damasteel forging; craft the item and present it; gather blood with a Blood Extractor. Sub-parts "Pupils Past" and "Forged in Regret". |
| The Last Stand of Seron Varlin | Deliver a written challenge to Fenring's associate; encourage or dissuade Seron; meet him after the duel; receive Seron's armor; learn the final Swordmaster passive. |

Duncan Idaho's dojo is in Arrakeen ("Duncan's Dojo", game text, district list in the world chapter). The journey completion text says you "learned the secrets of forging Damasteel" (game text).

## Notes

- Art of the Sword needs those three abilities unlocked and slotted (inference from the conditions).
- The final contract's choice changes dialogue; whether it changes rewards is unverified. Its reward includes Seron's armor (game text).
