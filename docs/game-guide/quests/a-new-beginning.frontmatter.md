# A New Beginning

Quick answer: this deck is the survival tutorial in Hagga Basin South, voiced by Zantara over the communinet. It walks you from plant-fiber clothes to a sandbike: craft gear, get water from blood, build a shielded base, kill scavengers, make a stillsuit after the First Trial of Aql, reach Griffin's Reach Tradepost, loot the Wreck of the Alcyon, loot Imperial Testing Station No. 2 for sandbike parts, then cross north into the Vermillius Gap and move your base (game text, `EARLYJOURNEY_*`).

## Cards in order

Card order follows the wiki's Journeys page (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Journeys, edited 2026-10-04) and the cross-references in the card texts. Step lists and counts are game text from build 25610213.

| # | Card | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Getting Equipped | Harvest 15 plant fiber, research and craft a full set of Makeshift Clothing; salvage 65 metal with the Cutteray, craft an Improvised Maula Pistol, 90 Light Darts and Binoculars. |
| 2 | The Attitude of the Knife | Craft and place a Respawn Beacon; scout a scavenger camp with binoculars; use your ability and kill the scavengers; open the Skills menu and learn a new ability. |
| 3 | Shelter and Shields | Mine 150 granite, salvage 125 metal and 24 fuel cells; craft and equip a Construction Tool; place a Sub-Fief Console; build 4 foundations, a door, 7 walls and 4 roof pieces; place a Small Storage Container and a Fuel-Powered Generator and load fuel cells to power the base shield. A step also asks you to move the console to a sheltered spot. |
| 4 | Water from Blood | Salvage 110 metal and harvest 25 fiber; craft a Blood Extractor and a Small Blood Sack; kill scavengers; extract 1000 ml of blood from corpses; build a Blood Purifier and deposit the blood. |
| 5 | Echoes of the Past | After the First Trial of Aql: find a moisture-sealed cave, gather 6 Micro-Sandwich Fabric and an EMF Generator; salvage 165 metal, 17 fiber, 12 copper ore; drink from the Blood Purifier; build a Fabricator and craft and equip a Scavenger Stillsuit. |
| 6 | A Touch of Civilization | Build a Small Ore Refinery, refine 12 copper ore into 3 ingots, fabricate a Survey Probe Launcher and probe, fire it from high ground; travel to Griffin's Reach Tradepost, take the first SCRAP METTLE contract and buy the unique Hajra Literjon schematic. Zantara has already left because of a bounty on him. |
| 7 | The Wreck of the Alcyon | Loot the nearby Atreides frigate wreck for 10 Plasteel Microflora Fiber and 2 Mechanical Parts; mine 160 copper and 25 fiber; craft Scavenger Rags, a Karpov 38 and a Kindjal. |
| 8 | Across the Gap | In Imperial Testing Station No. 2 gather 6 Particle Capacitors and 13 Advanced Servoks; mine 376 copper ore and 25 fuel cells; fabricate the sandbike PSU, 3 treads, engine, chassis and hull; build a Small Chemical Refinery and refine vehicle fuel; make a Welding Torch Mk1, assemble, fuel and drive the sandbike north; research and use the Base Reconstruction Tool to back up your base. |
| 9 | Moving On | Cross the Vermillius Gap ("the shortest, flattest route ... just northeast of the Griffin's Reach Tradepost"), research the Advanced Construction Kit, place an Advanced Sub-Fief Console, place your backed-up base, and abandon your Hagga Basin South base from the map. |

The wiki lists "Moving On" under the Vermillius Gap deck rather than here (wiki, same page); the game keys it `EARLYJOURNEY_RELOCATE` (game text).

## Common sticking points

- Echoes of the Past starts only after the First Trial of Aql; see [Trials of Aql](trials-of-aql.frontmatter.md). The trial's completion text says the vision taught you the stillsuit (game text).
- Moisture-sealed caves are Fremen caves; the components for the stillsuit come only from them (game text, card 5).
- Testing Station No. 2 is guarded; the Planetologist trainer Derek Chinara camps above it, so this run can double as his first contract (game text, Planetologist contract text). See [Planetologist training](planetologist-training.frontmatter.md).
- The sandbike makes worm noise; avoid open sand at speed and keep to rock where possible (game text, "Desert Ride": "These too anger the worms").
- Side cards in the Hagga Basin South deck run alongside this one: "A Wider World" (talk to a tradepost ornithopter pilot and reach Arrakeen or Harko Village), "A Backup Plan" (Sandbike Inventory Mk1 and the Vehicle Backup Tool) and "Location, location, location" (Solido Replicator, base teardown) (game text, `JOURNEY_HBS_*`). See [Regional journeys](regional-journeys.frontmatter.md).

## Rewards

Journey cards themselves unlock no gear; the deck's payoff is the recipes you research along the way and contract pay from the SCRAP METTLE chain, whose last contract ("Bike Building") hands over sandbike components (game text, `SCAVENGERS1_CONTRACT5_DEBRIEF_DESC`). Story XP is scaled by the server's "Mission Experience" setting (game text; see [progression-and-skills.md](../progression-and-skills.md)).
