# The Great Convention, Part II

Quick answer: Elara Tuek traces the Face Dancer from Chapter 2 to an illegal dueling ring near Neo Carthag. You fight up five tiers of the arena under the name "Degenerate Killer"; before each tier the arena master Maksim Dubrov sends you to fetch something (a radiation core, an elacca shipment, a suboid, an abomination). The fifth opponent is the Face Dancer. Then Elara sends you to the Smugglers' hideout Fallen Light for a gauntlet run beside Feyd-Rautha (game text, `JOURNEY_B1C3_*`; deck "The Great Convention — Part II").

This chapter arrived with Chapter 3, update 1.3, on 2026-02-03 (wiki, https://www.awakening.wiki/Chapter_3, edited 2026-09-22).

## Cards in order (wiki order; steps game text)

| # | Card | Steps and places |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Call to Arms | Travel to any safe settlement and wait for Elara's call; talk to Elara in Harko Village. She gives you a passphrase for the ring. |
| 2 | The Art of Kanly | Talk to Elara's contact near the North Terminus exit in Harko Village; enter the arena lobby; ask Maksim Dubrov about fighting; win a first fight against scavengers; defeat Brute the Scrapper. |
| 3 | Secrets of the Exile | Talk to Maksim; travel to the Sheol and search for clues about a radiation core; look near the Wreck of the Ourea, find the core's control room, use the console to close the container, take the core back. Then beat a Kirab encounter and Aren the Exile. |
| 4 | Breaking the Chains | Talk to Maksim; find a lost elacca shipment in the Deep Desert, follow footprints into Testing Station No. 217, secure the crates, return. Then beat elacca-drugged fighters, slavers and the Master of Chains. |
| 5 | Extinguishing the Flames | Talk to Maksim; go to the Wreck of the Hephaestus, search the cargo deck, hangars and aft section, knock out a suboid with a floodlight and capture it, deliver it. Then beat two Sandflies groups, suboids and Thrax the Flamer. |
| 6 | A Deadly Poison | Talk to Maksim; go to the ruins of Old Carthag, search the rooms and incubators, survive an ambush, open the pods and capture an unconscious abomination, leave by ornithopter, deliver it. Then beat Maas Kharet cultists, abominations and Sinnu the Poisoner. |
| 7 | Outside the Law | Report to Elara; travel to Fallen Light; run the Free Trader gauntlet with Feyd-Rautha through Kirab, Sandflies and Maas Kharet assaults; kill the Minotaur and catch the Face Dancer; report back to Elara in Harko Village. |
| 8 | Call of the Night | Travel to a tradepost in the Hagga Basin; Noctua brings the message "Ibn Qirtaiba, Zantara. We are coming." |

## Sticking points

- The fetch errands reach late-game zones: the Sheol needs radiation protection (see the Sheol regional cards in [Regional journeys](regional-journeys.frontmatter.md)), and the elacca errand is in the Deep Desert (game text).
- Each tier's fights are arena waves ending in a named champion (game text, `..._STEP4_SUBSTEP*`). Prepare between tiers; the fetch errand comes first.
- Part II of the story runs in parallel with the Chapter 3 faction and Landsraad journeys ("Greater Arrakis" deck); see [Faction Chapter 3 journeys](faction-chapter-3.frontmatter.md).
- Patch 1.5.15.0 (public test, 2026-09-30) made the Chapter 3 and 4 journeys award XP on completion (patch notes 1.5.15.0, https://duneawakening.com/news/public-test-client-patch-1-5-15-0/); whether that is live on this server's build is unverified.
