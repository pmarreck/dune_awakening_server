# Lost Harvest (DLC)

Quick answer: a standalone paid story arc released with Chapter 2 in September 2025 (community, https://alcasthq.com/dune-awakening-dlc-the-lost-harvest/, 2025). A distress call from the spice harvester Mithra sends you to its crash site northwest of Griffin's Reach Tradepost. You help the stranded Dune Men, chase a stolen package for Keif Villari, and finally bring Elara Tuek the gelspheres of "thinking machines" found in forgotten labs (game text, `JOURNEY_MTX_CHAPTER1_LOSTHARVEST_*`).

## Cards in order (game text)

| # | Card | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Fall of the Mithra | Listen to the distress call; reach the crash site northwest of Griffin's Reach; locate survivors; complete contracts for Quartermaster Hix, Lieutenant Drangh and Chief Hanso; talk to Hanso and complete "Following Up". |
| 2 | Villari's Prize | Complete Keif Villari's contract: the package went to a Kirab hideout and was stolen again by slavers. |
| 3 | Digging Deeper | Complete the "Digging Deeper" contract (recover a gelsphere and capture a thinking machine's essence in a lab); return to the surface; bring the package to the contact in Harko Village; return to Hagga Basin and wait for Elara; return to her in Harko Village. |
| 4 | Secrets of the Past | Complete "Secrets of the Past" and "Once Upon a Time", reporting to Elara after each. |

The Dune Men contracts include "Radio Silenced", "Boosting the Signal", "A Fighting Chance", "No Trespassing!", "A Healing Hand", "Search and Retrieval" and "Lost Within" (game text). The two last-card contracts are filed as "Lost Harvest — No. 60" and "Lost Harvest — No. 163", matching Imperial Testing Stations 60 and 163 (game text, chain names; the station match is inferred).

## Notes

- Starting it needs the DLC on your account; the content appears in a normal Hagga Basin world (inference from the start being a distress call in Hagga Basin South).
- The early steps sit in the starter region, but the last card's labs are in the O'odham, a late region (game text; world chapter).
- The DLC also adds the Treadwheel vehicle and cosmetics (community, same alcasthq page; see [vehicles.md](../vehicles.md)).
