# The Footsteps of the Fremen

Quick answer: after the seventh trial the altar shows a map to a sietch. Inside you meet the scholar Ari, solve two Fremen puzzles, open a hidden chamber holding a recording of another man sent to "Find the Fremen", take a minimic film, and flee when the Maas Kharet cult storms the place. The Epilogue has you wait for Ari, who has gone looking for an Ixian decoder for the film (game text).

## Where

- Game text: "a place at the border of Mysa Tarill in the west and Jabal Eifrit Al-gharb", with the riddle "The crevice that hides ... the mountain that protects ... the sands that provide. Home." (`JOURNEY_CARD_DESCRIPTION_THESIETCH`, `JOURNEY_MAP_Sietch_Description`). A trial step also reads "Survey the Eastern Shield Wall to locate the next trial" (game text).
- Community: Sietch Ta'lab in the Eastern Shield Wall, entrance in a crack in the cliff reachable from the ground or by dropping in from above; bring a Cutteray and a weapon (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/where-is-the-footsteps-of-the-freeman-eighth-trial-of-aql-in-dune-awakening, 2025, pre-1.5). The world chapter also lists Sietch Ta'lab under the Shield Wall (see [world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md)).

## Steps (game text, `JOURNEY_ACT1_Sietch_Task_*` and card tasks)

1. A Final Test: find the cave.
2. Explore the sietch with Ari.
3. Complete the ecology lesson (a symbol puzzle; the sietch holds fifteen "Fremen Symbol" scripture pickups such as Sand, Dune, Shelter and Moon (game text, `DOCUMENT_SIETCH_SCRIPTURES_*`)).
4. Continue exploring the abandoned sietch.
5. Realign the mechanism and activate the ancient deathstill.
6. Enter the secret chamber; find the safe mentioned in the projection.
7. Find a way out of the secret chamber.
8. Survive the Assault (the Maas Kharet invasion).
9. Find your way out.
10. Epilogue: wait for contact from Ari about decoding the minimic film.

The step order is reconstructed from the task names and the card's completion text; the string table does not number them.

## Rewards and what it unlocks

- method.gg lists the Fremen Deathstill schematic as the reward (community, same page). The Sheol regional card "Life from Death" agrees that the deathstill idea comes from the sietch ("In the Sietch, I discovered and analyzed a curious Fremen device") (game text).
- Finishing the Epilogue leads into Act 2, [The Assassin's Handbook](the-assassins-handbook.frontmatter.md): Ari asks you to look for a Black Market Trader in Harko Village (game text, `JOURNEY_ACT1_EPILOGUE_CARD_COMPLETION_EPILOGUE`).

## Sticking points

- "Wait for contact" is a timed or location-triggered step: the game says to wait for Ari's call; there is no NPC to walk to (game text). The trigger condition is unverified; later chapters trigger similar calls by going to "any safe settlement" or "a watersealed base" (game text).
- Answers to the puzzles are not reproduced here; method.gg links a puzzle guide (community).
