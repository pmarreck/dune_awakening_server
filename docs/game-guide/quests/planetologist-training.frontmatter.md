# Knowledge of the Planetologist

Quick answer: Planetologist is not a starting class. Derek Chinara camps "above Imperial Testing Station No. 2" in "the rocky clefts above the Blight" in Hagga Basin South. Talk to him and do "Minimic Film Recovery" (enter Testing Station No. 2 and recover the film reels) to open the tree. The same NPC runs the advanced chain of eight more contracts ending in "The Final Piece" (game text, `JOURNEY_TRAINER_PLANETOLOGIST_1_*`).

## How players find him

Two kinds of lead-in contracts point to Derek (game text):
- "I Spy a Planetologist" from Cyprian Io: find Derek at his camp, do his contract, deliver your report at Griffin's Reach and call the issuer.
- "Planetology Aid" notices for Testing Stations 197 (Vermillius Gap), 76 (Jabal Eifrit Al-janub), 29 (Hagga Rift), 71 and 163 (O'odham): find and help Derek there.

Testing Station No. 2 is also where [A New Beginning](a-new-beginning.frontmatter.md) sends you for sandbike parts, so the basic contract fits naturally into that trip.

## Contracts (game text, journey order)

| Contract | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Minimic Film Recovery (basic) | Enter Imperial Testing Station No. 2 and recover the hidden minimic film cache past the scavengers. |
| Buried Archives | Recover the records of Testing Station No. 197. |
| Adhering to Hierarchy | Find Cyprian Io on the second floor of the Salusan Bull in Arrakeen, deliver Derek's report, request access to Kynes's notes, return to Derek's camp. |
| Science Unlocked | Derek is caged by slavers in a testing station: find the cage keys and the films, deliver the films, free him. |
| The Search for Kynes | Talk to Thufir Hawat (Atreides) or Piter de Vries (Harkonnen) about Kynes; investigate Sietch Tarl ("north of Mysa Tarill, where the O'odham meets the Shield Wall", dialogue); bring the note back. |
| A Reasonable Routine | (conditions not in the strings read) |
| Clues from the Past | A cultist-held testing station: find Kynes's research notes and the films, deliver both. |
| The Planetologist Report | Deliver the report to Cyprian Io; save Derek from two sniper teams; search his camp for clues. |
| The Final Piece | Find a recording of Kynes's lecture (an old holo projector) in a testing station and deliver it. |

A community guide places a later stage at Imperial Testing Station No. 197 (community, pcgamer, 2025) and the advanced text mentions Testing Station 142, lair of the Sandflies' Red Scorpion (game text, `TRAINER_ADVANCED_PLANETOLOGIST_04_LONGDESC`). Which station each contract uses beyond those named is unverified.

The Planetologist chain is the longest of the five schools (nine contracts) (game text).
