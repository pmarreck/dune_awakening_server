# Regional journey decks

Quick answer: beside the story, the Journey menu has one deck per region of the Hagga Basin plus the Deep Desert. Each card is a short checklist: research X, fabricate X, use X, visit the region's landmarks. Following them in region order is a reliable gear path. Copper and the sandbike in the south; iron, shields and dew reapers in the Vermillius Gap; steel, the buggy and specialized fabricators in Jabal Eifrit and the Hagga Rift; aluminum, the ornithopter, the spice mask and radiation suits on the Shield Wall and in the O'odham; then the Sheol and the Deep Desert (game text, `JOURNEY_HBS_*`, `_VG_*`, `_JE_*`, `_OH_*`, `_SL_*`, `_DD_*`; deck grouping per the wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Journeys, edited 2026-10-04).

## Hagga Basin South (alongside A New Beginning)

- A Wider World: travel to a tradepost, talk to the ornithopter pilot, reach Harko Village or Arrakeen. Pilots fly between the tradeposts and the two cities for payment up front.
- A Backup Plan: research and fabricate a Sandbike Inventory Mk1 and a Vehicle Backup Tool; store a vehicle with it.
- Location, location, location: research and fabricate the Solido Replicator, copy a base, demolish the old one (door, 4 foundations, 4 roof, 7 walls, the console).

## Vermillius Gap ("Explore the Vermillius Gap and discover its secrets")

| Card | Main tasks |
|---|---|
| Preparing for life in Vermillius | Cutteray Mk1 (copper upgrade for iron mining); Holtzman Shield Mk2, kill 5 enemies with it equipped. The card warns the shield "enrages the worm". |
| Styles of Sand | Handheld Resource Scanner; find and gather 20 Flour Sand, refine to Silicone; gather 75 Spice Sand and refine to Spice Melange. |
| Reap and Stow | Water Cistern, Literjon, Dew Reaper Mk2; reap dew at night and deposit it. |
| Weapons of War | Iron gear: Standard Sword, GRDA 44 scattergun, Disruptor M11, Kirab Heavy Armor; 15 kills with each weapon. |
| Touring the Gap | Visit the Anvil Tradepost, Southeast Ironworks, Testing Station No. 10, Flatrock Outpost, Testing Station No. 197, Wreck of the Pallas. |
| The Vermillius Contract | At the Anvil, complete the chains PAYBACK and MONSTER SLAYING. |
| Maintenance | Welding Torch repairs a vehicle; Repair Station; Recycler (recycle 10 items). |
| Interior Decoration | Buy a CHOAM Furniture set and place a decoration. |
| Buried Treasure | Static Compactor plus scanner: find and unbury a treasure on open sand. |

## The Sheol and the Deep Desert

- The Graveyard: survey the Sheol, the Wreck of the Ourea, the Harkonnen base in the Northern Sheol.
- Mitigation Iteration: Iodine Pills (from O'odham agave flowers) to stretch radiation time.
- Life from Death: Fremen Deathstill and Medium Water Cistern; kill an enemy, carry the corpse home and render it into water.
- Deep Desert: Charting the Depths (find a Control Point, Testing Station, shipwreck and large spice field), Logistical Issues (temporary base), Operational Readiness (Sandcrawler Mk6 with vacuum and centrifuge, Carrier Ornithopter Mk6), There Is Never Enough (sandcrawler harvesting with a thumper decoy), Smoke Trail (Regis Missile Launcher, 20 missiles, 15 kills).

## Miscellaneous

- Imperial Taxes: carry 6500 Solari and pay an Imperial Tax Representative in Harko Village or Arrakeen (game text). The 1.5 update removed base upkeep taxes and forgave overdue taxes (patch notes 1.5); whether this card still appears is unverified.
- The Song of Shai-Hulud: see [its entry](song-of-shai-hulud.frontmatter.md).
