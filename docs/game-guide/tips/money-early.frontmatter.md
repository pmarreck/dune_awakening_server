# Making Solari early

**Quick answer.** Take contracts from the boards at tradeposts (start with the SCRAP METTLE chain at Griffin's Reach that the opening journey points you to), do kill-count bounties, and sell surplus to players on the Exchange rather than to NPC vendors (game text; wiki; community). Bank your Solari: the Exchange spends from your bank (game text).

## Sources

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Contracts | Boards at all four Hagga Basin tradeposts, fortresses and the cities; rewards go into your backpack or are claimable later from Journey, Contracts (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Contracts). The game's contract reward table starts at 1,500 / 2,000 / 3,000 Solari for small / medium / large tier-1 rewards (game config, see [Earning Solari](../economy-social-versions.md#earning-solari)). |
| Bounties | Kill-count contracts such as "Bounty: Deserters" and "Kirab Bounty" (game text). |
| Scavenger camps and outposts | Camps hold salvage, Fuel Cells and other valuables "if you are willing to fight for them" (game text); outposts hold loot and Intel (game text). |
| The Exchange | Player market at CHOAM Exchange terminals in Arrakeen and Harko Village (wiki). Sell orders cost 20 Solari per day plus 2% of the price, lost if you cancel (game config). |
| NPC vendors | Buy back at a small fraction of their sell price (community, 2025, pre-1.5). Sell to them only what nobody else wants. |
| Landsraad missions | Pay Solari, XP and Landsraad contribution once you have joined a faction and a faction-aligned guild (game text; patch notes Chapter 3). |

## First things worth buying

- The Hajra Literjon schematic at Griffin's Reach, which the journey sends you to buy (game text).
- Taxi flights instead of long walks: 150 Solari to another tradepost, 500 to a city (patch notes 1.5.1.0 PTC). Pilots take inventory or bank Solari (patch notes, Chapter 3).
- Guild creation costs 1,000 Solari (game config), if you play with friends.

## Pitfalls

- Solari carried in your inventory are an item (`SolarisCoin`, stack 50,000; game config); deposit them with a banker so a death cannot drop them (inference from the death rules; the drop flag for Solari is unverified).
- Cancelling an Exchange listing forfeits its fee (game text).
