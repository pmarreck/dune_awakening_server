# Stamina and climbing

**Quick answer.** Climbing drains stamina, and stamina recharges automatically unless you are sprinting (game text). Keep hydration above one third (and ideally two thirds) for a larger stamina bar, stop sprinting before a climb, and rest on ledges.

## What changes your stamina

- Drains: climbing, sprinting (blocks recharge), dashing, attacking and blocking; items list their Dash, Attack, Block and Climbing stamina costs (game text).
- Larger bar: hydration above one third, larger above two thirds (game text). Specialization Trait "Max Stamina Increase" (game text).
- Refills: a successful parry restores some stamina (game text); 10 for daggers, swords and dual blades in the 1.5 release notes (patch notes 1.5).
- Light armor reduces dash cost; heavy armor does not (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-best-armor-tier-list).
- Holding a weapon lowers movement speed; holster to travel faster (game text, "Holster / Unholster").

## Climbing habits

- Hold forward against a surface to attach (game text).
- Start a climb with a full bar: sprint to the wall, then wait a moment before you climb.
- Look for ledges to rest on partway up a tall face.
- Climbing to high points is how you fire survey probes and spot outposts with binoculars (game text).
- Suspensors soften or prevent falls and some allow a double jump (community, https://www.thegamer.com/dune-awakening-traversal-tips-tricks/, pre-1.5), but they enrage worms on sand (game text). The Planetologist Explorer subtree has a passive named "Mountaineer" (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Planetologist); that it helps climbing is inferred from the name.

## This server

`PlayerStaminaDrain` is 0.8, so stamina drains 20% slower than default (verified on this server).
