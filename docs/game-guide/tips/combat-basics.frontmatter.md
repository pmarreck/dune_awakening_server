# Combat basics for newcomers

**Quick answer.** Three quick light attacks stagger an enemy; a heavy (slow blade) attack breaks block and gets through shields after a stagger or parry; time your block to the enemy's swing to parry, which staggers them and refunds stamina; dash to escape a stagger (game text). Heal with Healkits crafted from Plant Fiber (game text).

## Melee in four rules

1. Light attacks are quick; several in a row stagger the target (game text).
2. Heavy attacks are slow, break block and stagger if they land (game text).
3. Parry by blocking just as the enemy attacks; it staggers them and restores some stamina (game text). The 1.5 update widened the parry window to 0.33 s for swords, daggers and dual blades (patch notes 1.5).
4. When staggered, dash in any direction to snap out; dashing out of an ability-caused stagger costs more stamina (game text).

## Shields

- Shielded enemies: stagger then heavy attack, or poison or fire, which pass through shields, or concentrated fire to disable the shield briefly; disruptors are best at breaking shields (game text).
- Your own Holtzman shield must be switched on, drops briefly when you fire a ranged weapon, and reactivates by itself (game text).
- Never use a shield on open sand: it "attracts sandworms much faster" (game text), at 500 threat per second against 15 for walking (server config).

## Healing

- Healkits heal wounds and cleanse ongoing effects; use one from the radial wheel, hotbar or inventory (game text). Early Healkits are crafted from Plant Fiber, which grows all over Arrakis (game text).
- Keep a stack: Healkits stack to 20 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Healkit).

## Picking fights

- Scout outposts and camps with binoculars from a safe distance first (game text).
- Bodies of downed enemies carry tools, resources and components, and their blood is water (game text).
- Spend skill points as soon as you get them; you can equip up to three abilities (game text). Respec costs Spice Melange after the first in a period and needs a watersealed base or social hub (game text; patch notes 1.3.0.0).

## This server

Players deal 1.2x damage to enemies, enemies deal 0.8x to players, and your personal shield mitigates twice the default (`PlayerShieldDamageAbsorptionMultiplier=2`) (server config).
