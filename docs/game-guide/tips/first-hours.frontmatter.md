# Your first hours on Arrakis

**Quick answer.** Follow the "A New Beginning" journey deck (Journey menu, key J): it is the game's own ordered checklist and every step names exactly what to research, gather and craft. In short: plant-fiber clothes and a pistol, a respawn beacon, a small powered shelter, blood-to-water, a survey probe and the Griffin's Reach tradepost, the Wreck of the Alcyon for better gear, a stillsuit, then a sandbike to cross north into the Vermillius Gap (game text).

## The opening journey, in order

The early journey cards and their steps are game text (`PROGRESSION/EARLYJOURNEY_*`). Quantities are what each step asks for.

| Card | What you do |
|---|---|
| Getting Equipped | Harvest 15 Plant Fiber, research and craft Makeshift Clothing; salvage 65 metal with the Cutteray, research the Maula Pistol Kit and Binoculars, craft an Improvised Maula Pistol, 90 Light Darts and Binoculars. |
| The Attitude of the Knife | Research, craft and place a Respawn Beacon; scout a scavenger camp with binoculars; kill scavengers with your class ability; spend a skill point on a new ability. |
| Shelter and Shields | Mine 150 Granite Stone, salvage 125 metal and 24 Fuel Cells; research the Basic Construction Kit, craft the Construction Tool, place a Sub-Fief Console; build 4 foundations, 7 walls, a door and 4 roofs; move the console to a sheltered spot; add a Small Storage Container and a Fuel-Powered Generator and feed it Fuel Cells to raise the base shield. |
| Water from Blood | Salvage 110 metal and 25 Plant Fiber; research the Basic Exsanguination Kit; craft a Blood Extractor and Small Blood Sack; kill scavengers and extract 1,000 mL of blood; research and build a Blood Purifier and deposit the blood. |
| A Touch of Civilization | Research and build a Small Ore Refinery, refine 12 Copper Ore into 3 Copper Ingots; research the Surveying Kit; make a Survey Probe Launcher and a probe; fire it from high ground; travel to Griffin's Reach Tradepost, take the first SCRAP METTLE contract and buy the unique Hajra Literjon schematic. |
| The Wreck of the Alcyon | Gather Plasteel Microflora Fiber and Mechanical Parts at the wreck; mine 160 Copper Ore; research and fabricate Scavenger Rags, a Karpov 38 rifle and a Kindjal. |
| Echoes of the Past | Find a moisture-sealed cave (orange marker), collect Micro-Sandwich Fabric and an EMF Generator; research and build a Fabricator; fabricate and equip a Scavenger Stillsuit; drink from your Blood Purifier. |
| Across the Gap | Get Particle Capacitors and Advanced Servoks inside Imperial Testing Station No. 2; mine 376 Copper Ore and 25 Fuel Cells; research Sandbike Mk1 Assembly and fabricate its PSU, 3 treads, engine, chassis and hull; research a Small Chemical Refinery and refine vehicle fuel; fabricate a Welding Torch Mk1, assemble, refuel and drive the sandbike north; research and use the Base Reconstruction Tool to back up your base. |
| Moving On | Cross the Vermillius Gap by "the shortest, flattest route, which looks to be just northeast of the Griffin's Reach Tradepost", research the Advanced Construction Kit, build an Advanced Sub-Fief Console in your new area and abandon the old Hagga Basin South base. |

## Habits to start on day one

- Place a Respawn Beacon before you go anywhere risky, and move it as you move (game text; see [death and recovery](death-and-recovery.frontmatter.md)).
- Pick up every loose rock, ore piece and salvage scrap you pass; small pieces are collected by hand, big wrecks need the Cutteray (game text).
- Read the map legend colors: green markers (shipwrecks) hold weapon and armor parts, blue (testing stations) vehicle and tech parts, orange (moisture-sealed caves) water and gathering parts (game text).
- Spend skill points and Intel as they arrive. The game nags you ("You have unspent Skill Points", "You have unused Intel") because idle points do nothing (game text).
- Hagga Basin South is the place to make mistakes: the Base Reconstruction Tool has no re-placement cooldown there (game text; patch notes 1.5.1.0).

## Notes

A 2025 guide mentions a free, non-modifiable sandbike at Broken Stone Station in Hagga Basin South that the Vehicle Backup Tool cannot store (community, https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/dune-awakening-how-to-get-sandbike-fast-crafting-guide/, pre-1.5). Broken Stone Station is a real outpost name (game text), but the free bike is unverified for 1.5.
