# Crossing the Vermillius Gap

**Quick answer.** Cross on a fueled sandbike, by the route the journey itself recommends: "the shortest, flattest route, which looks to be just northeast of the Griffin's Reach Tradepost" (game text, journey "Moving On"). Before you go: place a Respawn Beacon on the south side, store valuables at base, back your base up, and carry the Vehicle Backup Tool.

## Before you leave

- Respawn Beacon placed near the crossing point. Only one beacon is active at a time, and a new one replaces the old (game text).
- Valuables left in base storage: a worm takes what you carry (game text; on this server, your backpack, per `SandwormConsequences=Backpack`, server config).
- Base backed up with the Base Reconstruction Tool; the journey has you do this before crossing (game text, "Across the Gap").
- Sandbike fueled: refuel by carrying a vehicle fuel cell and interacting with the bike (community, pre-1.5; HUD shows "Refueled. New Status: n %", game text).
- Full water and a blood extractor.

## The crossing

1. Start from the rock nearest the far side; the game's tutorial for this crossing says "avoid lingering and take the shortest path to safety" (game text, "Crossing to Vermillius Gap").
2. Drive straight; do not stop to look at loot on the sand. The vibration meter does not drain while you are on sand (server config; see [Avoiding sandworms](worm-avoidance.frontmatter.md)).
3. Watch for quicksand and drumsand markers on the map; quicksand can kill and drumsand attracts the worm (game text). Drumsand shows on the map as red spots (verified on this server, 2026-10-26, during a buggy crossing that drew a worm). Western Vermillius Gap is known for quicksand (community, pre-1.5).
4. If the meter goes red, steer for the nearest rock rather than pressing on.
5. On arrival, get off the sand before stopping. Ground vehicles left on open sand are worm bait.

## After

The journey continues: research the Advanced Construction Kit, build an Advanced Sub-Fief Console in the new area, and abandon the Hagga Basin South console from the map (game text). The Anvil tradepost is in the Eastern Vermillius Gap, with faction representatives and a sandbike garage (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tradeposts).

On foot is possible but slow, and the whole walk stays on sand where the meter only rises (server config); a sandbike crosses the same distance in far less time. The sandworm-death tutorial gives the same advice: "Stick to rocky ground ... or use a vehicle to travel quickly across the open sands" (game text).
