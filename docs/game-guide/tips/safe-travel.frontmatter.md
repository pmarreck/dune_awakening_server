# Traveling safely

**Quick answer.** Reveal an area with a survey probe from high ground, scout with binoculars, set a waypoint, then move by night along rock and shade, with water full and a Respawn Beacon placed behind you (game text). For long trips between tradeposts or to the cities, a taxi ornithopter from a tradepost is cheap and worm-proof.

## Scouting tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Survey Probe Launcher and Survey Probe | "Seek out high points in the region and launch a survey probe"; it fully reveals an area on the map: locations, collectibles, hazards, resource distribution and topography (game text). A probe costs one Copper Ingot (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Survey_Probe). |
| Binoculars | Locate points of interest and set a waypoint; use them to find enemy outposts from a safe distance (game text). |
| Scanner | Reveals components, vehicles, bases and people in range; also helps spot quicksand and drumsand (game text). |
| Map and compass | Discovered places show gray, then change color once visited; only larger places appear on the compass (game text). Map filters include Ores, Crystals, Foliage, Salvageable, Spice Field and Flour Sand (game text). |
| Journey tracking | Tracking a journey step or Overland location gives a directional arrow (game text). |

## Route rules

- Night travel avoids sunstroke ("Travel by night", game text).
- Prefer rock ridges and caves to sand flats (game text: "Stick to rocky ground to avoid the sandworm's attention").
- Note sandstorm markers on the map and head for shelter early (game text).
- Plan water stops; in a group, carry extra for revives (game text).
- After a Coriolis storm, fog of war returns over unsurveyed parts of Hagga Basin and resource nodes move (game text; wiki), so survey again.

## Between maps

- Only flying vehicles can leave a map; you need at least 50% fuel and 30% hydration, no passengers and no harnessed vehicle (game text).
- On the Overland Map movement costs fuel and time costs water; stop at Refuel Points (game text).
- A ground vehicle left behind when you change maps "will be destroyed and can only be recovered using the Vehicle Backup Tool" (game text). Store it first.
- Taxi pilots at every tradepost fly to Arrakeen, Harko Village and other tradeposts; fares are 500 Solari to a city and 150 to a tradepost (game text; patch notes 1.5.1.0 PTC).
- On this server every map except Hagga Basin starts on demand, so a first trip to a map can take a little longer while it boots (verified on this server, `docs/operations.md`).
