# What to research first with Intel

**Quick answer.** Research what the opening journey asks for, in its order: Makeshift Clothing, Maula Pistol Kit, Binoculars, Respawn Beacon, Basic Construction Kit, Basic Exsanguination Kit, Blood Purifier, Small Ore Refinery, Surveying Kit, Scavenger gear, Fabricator, Sandbike Mk1 Assembly, Small Chemical Refinery, Base Reconstruction Tool, Advanced Construction Kit (game text, journey steps). After that, put Intel into the Vehicle Backup Tool, better water containers and stillsuits, and the next metal tier of tools.

## How Intel works, briefly

- The Research menu (key Y; game data, `docs/admin.md`) lists designs by category and tier and shows where to find each design's components (game text). Tiers unlock by total Intel spent; the wiki gives 19 Intel spent for Copper up to 1,200 for Plastanium (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Intel).
- Intel comes from levels (about 2,779 in total, none after level 125) and from pickups in enemy outposts, marked "Intel" on the map (verified on this server; game text). Enemy outposts "contain valuable Loot and Intel. Use your Binoculars to locate outposts from a safe distance" (game text).
- Intel can also buy Imperial Permits for unique items (game text). Early on, permits are a poor use of scarce Intel compared with unlocking basics.

More detail is in the progression chapter, [Intel and the Research menu](../progression-and-skills.md#intel-and-the-research-menu).

## Suggested priority after the opening journey

| Priority | Research | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vehicle Backup Tool | A Copper-tier item that stores your sandbike and recovers a destroyed vehicle for Solari (game text; wiki). The journey card "A Backup Plan" recommends carrying it with a spare vehicle stored (game text). |
| 2 | Literjon and better stillsuits | Water capacity and catchpockets decide how far you can travel (game text). |
| 3 | Next Cutteray tier | Each Cutteray tier opens new ores: Mk2 carbon and erythrite, Mk3 aluminum and basalt, Mk4 jasmium (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Cutteray). Steel needs carbon, so Mk2 gates the Steel tier. |
| 4 | Recycler and Repair Station | Turn junk into materials and keep gear working (game text). A 2025 guide says to unlock the Recycler early (community, https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=233039, pre-1.5). |
| 5 | Windtrap and cisterns | Passive base water once blood runs short (game text). |

The order of priorities 2 to 5 is advice, not game rule; adjust it to what you are short of.

## Pitfalls

- Intel has a cap; at the cap pickups say "Intel Point Limit Reached" (game text). Spend before you farm outposts.
- Researching a schematic you found needs you to be watersealed at a base, city or tradepost (game text).
- On this server `IntelPointsGainMultiplier` is 1 (default) (server config, `docs/game-guide/economy-social-versions.md`).
