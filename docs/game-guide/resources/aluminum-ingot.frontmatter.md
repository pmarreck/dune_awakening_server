# Aluminum Ingot

"An Aluminum ingot, refined from Aluminum Ore at a Medium Ore Refinery ... Can also be further processed into Duraluminum with Jasmium Crystals" (game text). Item id `AluminiumBar` (British spelling in the id), stacks to 500 (item data).

| Refinery | Input | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Small Ore Refinery | not possible | |
| Medium Ore Refinery | 7 Aluminum Ore + 200 mL water | 30 s |
| Large Ore Refinery | 4 Aluminum Ore + 200 mL water | 20 s |

(wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Aluminum_Ore, edited 2026-10-05.)

Examples of use (wiki, edited 2026-10-05): Industrial Cutteray Mk4 (25 Aluminum Ingot + 14 EMF Generator + 12 Cobalt Paste + 7 Ray Amplifier + 225 mL), Industrial Static Compactor (24 Aluminum Ingot + 14 EMF + 12 Cobalt Paste + 225 mL), and 1 per Duraluminum Ingot. 1.5 cut aluminum-tier component costs by 10 to 25% (patch notes 1.5).
