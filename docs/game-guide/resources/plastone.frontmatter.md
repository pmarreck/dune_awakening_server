# Plastone

"An artificial composite of Silicone and Basalt used in sandstorm-resistant construction" (game text). Item id `Plastone`, stacks to 500 (item data). It is used for the higher-tier variants of buildings (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Plastone, edited 2026-10-05), such as the CHOAM Facility building set (game text journey card "Raise the Roof").

| Refinery | Input | Output | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small Ore Refinery | 200 Basalt Stone + 1 Silicone Block + 50 mL | 200 Plastone | 15 s |
| Medium Ore Refinery | 200 Basalt Stone + 1 Silicone Block + 50 mL | 250 Plastone | 10 s |
| Large Ore Refinery | 175 Basalt Stone + 1 Silicone Block + 50 mL | 250 Plastone | 5 s |

(wiki, same page; the output counts are as the wiki lists them and are unverified in game.) Vendor base price 10 Solari (wiki).
