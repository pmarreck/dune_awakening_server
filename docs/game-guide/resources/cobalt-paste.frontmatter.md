# Cobalt Paste

"A dissolution of Cobalt, refined from Erythrite Crystal from the Hagga Rift at a Chemical Refinery" (game text). Item id `CobaltBar`, stacks to 500 (item data). The building text for the Small Chemical Refinery calls it "Cobalt Ingots" (game text).

| Refinery | Input | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Small Chemical Refinery | 3 Erythrite Crystal + 75 mL water | 15 s |
| Medium Chemical Refinery | 2 Erythrite Crystal + 75 mL water | 10 s |

(wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Cobalt_Paste.) Vendor base price 20 Solari (wiki).

The wiki lists 210 recipes using it. Examples: Cutteray Mk3 to Mk6 (10, 12, 16, 30), Power Pack Mk3 (6) and Mk6 (23), Industrial Static Compactor (12), Omni Static Compactor (30), Blood Extractor Mk4, most Buggy modules from Mk4 and every Focused Buggy Cutteray (wiki, per-item pages, edited 2026-10-05). Keep a stock before moving into aluminum tier.
