# Scanners and Survey Probes

The hand scanner "will reveal various things like components, vehicles, bases and people within its scan range" and marks them on your map (game text). Survey Probes "map the local area" when launched high (game text). Resource nodes then show on the map, which has filters for Ores, Crystals, Foliage, Salvageable, Spice Field and Flour Sand and markers such as "Titanium Ore", "Stravidium Mass", "Jasmium Crystal", "Erythrite Crystal", "Basalt Stone" (game text).

Uses for resource hunting:

- Survey fog first: the Coriolis storm returns fog of war over unsurveyed Hagga Basin regions and wipes the Deep Desert map (game text), and node positions move with each storm (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Coriolis_Storms). Guides recommend probes early in the Deep Desert week (community, method.gg, pre-1.5).
- A journey task asks you to "Locate a Flour Sand Field using a Scanner" (game text).
- After a storm, scan for buried treasure and dig it out with a Static Compactor (game text).
- Agave plants are easier to find with a resource scanner (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Agave_Seeds).
- Planetologist's Cartographer passive lets you fill a Blank Sinkchart with survey data (game text).
