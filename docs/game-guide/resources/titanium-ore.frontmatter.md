# Titanium Ore

Titanium Ore "is found in the deep desert, and most commonly utilized in the production of plastanium" (game text). It does not spawn in Hagga Basin (community, method.gg below). Mine it with a Cutteray Mk5, "capable of mining titanium and stravidium" (game text), or a Mk6.

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Item id | `T6ResourceA`, stacks to 500 | item data |
| Mining tier | Tier 6 ("Titanium and Stravidium") | game text, mining info card |
| Tool | Cutteray Mk5, Mk6, or the uniques Tarl Cutteray and Kynes's Cutteray; Buggy Cutteray Mk6 | game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Cutteray |

## Yields and node behavior

- Cutteray "Mining Yield" for titanium/stravidium is low on every tool: Mk5 36, Mk6 48, Tarl 48, Kynes's 63, Buggy Mk6 90, Focused Buggy Mk6 116 (wiki, unit not stated). A buggy laser roughly doubles a handheld Mk6, so bring a buggy with storage if you can.
- Deep Desert node counts and respawns were tuned several times before 1.5: patch 1.1.0.17 lowered titanium and stravidium spawns in PvE areas and raised their respawn from 30 to 45 minutes, keeping large quantities in the northern PvP zones; a later patch added nodes near the entrance rows (community reports of patch notes, https://method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-patch-1-1-0-17-brings-first-deep-desert-pve-changes and https://mein-mmo.de/en/dune-awakening-patch-mehr-mats-deep-desert-pve,1519946; pre-1.5, unverified for 1.5).
- Official Deep Desert PvP areas give 2.5x yields (patch notes 1.3.20.0, cited in [world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md)).
- The weekly Coriolis storm wipes and reshapes the Deep Desert (game text); guides recommend going early in the week and using Survey Probes to reveal fogged areas (community, method.gg).

## Refining

Titanium is never refined alone. Medium Ore Refinery: 6 Titanium Ore + 1 Stravidium Fiber + 1,250 mL water makes 1 Plastanium Ingot in 30 s; Large: 4 ore, 20 s (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Titanium_Ore, edited 2026-10-05). See [plastanium-ingot](plastanium-ingot.frontmatter.md).

On this server the Deep Desert's PvP mode is not recorded (see [world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md)).
