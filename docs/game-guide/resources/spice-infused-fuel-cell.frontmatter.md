# Spice-infused Fuel Cell

"A fuel cell for spice-powered generators. Made from fuel cells and spice residue" in a Medium Chemical Refinery (game text). Item id `SpicedFuelCell`, stacks to 500 (item data).

Recipe: Medium Chemical Refinery, 30 Fuel Cell + 65 Spice Residue + 3 Irradiated Slag + 200 mL water makes 10 in 30 s (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Spice-infused_Fuel_Cell, edited 2026-10-05). Irradiated Slag is looted in the "Radiated Core in the Sheol" (game text). The Spice Generator burns one cell per 1.5 hours and supplies 1,000 power (wiki, as cited in [bases-and-building.md](../bases-and-building.md)).
