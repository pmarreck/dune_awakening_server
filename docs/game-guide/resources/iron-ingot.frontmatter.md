# Iron Ingot

"An Iron ingot, refined from Iron Ore at any Ore Refinery ... Can also be further processed into Steel with Carbon Ore" (game text). Item id `IronBar`, stacks to 500 (item data).

| Refinery | Input | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Small Ore Refinery | 5 Iron Ore + 25 mL water | 10 s |
| Medium Ore Refinery | 4 Iron Ore + 25 mL water | 7 s |
| Large Ore Refinery | 3 Iron Ore + 25 mL water | 5 s |

(wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Iron_Ore, edited 2026-10-05.)

Examples of use (wiki, edited 2026-10-05): Cutteray Mk2 (10 Iron Ingot + 7 EMF Generator), Static Compactor (10 Iron Ingot + 7 EMF Generator), Handheld Resource Scanner (9 Iron Ingot + 6 EMF Generator), and 1 per Steel Ingot.
