# Steel Ingot

"A Steel ingot refined from Carbon Ore and Iron Ingot at any Ore Refinery" (game text). Item id `SteelBar`, stacks to 500 (item data).

| Refinery | Input | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Small Ore Refinery | 1 Iron Ingot + 4 Carbon Ore + 50 mL water | 5 s |
| Medium Ore Refinery | 1 Iron Ingot + 3 Carbon Ore + 50 mL water | 4 s |
| Large Ore Refinery | 1 Iron Ingot + 2 Carbon Ore + 50 mL water | 3 s |

(wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Carbon_Ore, edited 2026-10-05.) Full ore cost per steel ingot is therefore 9 ore at Small (5 iron + 4 carbon) and 5 at Large (3 + 2) (arithmetic).

Examples of use (wiki, edited 2026-10-05): Cutteray Mk3 (15 Steel Ingot + 10 EMF Generator + 10 Cobalt Paste + 5 Ray Amplifier + 90 mL), Power Pack Mk3 (9 Steel + 8 Particle Capacitor + 6 Cobalt Paste + 70 mL), Compact Compactor Mk3 (14 Steel), Buggy Cutteray Mk3 (30 Steel). Steel Welding Wire is "crafted from Steel and reduces the rate of durability loss from repairs" (game text).
