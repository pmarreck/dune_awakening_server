# Duraluminum Ingot

"A Duraluminum ingot, refined from Aluminum Ingots and Jasmium Crystals at a Medium Ore Refinery" (game text). Item id `DuraluminumRod`, stacks to 500 (item data).

| Refinery | Input | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Medium Ore Refinery | 1 Aluminum Ingot + 4 Jasmium Crystal + water | 5 s |
| Large Ore Refinery | 1 Aluminum Ingot + 3 Jasmium Crystal + water | 4 s |

Water: 500 mL on the wiki's Duraluminum Ingot page, 650 mL on its Jasmium Crystal page (both edited 2026-10-05); unverified which is current. Vendor base price 375 Solari; not dropped on death (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Duraluminum_Ingot).

Ore cost per ingot at Large: 4 Aluminum Ore + 3 Jasmium (arithmetic).

Examples of use (wiki, edited 2026-10-05): Cutteray Mk5 (40 Duraluminum + 18 EMF Generator + 16 Cobalt Paste + 10 Ray Amplifier + 7 Thermoelectric Cooler + 450 mL), Iodine Pill x5 (1 Duraluminum + 10 Agave Seeds + 170 mL). Duraluminum Welding Wire "greatly reduces the rate of durability loss from repairs" (game text). Contracts sometimes ask for duraluminum deliveries (game text).
