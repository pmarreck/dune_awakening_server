# Stravidium Fiber

"These fibers are carefully drawn from a chemically-treated stravidium mass, and used in the production of plastanium" (game text). Item id `T6RefinedResourceB`, stacks to 500 (item data).

Recipe: Medium Chemical Refinery, 3 Stravidium Mass + 100 mL water makes 1 Stravidium Fiber in 10 s. There is no Small Chemical Refinery recipe (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Stravidium_Mass, edited 2026-10-05). Its only listed use is the Plastanium Ingot (game text; wiki).
