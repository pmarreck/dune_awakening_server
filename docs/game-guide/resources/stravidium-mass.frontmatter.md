# Stravidium Mass

"Raw stravidium mass can be found in the deep desert, and refined into fibers" (game text). It is the scarcer half of plastanium. Mine it with a Cutteray Mk5 or Mk6 (game text), then turn it into Stravidium Fiber in a Medium Chemical Refinery.

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Item id | `T6ResourceB`, stacks to 500 | item data |
| Tool | Cutteray Mk5 or better (same tier 6 group as titanium) | game text |
| Where | Rock formations in the Deep Desert; "much harder to find" than titanium; look for a shine or glow on rocks from the air | community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/where-to-find-and-farm-stravidium-mass-and-titanium-ore-in-dune-awakening (2025-06-18, pre-1.5) |
| Density | Denser farther from the Shield Wall, toward the PvP north on official servers | community, as cited in [world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md) |
| Map marker | "Stravidium Mass" | game text |
| Vendor base price | 15 Solari | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Stravidium_Mass (edited 2026-10-05) |

Refining: Medium Chemical Refinery, 3 Stravidium Mass + 100 mL water makes 1 Stravidium Fiber in 10 s; that is its only recipe (wiki). Each Plastanium Ingot uses 1 fiber, so 3 mass per ingot.

Yields, respawn history and the weekly wipe are the same as for [titanium-ore](titanium-ore.frontmatter.md).
