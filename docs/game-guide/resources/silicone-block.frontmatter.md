# Silicone Block

"A block of plastic refined from Flour Sand in a Chemical Refinery" (game text). Item id `Silicone`, stacks to 500 (item data).

| Refinery | Input | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Small Chemical Refinery | 5 Flour Sand + 50 mL water | 15 s |
| Medium Chemical Refinery | 3 Flour Sand + 50 mL water | 10 s |

(wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Silicone_Block.) Vendor base price 45 Solari (wiki).

The wiki lists it as an ingredient in 347 recipes, mostly armor, weapons and extractors (for example 7 in the Filter Extractor Mk3, 47 in The Baron's Bloodbag), and 1 per batch of Plastone. A Vermillius Gap journey task asks you to "Acquire Silicone Blocks by refining Flour Sand" (game text). Unreleased Polar Sink text mentions "Salvaged Plastic" that recycles into silicone (game text, placeholder; not in item data).
