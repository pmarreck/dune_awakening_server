# Spice Sand

Spice Sand "can be harvested by hand or with a Static Compactor at Spice Blow sites before the Worm arrives" and refined into Spice Melange at a Spice Refinery (game text). Item id `SpiceSand`, stacks to 2,500 (item data). The Sandcrawler with a Vacuum and Centrifuge harvests it in bulk (game text; see [vehicles.md](../vehicles.md)).

The spice section covers it in detail: where fields appear ([spice/spice-fields](../spice/spice-fields.frontmatter.md), [spice/spice-blows](../spice/spice-blows.frontmatter.md)), gathering ([spice/harvesting-by-hand-and-compactor](../spice/harvesting-by-hand-and-compactor.frontmatter.md), [spice/sandcrawler-and-carrier](../spice/sandcrawler-and-carrier.frontmatter.md)), worm risk and refining ([spice/refining-melange](../spice/refining-melange.frontmatter.md)).

Refining in brief (wiki, as cited in [survival-combat-crafting.md](../survival-combat-crafting.md)): Spice Refinery 100 sand + 750 mL water gives 1 melange + 5 residue in 180 s; Medium 750 sand + 7,000 mL gives 10 + 75 residue; Large 10,000 sand + 75,000 mL gives 200 + 1,000 residue. Water, not sand, is often the limit.
