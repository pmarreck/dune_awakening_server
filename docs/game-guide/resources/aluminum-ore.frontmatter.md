# Aluminum Ore

Aluminum Ore is "found in the Shield Wall" (game text, equipment progression tutorial), the mountain rim along the north of Hagga Basin. It needs a Cutteray Mk3, a "heavy cutteray capable of mining aluminum" (game text), and only a Medium or Large Ore Refinery can smelt it (game text: "refined into Aluminum Ingots at a Medium Ore Refinery").

## Refining

| Refinery | Input | Output | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small Ore Refinery | not possible | | |
| Medium Ore Refinery | 7 Aluminum Ore + 200 mL water | 1 Aluminum Ingot | 30 s |
| Large Ore Refinery | 4 Aluminum Ore + 200 mL water | 1 Aluminum Ingot | 20 s |

(wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Aluminum_Ore.) The Large refinery almost halves the ore per ingot, so aluminum is where a Large Ore Refinery starts to pay off.

Aluminum yield by cutteray (wiki, unit not stated): Mk3 36, Mk4 48, Mk5 72, Mk6 104. The handheld Mk3 can mine aluminum, but the wiki's Buggy Cutteray Mk3 stats list only the copper, iron and carbon groups (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Buggy_Cutteray_Mk3), so a Mk4 buggy laser may be needed for aluminum from a vehicle (inference, unverified).

Rarity: moderate; concentrated in the north.
