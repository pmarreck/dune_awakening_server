# Deep Desert spice

"The largest and richest spice fields are found in the Deep Desert, far beyond the Imperial sphere" (game text). It is the only map with medium and large fields: up to 60 small, 12 medium and 1 large active at once, against 5 small in Hagga Basin (server config; sizes and rows from the wiki, see [spice-fields](spice-fields.frontmatter.md)).

## The Deep Desert journey

The endgame journey deck "Deep Desert" ("Explore the Deep Desert and increase your spice production") has five cards (game text):

| Card | Tasks |
|---|---|
| Charting the Depths | Survey the Deep Desert; locate a Large Spice Field, an Imperial Testing Station, a Shipwreck and a Control Point |
| Operational Readiness | Research and build a Sandcrawler Mk6 with Vacuum and Centrifuge, fuel it, harvest with it; build and fuel a Carrier Ornithopter Mk6; transport a vehicle with it |
| Logistical Issues | Build a temporary base: at least 4 foundations, 7 walls and a door, 4 roof pieces |
| Smoke Trail | Research and fabricate the Regis Missile Launcher, 20 missiles, 15 kills |
| There Is Never Enough | Bring a Sandcrawler to a large field, harvest it, and use a Thumper to pull a worm away from the Sandcrawler |

## Practical notes

- Build a temporary staging base near the fields: Deep Desert building costs half and demolishing refunds everything, and the journey points to the Solido Replicator for fast build and teardown (game text).
- A community solo guide reports a good three minutes before a worm attacks in the Deep Desert, more than in Hagga Basin (community, https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=262586, pre-1.5, unverified).
- On official worlds the PvP instance pays 2.5x harvesting yield (patch notes 1.3.20.0); see [selling-spice](selling-spice.frontmatter.md) for this server's setting.
- The Coriolis storm removes every spice field and anything you left behind: "All spice fields will be gone" (game text). Refine or carry the sand home before the reset. On this server the storm is scheduled with a 6-hour warning and does no damage unless the admin turns it on (server config; see [../world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md)).
- Grandfather worms only rise over the large field; see [harvesting-worm-risk](harvesting-worm-risk.frontmatter.md) for why they may not appear with two players.
