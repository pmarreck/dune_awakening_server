# Jabal Eifrit

Quick answer: a mountainous mid-game region held by Slavers (Trapper, Scorcher, Sawbones, Sniper; game text). The journey groups it with the Hagga Rift as one mid-game deck, "Jabal Eifrit & Hagga Rift" (game text). Its three parts are Al-gharb (west), Al-janub (south) and Al-sharq (east by name; the game's key calls it "north") (game text, `WORLD_REGION_NAMES_SURVIVAL1_JABAL_EIFRIT_*`).

## Journey stops (game text)

"Travel to Khidr's Shadow outpost", "Climb the Hand of Khidr", "Explore Jabal Eifrit Al-sharq", "Travel to the Wreck of the Tisiphone", "Travel to Imperial Testing Station No. 63", "Explore Jabal Eifrit Al-gharb", "Travel to the Pinnacle Tradepost", "Travel to Eastern Jumble Outpost". The same deck unlocks the specialized fabricators, the buggy, the thumper (after the Fourth Trial of Aql) and the Holtzman Shield Mk3, and sends you to the Exchange to bank Solari.

## Good for

- Carbon ore (Al-janub) and mixed ores (community, pre-1.5). Guides from 2025 also list aluminum and granite plateaus near Testing Station No. 76 and primrose dew fields in Al-gharb and Al-janub (community, pre-1.5, https://itemlevel.net/dune-awakening-top-5-best-base-locations-for-all-materials/). Resource nodes in Hagga Basin move at each Coriolis storm (wiki), so treat node spots as approximate.
- Third-tier components: Enhanced Servoks and Enhanced Particle Capacitors in its testing stations, Enhanced EMF Generators and Micro-sandwich Fabric in its caves, Enhanced Plasteel Microflora Fiber and Mechanical Parts in its wrecks, Thick Armor Plating from Heavy enemies and Serrated Blade Parts from rushers and weapon lockers (game text). Calibrated Servoks come from heavy mining equipment "used extensively in Jabal Eifrit" (game text).
