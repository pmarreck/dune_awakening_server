# Arrakeen

Quick answer: the "Battle-scarred capital of the Atreides, home for merchants and refugees alike" (game text), a separate map under "KANLY - GUILD PEACE [NO COMBAT]" (game text). Come here to bank Solari, use the Exchange, train as a Trooper (advanced) or Mentat (advanced), buy vehicle parts, weapons and water, and deal with Atreides and Landsraad representatives. Get there by tradepost taxi (500 Solari to a social hub since 1.5; patch notes 1.5.1.0 PTC, https://duneawakening.com/news/public-test-client-patch-1-5-1-0/) or fly your own ornithopter over the Overland Map (game text).

Districts (game text): Central Plaza, The Warrens, CHOAM Exchange, Residency Approach, Imperial Consulate, The Salusan Bull, Arrakeen Depot, Duncan's Dojo, Landsraad Services, and Shaffat's Clinic.

## Notes

- Arrakeen is a story location as well: the Arrakeen Residency (Duke Leto) and an apartment in the upper Central Plaza appear in quests (game text). Neither is player housing; see [../residences/city-housing.frontmatter.md](../residences/city-housing.frontmatter.md).
- Prices at city vendors are lower than at Pinnacle Station, according to an NPC there (game text).
