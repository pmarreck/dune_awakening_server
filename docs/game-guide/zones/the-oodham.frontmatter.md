# The O'odham

Quick answer: a "comparatively lush" valley (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Hagga_Basin) in Maas Kharet Territory (game text), part of the late-game "Shield Wall & O'odham" journey (game text). The O'odham pyons were "displaced from their old villages" (game text), whose ruins now hold enemies and Insulated Fabric, the pyons' old cottage industry (game text).

## Places (names game text)

| Type | Places |
|---|---|
| Pyon villages | Stonestep Village, Rockwarren Village, Windsong, Mendek's Shithole |
| Landmark | Kynes's Promise (a saguaro tree by its key) |
| Harkonnen base | The Beast's Claw |
| Imperial Testing Stations | No. 163, No. 71 (both listed as O'odham "outposts" in the map keys) |
| Enemy outposts | Taqwa's Watch, Shaitan's Respite, House of Ilm, House of Fiqh |
| Caves | Khala Cavern, The Fable, Musky Cave, Batigh Grotto, Deep Whisper, Wormsight Spire, Cave 7 |
| Instanced room | The Paranoid's Contemplation Chamber (probably not served on this server) |

The game's map keys also file The Crossroads Tradepost and Mysa Tarill under The O'odham.

## Good for

- Agave seeds (map marker "Agave Seeds", game text) and Insulated Fabric (game text).
- Fourth-tier ("Efficient") components and Fortified Armor Plating, shared with the Shield Wall (game text).
- A 2025 guide recommends the western rim of the O'odham for an endgame base close to the Overland Map and the Deep Desert (community, pre-1.5, https://itemlevel.net/best-base-locations-in-hagga-basin-which-to-choose-in-dune-awakening/).

Enemies: Maas Kharet (game text).
