# Riftwatch

Quick answer: the main Harkonnen base in Hagga Basin (game text map key `HaggaRift_FortressHarkonnen`), in the Hagga Rift "near the southern border with the Western Vermillius Gap", close to The Anvil and Imperial Testing Station No. 10 (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Riftwatch, 2026-10-08). Mentat training starts here with Samin Moro (game text). The erythrite tunnel starts nearby (community, thegamer).

| NPC | Role | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Samin Moro | Mentat training lead | game text |
| Harkonnen Acquisitor | Faction reward vendor (the wiki page says "Atreides Faction rewards", probably a copy error) | wiki; speaker name in game text |
| Simone Von Konig | Harkonnen spymistress under Piter de Vries | wiki; game text |
| Lanov; Katrina Chloros (sole survivor of the Kytheria); a Mobula Gang Member | Story and quest NPCs | wiki; game text names |
| Harkonnen Landsraad Representative | Outside, north of the fortress | wiki |
| Lupino Ord (Moritani, inside), Thusnelda (Dyvetz, northwest outside), Dren Silar (Ecaz, cave to the north) | Landsraad contacts | wiki |

The wiki calls it "prime early-game real estate for base building" thanks to erythrite, carbon ore, iron ore and early contracts nearby (wiki). A 2025 guide says not to rely on the far east of the Rift for general Hagga Basin farming (community, pre-1.5, itemlevel).
