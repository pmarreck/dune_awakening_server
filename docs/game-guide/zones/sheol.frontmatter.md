# Sheol

Quick answer: an irradiated graveyard of wrecks, "No-man's Land" (game text), with its own late-game journey deck "The Sheol" (game text). Wear a Radiation Suit and carry Iodine Pills: the deck's card "Mitigation Iteration" has you research, fabricate and consume an Iodine Pill (game text). The wiki puts it in the "far south-west corner" of the map (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Sheol, 2026-09-26); Sheol contracts deliver to The Crossroads, which "sits between ... Mysa Tarill and the Sheol" (game text).

## Journey stops (game text)

"Survey the Sheol", "Travel to the Harkonnen base in the Northern Sheol", "Travel to the Wreck of the Ourea", plus the card "Life from Death": build a Fremen Deathstill and Medium Water Cistern, kill an enemy and bring the corpse home.

## Places (names game text)

| Type | Places |
|---|---|
| Fortress | Edge of Acheron (Harkonnen) |
| Camps | Atreides Camp, Harkonnen Camp |
| Caves | Limbo, Lust, Greed, Wrath, Dis; Shaitan's Grotto (map key `CaveAltar`) |
| Shipwrecks | Wreck of the Ourea (with Forward and Aft sections), Wreck of the Tartarus, Wreck of the Leto, Wreck of the Delphis (Fore and Aft), Wreck of the Euporia |

## Good for

- Jasmium crystals in irradiated pools inside rock outcrops (community, https://www.pcgamer.com/games/mmo/dune-awakening-jasmium-crystal-locations/). Jasmium is wanted at Pinnacle Station by contract (game text).
- Fifth-tier components: Overtuned EMF Generator, Durable Micro-sandwich Fabric, Optimized Servoks, Charged Particle Capacitor, Braided Plasteel Microflora Fiber, Precision Mechanical Parts, Reinforced Armor Plating, Precision Gun Parts, High-tensile Springs, and Irradiated Slag "Found in Radiated Core in the Sheol" (game text).
- Bodies for a deathstill (the journey teaches it here).

Enemies: House troops at the camps and enemies in radiation suits (game text; community, pre-1.5).
