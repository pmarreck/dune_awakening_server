# Hagga Basin

Quick answer: the persistent survival map (`Survival_1`, always running on this server; verified on this server), "Region laid to waste by the war, partly sheltered by the Shield Wall" (game text). About 8 by 8 km (community, pre-1.5). Players progress roughly south to north: Hagga Basin South, then the Vermillius Gap, Jabal Eifrit and the Hagga Rift, then the Shield Wall, O'odham and Sheol, then out over the Shield Wall to the Deep Desert (journey decks, game text).

| Region | Controlled by (game text subtitle) | Stage (journey deck) | Nearest hub |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Hagga Basin South](hagga-basin-south.frontmatter.md) | Scavenger Territory | early | Griffin's Reach |
| [Western / Eastern Vermillius Gap](vermillius-gap.frontmatter.md) | Kirab Territory | mid | The Anvil |
| [Jabal Eifrit Al-gharb / Al-janub / Al-sharq](jabal-eifrit.frontmatter.md) | Slaver Territory | mid | Pinnacle Station |
| [Hagga Rift](hagga-rift.frontmatter.md) | Contested Territory | mid | Riftwatch (Harkonnen) |
| [Western / Eastern Shield Wall](shield-wall.frontmatter.md) | Sandflies Territory | late | Helius Gate (Atreides) |
| [Mysa Tarill](mysa-tarill.frontmatter.md) | Maas Kharet Territory | late | The Crossroads |
| [The O'odham](the-oodham.frontmatter.md) | Maas Kharet Territory | late | The Crossroads |
| [Sheol](sheol.frontmatter.md) | No-man's Land | late | none; The Crossroads is nearest (Edge of Acheron is a Harkonnen fortress; its services are undocumented) |

The map legend groups places as Tradeposts, Outposts, Camps, Caves, Shipwrecks, Imperial Testing Stations, Fortresses, Landmarks and Sietches; resource filters are Ores, Crystals, Foliage, Salvageable, Spice Field and Flour Sand, and there is a "Primrose Field" marker for dew (game text). Resource nodes move at each Coriolis storm (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Coriolis_Storms).

Hagga Basin has 13 numbered Imperial Testing Stations, 12 named wrecks (some split into sections) plus the DLC Mithra, and about 80 named caves (game text map keys; see [imperial-testing-stations](imperial-testing-stations.frontmatter.md), [shipwrecks](shipwrecks.frontmatter.md), [caves-and-trials-of-aql](caves-and-trials-of-aql.frontmatter.md)).
