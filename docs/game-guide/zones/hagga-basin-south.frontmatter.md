# Hagga Basin South

Quick answer: where every character starts. The game's journey labels it the early-game deck (game text, `JOURNEY_HBS_EARLYGAME_DECK`). Enemies are Scavengers (Thug, Cut-throat, Scrapper; game text). It holds the first tradepost, the first wreck and testing station, and the first Trial of Aql. Leave when you have a sandbike and are ready to cross the open sand northeast into the Vermillius Gap (game text).

## Good for

- Copper ore, granite, salvaged metal and plant fiber (community, pre-1.5). Granite is "found almost everywhere there is dirt" (game text).
- Starter components: caves give EMF Generators and Micro-sandwich Fabric, the testing station Servoks and Particle Capacitors, the wreck Plasteel Microflora Fiber and Mechanical Parts (component families from game text; see [loot-components-by-region](loot-components-by-region.frontmatter.md)). The lowest-tier versions carry no region in their descriptions.
- Learning bases: the journey card "Location, location, location" teaches base teardown and the Solido Replicator here (game text), and since 1.5 a Base Reconstruction placement in Hagga Basin South has no cooldown (patch notes 1.5.1.0 PTC, https://duneawakening.com/news/public-test-client-patch-1-5-1-0/).

## Getting out

The sand between Hagga Basin South and the Vermillius Gap is worm country. The game's own advice: "take the shortest, flattest route, which looks to be just northeast of the Griffin's Reach" (game text). See [../world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md) for worm rules.
