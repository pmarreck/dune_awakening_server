# Pinnacle Station

Quick answer: a tradepost owned by Traj Axxari ("I own this block of cement. In Pinnacle Station, what I say goes", game text). Buy standard weapons, darts, fuel cells and vehicle/tool components from him, train as a Swordmaster with Arno, and visit the Tleilaxu trader for the Mentat questline. It is a respawn point (game text, `SPAWN_LOC_HaggaBasin_Tradepost_Pinnacle`). Prices are higher than in the cities: an NPC here says "you'll find the prices in the village a good measure lower than Traj's here" (game text).

Region: the game's own map key files it under Hagga Rift (`WORLD_MAP_LOCATION_Survival_HaggaRift_PinnacleStation`, game text); the wiki places it in Jabal Eifrit Al-gharb (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Pinnacle_Station). It sits where the two meet. The Jabal Eifrit journey sends you there ("Travel to the Pinnacle Tradepost", game text).

## Why go

- Calibrated Servok and Ray Amplifier for sale, which otherwise come from the CHOAM Mineral Extraction Facilities in the Rift (game text for the components; wiki for the stock). See [loot-components-by-region](loot-components-by-region.frontmatter.md).
- Jasmium and irradiated Sheol crates are delivered here by contract (game text).
- The Swordmaster school.
