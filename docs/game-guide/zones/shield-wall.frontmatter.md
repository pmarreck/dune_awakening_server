# Shield Wall (Hagga Basin side)

Quick answer: the mountain rim on the north of Hagga Basin, "Sandflies Territory" (game text). The journey's late-game deck is "Shield Wall & O'odham" (game text). Come for aluminum ore (community, pre-1.5) and basalt, the Atreides fortress Helius Gate, Sietch Ta'lab and fast access north to the Deep Desert. The Deep Desert is entered and left over the Shield Wall ("exited ... from the Shield Wall to the south", game text).

## Journey stops (game text)

Card "The Wall and the Valley": "Survey the Eastern Shield Wall", "Travel to Imperial Testing Station No. 142", "Travel to the Stone Sentinel", "Survey the Western Shield Wall", "Travel to Imperial Testing Station No. 60", "Travel to the Wreck of the Alecto". The same deck has you research the CHOAM Facility building set ("Raise the Roof"), the Radiation Suit Mk4, the Spice Mask, the Stilltent and the Scout Ornithopter Mk4 ("Airborne!").

## Good for

- Aluminum, copper, carbon and basalt on high plateaus (community, pre-1.5, keengamer and itemlevel URLs in [../residences/base-sites-by-region.frontmatter.md](../residences/base-sites-by-region.frontmatter.md)); water is scarce here by the same accounts.
- Fourth-tier ("Efficient") Fremen, Old Imperial and Great House components, Fortified Armor Plating from Heavy enemies and Lightweight Gun Parts from Assault and Marksman enemies, all tagged "in the Shield Wall & the O'odham" (game text). Dart-grooved Barrels come from "High-up bases in Shield Wall" (game text).
- Bene Gesserit training starts at Helius Gate with Sister Mesa (game text).

Enemies: Sandflies, including the Sand Fly Leader, Negotiator and Sniper (game text).
