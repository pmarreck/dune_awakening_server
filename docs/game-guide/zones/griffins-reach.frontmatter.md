# Griffin's Reach Tradepost

Quick answer: the first tradepost you reach, in Hagga Basin South. Buy early gear and components from Myr Charki, start Trooper training with Ghavouri, take contracts from the board, and pay a thopter pilot to reach Arrakeen or Harko Village. It is also a respawn point (game text, `SPAWN_LOC_HaggaBasin_Tradepost_GriffinsReach`).

## Who is here

| NPC | Role | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Myr Charki | The trader. Sells Maula clothing (breeches, bracers, boots, vest), Healkit, Cup of Water, light and heavy darts, Welding Material, Mechanical Parts, Micro-Sandwich Fabric, Advanced Servoks, Particle Capacitor, Plasteel Microflora Fiber, Off-world Medical Supplies, Power Pack Mk1, Small Vehicle Fuel Cell, EMF Generator, Scrap Metal Knife, Cutteray, CHOAM Office Furniture Patent and the Hajra Literjon Mk1 schematic. Stands behind the bar, opposite the contract console. | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Myr_Charki (2026-10-11); name and role as "the trader there" in game text |
| Ghavouri | Trooper training lead; the Trooper journey starts with "Talk to Ghavouri at the Griffin's Reach Tradepost". | game text |
| Griffin's Reach Guard | Greets newcomers: pay up front and cause no trouble. | game text |
| Ornithopter pilot | Taxi to Arrakeen, Harko Village or another tradepost. The journey card "A Wider World" sends you here to "Talk to the ornithopter pilot". | game text |
| Contract board | Early contracts and the delivery point for several of them ("Deliver ... to Griffin's Reach Tradepost"). | game text |

Myr Charki also issues the courier contract "Courier Run" that sends you north to The Anvil, and warns that a vehicle is needed to cross (game text). Scavenger contract text calls him a Harkonnen sympathizer who still buys their scrap (game text). The name: a local says "The Griffin is the Harkonnen house symbol" and that the post has no official affiliation (game text).

## What it is good for

- First stop for contracts, the Trooper school and the first taxi (game text).
- Nearby early targets the starter contracts point to: caves for Fremen components, the Wreck of the Alcyon and Imperial Testing Station No. 2 (game text: "Deliver ... from caves", "from the wreck of the Alcyon", "from Testing Station No. 2 to Griffin's Reach").
- The Lost Harvest crash site (Wreck of the Mithra) lies "northwest of Griffin's Reach Tradepost" (game text; needs the DLC).
- Leaving for the Vermillius Gap: the journey says the shortest, flattest crossing is "just northeast of the Griffin's Reach" (game text). Bring a sandbike.

## Services not here

No banker or Exchange terminal: those are in Arrakeen and Harko Village (game text: deposit Solari with "a banker in villages"). No repair NPC appears in the game text; repair your own gear at a Repair Station or with a welding torch (see [../survival-combat-crafting.md](../survival-combat-crafting.md)).
