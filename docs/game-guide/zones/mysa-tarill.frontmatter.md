# Mysa Tarill

Quick answer: a small northern region, "Maas Kharet Territory" (game text), built around "the wrecked power station at Mysa Tarill" (game text), with the Ruined Landing Pad next to it and The Crossroads tradepost between it and the Sheol. It is part of the late-game "Shield Wall & O'odham" journey ("Survey Mysa Tarill", "Travel to the Crossroads Tradepost", game text).

## Places (game text)

Mysa Tarill (map key under The O'odham), Ruined Landing Pad, The Crossroads Tradepost. Mysa Overwatch is a Shield Wall outpost above it.

## Good for

- Diamondine Dust and Carbide Scraps: "This industrial byproduct is closely guarded by the Maas Kharet in their most sacred site" (game text, keys `T4MYSATARILCOMPONENT1/2`). Mysa Tarill is that site by the key name (inference). Zoe at The Crossroads and the city weapons merchants also sell both (wiki).
- Basalt stone and diamond dust (community, pre-1.5).
- The route to Sietch Ta'lab: "Travel through Mysa Tarill until you reach a large door", then answer questions to enter (community, pre-1.5, Trial of Aql guides).

Enemies: Maas Kharet (Initiate, Fanatic, Oppressor, Priestess, Sniper) and their sietch boss "HAND OF THE PROPHET" (game text).
