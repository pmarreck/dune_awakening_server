# Shipwrecks

Quick answer: crashed Great House ships, the source of Plasteel Microflora Fiber and Mechanical Parts at the region's tier (game text). Each is a respawn type (`SPAWN_LOC_Shipwreck`, game text). On official servers Hagga Basin wrecks became PvE in 1.3.20.0; a self-hosted server on "Limited" PvP still makes "most shipwrecks in Hagga Basin" PvP (game text; patch notes 1.3.20.0).

| Region | Wrecks (game text) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hagga Basin South | Wreck of the Alcyon | Starter contract target ("from the wreck of the Alcyon to Griffin's Reach", game text) |
| Hagga Basin South (DLC) | Wreck of the Mithra (Bridge and Engine entries) | Lost Harvest, northwest of Griffin's Reach (game text) |
| Vermillius Gap | The Wreck of the Pallas (west), The Wreck of the Actaeon | Pallas is a journey stop (game text) |
| Jabal Eifrit | Wreck of the Tisiphone | Journey stop (game text) |
| Hagga Rift | Wreck of the Kytheria | Journey stop; its sole survivor, the Atreides officer Katrina Chloros, is at Riftwatch (wiki) |
| Shield Wall | Wreck of the Limos, Wreck of the Alecto | Alecto is a journey stop (game text) |
| Sheol | Wreck of the Ourea (Forward and Aft), Wreck of the Tartarus, Wreck of the Leto, Wreck of the Delphis (Fore and Aft), Wreck of the Euporia | Ourea is a journey stop (game text) |
| Deep Desert Shield Wall | Wrecks of the Hicetas, Eumenes, Hyperbatas, Archidamas III, Cycliadas, Dioedas, Xenophon, Proxenus, Orsippus, Stasanor | Plus random "Unrecovered Shipwreck" markers (game text) |
| Overland Map | Wreck of the Tyche ("An irradiated shipwreck crashed inside the Shield Wall"), Wreck of the Hephaestus (story) | game text |

A third Vermillius Gap wreck key exists with a placeholder name ("Shipwreck 3"), probably unused (game text).
