# The Anvil

Quick answer: the mid-game hub of the Vermillius Gap. Tyg Rolsum runs the shop, the two Great Houses recruit here (Anton Tolliver for Atreides, Maxim Kazmir for Harkonnen), and its contract board drives the Vermillius journey. It is a respawn point (game text, `SPAWN_LOC_HaggaBasin_Tradepost_Anvil`). From the south you need a vehicle to reach it: faction recruitment contracts say "VEHICLE NEEDED IF CROSSING FROM THE SOUTH" (game text).

## Who is here

| NPC | Role | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tyg Rolsum ("Smiley Tyg") | Runs the tradepost ("Name's Tyg. I run this hell hole") and stocks nothing perishable. Sells, among other things, the Night Rider Sandbike Boost Mk2, Scipio's Bloodbag and Buoyant Reaper Mk2 (wiki list, probably incomplete). The Vermillius journey card "Interior Decoration" asks you to "Purchase a CHOAM Furniture set from the merchant" right after "Travel to the Anvil Tradepost", so he very likely sells CHOAM furniture sets (inference). | game text; wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tyg_Rolsum (2026-10-07) |
| Lieutenant Anton Tolliver | Atreides faction contact: "Speak to Anton Tolliver at the Anvil" starts the Atreides funnel. | game text |
| Levenbrech Maxim Kazmir | Harkonnen faction contact: "Speak to Maxim Kazmir at the Anvil". | game text |
| House Tseida representative | Landsraad house contact. | wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tradeposts (2026-10-10) |
| Mobula Gang Member | Mentat questline interrogation target ("Interrogate the Mobula Gang Member at the Anvil"). | game text |
| Ornithopter pilot, contract board | Taxi and contracts, as at every tradepost. | wiki, Tradeposts |

A House agent here describes the Anvil as "a unique melting pot where enemies gather together" (game text): both Houses share the post.

## Garage

An adjacent garage shelters you and a sandbike in a storm, and you can claim a partially damaged sandbike there (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tradeposts). Useful if you lost your bike crossing the Gap.

## What it is good for

- Joining a House (both recruiters stand here) (game text).
- Vermillius journey: "Travel to the Anvil Tradepost and pick up Contracts", the contract chains "PAYBACK" and "MONSTER SLAYING" (game text).
- Iron: delivery contracts say "iron ore is plentiful here in the Vermillius Gap" (game text). Riftwatch and Imperial Testing Station No. 10 are close (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Riftwatch, 2026-10-08).
- Many delivery contracts across Hagga Basin end here ("Deliver ... to the Anvil", irradiated crates from Sheol, erythrite, scrap) (game text).

No banker or Exchange terminal (cities only).
