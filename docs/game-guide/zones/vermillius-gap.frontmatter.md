# Vermillius Gap

Quick answer: the first mid-game region (game text, `JOURNEY_VG_MIDGAME_DECK`, "Vermillius Gap"). Two halves, Western and Eastern, both "Kirab Territory" (game text). Come for iron ore ("iron ore is plentiful here in the Vermillius Gap", game text), flour sand for silicone ("predominantly in the Vermillius Gap", game text), steel, the Anvil tradepost and three Trials of Aql. Enemies are the Kirab (Brute, Succor, bosses) (game text).

## Journey route (game text)

The Vermillius journey's "Touring the Gap" card sends you to:
- West: The Wreck of the Pallas, Flatrock Outpost, Imperial Testing Station No. 197.
- East: Imperial Testing Station No. 10, the Southeast Ironworks, the Anvil Tradepost.
Other cards teach the shield, flour sand to silicone, spice sand, the dew reaper and literjon, vehicle repair, recycling and buried-treasure hunting with a scanner and Static Compactor.

The Hollow Hammer is inside the tall hammer-shaped rock; climb it or land an ornithopter on top (community, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-trials-of-aql-locations-and-rewards). Tyg says he named the Anvil "'cause of the Hammer" (game text).

## Good for

- Iron, steel, flour sand; vehicle components from wrecks, testing stations and caves of the Gap (the second-tier "Servoks", "Particle Capacitor", "EMF Generator", "Micro-sandwich Fabric", "Plasteel Microflora Fiber", "Mechanical Parts" are tagged "in the Vermilius Gap", game text). Blade Parts drop from rushers and weapon lockers here (game text).
- Water: a 2025 guide calls the Western Gap the densest dew-plant area; treat as unverified for 1.5 (community, pre-1.5, https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/dune-awakening-best-base-locations-guide/).

## Hazards

Quicksand, notably in the Western Gap (community, pre-1.5). Reaching it from the south is a worm-exposed crossing (game text). A 2025 guide mentions a "Western Vermillius Gap PvP zone" (community, pre-1.5, https://itemlevel.net/dune-awakening-top-5-best-base-locations-for-all-materials/); official servers made all of Hagga Basin PvE in 1.3.20.0 (patch notes 1.3.20.0). This server's PvP mode is not recorded in the repo.
