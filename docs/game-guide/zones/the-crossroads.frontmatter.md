# The Crossroads

Quick answer: the late-game tradepost. "Crossroads Tradepost sits between the wrecked power station at Mysa Tarill and the Sheol. Right between a secretive cult and an irradiated wasteland" (game text). Zoe sells the widest tradepost stock (armor, furniture sets, tier-4 crafting components); Valentin runs the place and hands out contracts. It is a respawn point (game text, `SPAWN_LOC_HaggaBasin_Tradepost_Crossroads`).

Region: the map key files it under The O'odham (`WORLD_MAP_LOCATION_Survival_Oodham_TradingPost1`, game text); the wiki and the region table put it in Mysa Tarill (wiki, https://awakening.wiki/Tradeposts). The Shield Wall enemy outpost Ironwatch is "just northeast of the Crossroads tradepost" (game text).

## Why go

- Furniture and lights: the only tradepost seller of all three CHOAM furniture sets plus the advanced lights set found in this research (wiki, Zoe).
- Diamondine Dust and Carbide Scraps, which the game says the Maas Kharet guard "in their most sacred site", are on Zoe's list (game text; wiki).
- Thermoelectric Cooler, otherwise from the Deep Desert Shield Wall (game text), is also on Zoe's list (wiki).
- Closest tradepost to the Deep Desert crossing and to Sheol. Sheol delivery contracts say "The Crossroads tradepost isn't too far off" (game text).

Community guides from 2025 also said it stocks higher-end items (community, pre-1.5, https://game8.co/games/Dune-Awakening/archives/528207).
