# Caves and Trial of Aql sites

Quick answer: caves shelter you from sandstorms and sun and hold Fremen components (EMF Generators, Micro-sandwich Fabric) at the region's tier (game text). The eight Trials of Aql, the Fremen story trials, are in named caves; the Inner Sight marker leads you to each (game text).

## Trial of Aql caves

The map keys mark one or more caves per region as `CaveAltar` (game text). Order and regions from community guides (pre-1.5, https://www.method.gg/dune-awakening/dune-awakening-trials-of-aql-locations-and-rewards and https://pcgamer.com/games/mmo/dune-awakening-trial-of-aql-locations), names from game text:

| Trial | Cave | Region | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mihna Cavern | Hagga Basin South | "Complete the First Trial of Aql in Hagga Basin South" is a journey task (game text) |
| 2 | The Deep Struggle | Eastern Vermillius Gap | Guides differ on the approach (southeast of the hammer rock, or from Aren's Post) |
| 3 | Cave of the Lost Pilgrim | Western Vermillius Gap | Interact with a recording rather than an altar |
| 4 | The Hollow Hammer | Eastern Vermillius Gap | Top of the hammer-shaped rock; climb or land a thopter. Completing it unlocks the Thumper (game text: "Complete the Fourth Trial of Aql") |
| 5 | The Hidden Test | Hagga Rift | Faint glowing entrance, easiest to find at night |
| 6 | Devil's Eye Cavern | Jabal Eifrit Al-sharq (northeast) | Long trip; bring a sandbike and welding torch |
| 7 | The Red Maw | Hagga Rift | Look for a red glow |
| 8 | Sietch Ta'lab | Eastern Shield Wall | Through Mysa Tarill to a large door; answer questions to enter |

Shaitan's Grotto (Sheol) and Stoneheart Cave (Western Shield Wall) are also `CaveAltar` keys (game text); their role is unverified.

## Cave counts by region (named caves in game text map keys)

Hagga Basin South about 20, Vermillius Gap about 20, Jabal Eifrit 9, Hagga Rift 8, Shield Wall 7 plus Sietch Ta'lab and Sentinel City, The O'odham 7, Sheol 6. Full name lists are in each region's entry. The Deep Desert has fixed Shield Wall caves (The Duke's Latrine, The Watchway, The Endless Wait, Hanuman's Grotto, Among the Stones, Indara's Cradle, Shishakli's Saddle, Sand and Madness, The Last Post, The Sardaukar Promise) and random "Forgotten Cave" markers (game text).

## Practical

- A cave is a storm shelter (game text, via the world chapter). Server owners can lift "Area Building Restrictions", at the cost of no shelter inside caves (game text).
- Bring a Personal Light for the Rift and dark caves (game text journey).
