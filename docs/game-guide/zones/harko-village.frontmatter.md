# Harko Village

Quick answer: "Interim headquarters of the Harkonnens, and nexus of the Carthag slave trade" (game text), a no-combat social hub on its own map. Bank and trade at the CHOAM Exchange, train as an advanced Swordmaster (Seron Varlin) or Bene Gesserit (Jocasta Cleo), buy vehicle parts, weapons, scrap and water, and use the Landsraad crafting vendor. Thopter pilots wait at Spire Keep.

Districts (game text): Spire Keep (and Spire Keep Terminus), New Court Way, The Baron's Eye, CHOAM Exchange, Imperial Consulate, Hannivar's, Beast's Bend, Neo-Carthag Outskirts, Harko Terminus North and South, Phrakk's Clinic.
