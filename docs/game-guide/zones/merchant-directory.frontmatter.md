# Merchant directory

Quick answer: find the item in the table, then go to the nearest vendor listed. Stock lists come from each NPC's awakening.wiki page (edited 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-13, after 1.5); NPC names and locations are confirmed in game text where noted in the location entries. The map filter "Vendor" and markers such as "Vehicle Vendor", "Scrap Trader", "Base Vendor", "Weapons Merchant", "Water Seller", "Spice Merchant", "Barkeep" and "Landsraad Vendor" show them in game (game text). Vendor stock can run out ("Insufficient vendor stock") and some items restock on a timer ("Time Until Restock"); vendors show a "Demand" percentage when you sell (game text).

## Roaming merchants

Dynamic encounters put stranded merchants in the field: Shahbazi Hoon ("Shahbazi's Heritage Imports"), "Stranded Water Merchant" and stranded ore merchants in Jabal Eifrit, the O'odham, the Shield Wall and the Vermillius Gap (game text speaker ids `StrandedOreMerchant_*`). Their contracts send goods to a tradepost. Whether they also sell is unverified.

## Not found

No repair NPC appears in the game text; repair at your own Repair Station or with a welding torch. The Pinnacle Station Tleilaxu Trader's stock is not documented.
