# The Deep Desert

Quick answer: the endgame map (`DeepDesert_1`; verified on this server, `data/maps.tsv`), with its own journey deck "Deep Desert" (game text, `JOURNEY_DD_ENDGAME_DECK`). Fly in over the Shield Wall in an ornithopter; leave the same way, since it "can only be exited from the Shield Wall to the south" (game text). The full mechanics (sector grid, PvP instances, Coriolis wipe, 50% building cost) are in [../world-and-maps.md](../world-and-maps.md); this entry adds what to do there and where.

## Journey tasks (game text)

"Surveying the Deep Desert", "Locate a Large Spice Field", "Locate an Imperial Testing Station", "Locate a Shipwreck", "Locate a Control Point"; build a Sandcrawler Mk6 and a Carrier Ornithopter Mk6, "Transport a Vehicle with an Ornithopter"; "Construct a temporary base" (4 foundations, 7 walls and a door, 4 roof pieces); research the Regis Missile Launcher; "Use a Thumper to distract a worm away from the Sandcrawler" while harvesting a large spice field.

## Fixed places on the Shield Wall rows (game text)

| Type | Names |
|---|---|
| Caves | The Duke's Latrine, The Watchway, The Endless Wait, Hanuman's Grotto, Among the Stones, Indara's Cradle, Shishakli's Saddle, Sand and Madness, The Last Post, The Sardaukar Promise |
| Imperial Testing Stations | No. 185, 148, 37, 93, 186 (a Maas Kharet station by its key), 217 (a Sandfly station by its key) |
| Wrecks | Hicetas, Eumenes, Hyperbatas, Archidamas III, Cycliadas, Dioedas, Xenophon, Proxenus, Orsippus, Stasanor |
| Landmark | Execution Wall |
| Random | Buried Testing Station, Forgotten Cave, Unrecovered Shipwreck, Control Point |

The 186 and 217 key suffixes are `ECOLAB_MaasKharetBronze` and `ECOLAB_SandFlyBronze` (game text keys); that they hold those factions is an inference from the names.

## Good for

- Titanium ore, stravidium mass and large spice fields, none of which spawn in Hagga Basin (community, method.gg, via the world chapter).
- Thermoelectric Coolers from the Deep Desert Shield Wall (game text) and the top ("Advanced") component tier (game text; see [loot-components-by-region](loot-components-by-region.frontmatter.md)).
- Grandfather worms appear only here (wiki).

Enemies: house troops, seasoned deserters and smugglers (game text).
