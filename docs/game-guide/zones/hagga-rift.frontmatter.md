# Hagga Rift

Quick answer: a deep, dark rift ("Contested Territory", game text) between Harkonnen and Atreides forces. It is the only source of Erythrite Crystal in Hagga Basin ("mined from an Erythrite deposit in the Hagga Rift", refined to Cobalt Paste at a Chemical Refinery; game text). Bring a Personal Light: the journey card "A Light in the Darkness" ends with "Travel into the Hagga Rift and use the Personal Light" (game text).

## Journey stops (game text)

"Explore the Rift", "Survey Hagga Rift", "Travel to the Wreck of the Kytheria", "Travel to Imperial Testing Station No. 29", "Travel to the Spiral Outpost".

## Good for

- Erythrite: the long crystal-lined tunnel starts near Riftwatch and needs at least a Cutteray Mk2 (community, https://www.thegamer.com/dune-awakening-erythite-crystals-location-guide/).
- Calibrated Servoks and Ray Amplifiers: the CHOAM Mineral Extraction Facilities are the "mining gallery" sites for these components (inference from the item keys `T3MININGGALLERYCOMPONENT1/2`, game text names). Traj Axxari at Pinnacle Station and Soha Sirrendrin in Arrakeen also sell them (wiki).
- Enhanced (third-tier) Fremen, Old Imperial and Great House components, as for Jabal Eifrit (game text).
- Mentat training starts at Riftwatch with Samin Moro (game text).

Enemies: Harkonnen and Atreides forces and deserters (game text; community).
