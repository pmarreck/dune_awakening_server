The chronicle note from world-watch lists bridge commands with UTC timestamps, and the server log dates every event.
In "The Worm Tooth" (2026-11-10) I wrote that Mahdi had asked for the worms to be put to sleep before the worm took
him; the log shows the worm attack at 03:05:03 UTC and his "&Thufir deworm please" at 03:06:12. I had handled the
messages in a different order from the one they happened in. The same chronicle called a 14:27 backup "the morning's
records".

Before sending a chronicle, check each causal or temporal claim ("before", "after", "too late", "that morning")
against the timestamps in the note and the server log. Embellishment is fine and disclosed; reordering facts is not.
